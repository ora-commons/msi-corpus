## Supreme Court to hear Colorado pre-K religious-freedom case

The Supreme Court was scheduled to hear arguments in a case between the Catholic Church and Colorado's taxpayer-funded pre-K programs. The case asks whether religious schools can refuse admission to children of gay and trans parents, which would violate a state law against discrimination. For decades, the court upheld the separation of church and state, but over the past 10 years its conservative majority had increasingly focused on First Amendment protection of religious freedom. NPR reported that this shift complicates Colorado's inclusive pre-K initiative.

Colorado built its inclusive pre-K initiative to ensure that virtually all 4-year-olds in the state receive pre-K education. To meet that goal, the state included religious schools as long as they complied with laws prohibiting discrimination based on race, religion, sexual orientation or gender identity.

NPR's Allison Aubrey used her "Your Turn" series on navigating midlife to examine burnout, which can become a challenge during midlife. As work and home obligations take up more of a person's focus, burnout can slowly sap a sense of joy and wonder in everyday life. Psychiatrist Judith Joseph said anhedonia is the inability or reduced capacity to experience pleasure from things that are normally enjoyable. Joseph said it can accompany what she calls "high-functioning depression" and described the condition as depression's "silent sister."

Research published in the journal Cureus confirmed the hypothesis that people with high-functioning depression have increased levels of anhedonia and trauma, and parents and caregivers had the highest high-functioning depression scores. The feature noted that high-functioning depression is not an official diagnosis in the DSM-5 and that people who keep functioning may not recognize their symptoms or seek treatment until they are burned out. Joseph also developed an anhedonia quiz, which is not a diagnostic tool but can help people take stock of obstacles to living a more joyful life, and a framework for combating anhedonia called the Five V's: Validation, Venting, Values, Vision and Vitals.
