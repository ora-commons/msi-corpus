A federal judge in Alexandria, Virginia, issued an injunction Friday morning to block construction of President Donald Trump's proposed arch in the Washington, D.C. area. The order prevents construction from proceeding on the proposed structure.

During a hearing on Friday, the judge said the president's project was a “rushed job” that “lacked a legal basis.”
