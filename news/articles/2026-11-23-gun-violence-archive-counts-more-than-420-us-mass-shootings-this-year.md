## Congress has not passed federal gun restrictions, the Guardian reported

As of Monday, the Gun Violence Archive had counted more than 420 mass shootings in the US so far for the year. The archive is a nonpartisan resource that defines a mass shooting as a case in which four or more victims are either wounded or killed.

The Guardian reported that high numbers of mass shootings reported annually in the US have prompted many to call for more substantial federal gun control. Congress over the years has not passed such restrictions, the report said.

The Guardian's report on the year's tally described a Saturday night shooting at a concert in Strathmore, California, a small town in Tulare County, a mountainous region in the center of the state. Nine people were shot, the local sheriff's department said in a statement Sunday. The shooting broke out at about 7:30 p.m. Saturday at a concert attended by about 150 people. An unspecified number of suspects opened fire on the crowd, the department said, and no arrests were announced.

One 26-year-old man was shot in the chest and required surgery, the department said. He remained in critical condition. Two other victims, ages 19 and 22, also required surgery, while six people sustained minor injuries.

Authorities said they believe the shooting may be gang-related but did not elaborate. The show in the town of roughly 3,000 people featured performers from neighboring states, the department said.

The department said there was no longer an immediate threat to the public. It did not immediately respond to emailed questions seeking further details.

Nine people were wounded in Strathmore, above the archive's threshold of four or more victims either wounded or killed. The Guardian placed that account alongside Monday's count of more than 420 mass shootings for the year.

The Guardian's report carried a photograph credited to Matt Rourke/AP, and Guardian staff contributed to the reporting.
