## California DMV cited construction-zone incursions and a highway-patrol traffic break

The Guardian reported that Waymo, the Alphabet subsidiary, operates the largest fleet of self-driving cars in the US, and that California has barred the company's autonomous vehicles from state freeways. A photograph accompanying The Guardian's report showed a Waymo vehicle on the freeway in Hawthorne, California, in October, credited to Mike Blake/Reuters.

California issued the restrictions last Wednesday, according to Jonathan Groveman, a spokesperson for the state's department of motor vehicles. Groveman said the measure bars Waymo from "operating or testing its driverless vehicles on all California freeways."

The department said the measure follows three of Waymo's autonomous vehicles that "entered an active construction closure" south of San Francisco on 5 November, and another incident the next day involving highway patrol vehicles on a highway in San Francisco.

Video of the 6 November incident circulated widely on social media last week. In the footage, a Waymo vehicle can be seen trying to bypass California highway patrol vehicles conducting a traffic break.

"The department determined that Waymo's driverless freeway operations pose an imminent hazard to public safety due to recurring incursions by its vehicles into active construction zones where construction workers, vehicles and equipment are present," Groveman said, adding that "similar incidents" previously occurred on 18 May.

Waymo's autonomous vehicles expanded on to freeways and interstates around San Francisco, Los Angeles and Phoenix last year.

A Waymo spokesperson said the "recent freeway construction zone encounters on US 101 in California" did not "involve any injuries or collisions" and that the company prioritized safety.

"In alignment with our safety-first principles, we have also temporarily restricted freeway operations across our other operating markets to evaluate our performance," the spokesperson said. Waymo also said it was working with the California DMV and other state officials to resume freeway operations: "We are working with the California DMV and other state officials diligently and responsibly to resume freeway operations."

Waymo continues to operate the vehicles as normal on California city streets, according to the company.

California's restrictions will remain in effect until "Waymo demonstrates to the department that it has taken effective corrective actions to address the deficiencies that precipitated the restrictions," Groveman said.
