## Cessna went down around 3.30pm Tuesday between Pascagoula and Horn Island

The US Coast Guard said the Cessna 210 was descending at 10,000 feet per minute when contact with the aircraft was lost.

The plane went down around 3.30pm Tuesday in the Gulf of Mexico between Pascagoula, Mississippi, and Horn Island. Horn Island is part of the Gulf Islands National Seashore.

Rescue crews were searching Tuesday afternoon for six people aboard the small plane after the crash off the Mississippi coast.

Rescue crews had so far found three life jackets, a life raft and the plane's cabin. Juan Ros, a US Coast Guard spokesperson, said the cabin was floating on the water.

Authorities said four adults and two boys, ages eight and 10, were aboard the aircraft.

The Cessna 210 was en route from New Orleans to Fort Meyers, Florida, according to the FlightAware flight-tracking website.

The Federal Aviation Administration and the National Transportation Safety Board said Tuesday that they were aware of the crash. The National Transportation Safety Board will investigate the crash.
