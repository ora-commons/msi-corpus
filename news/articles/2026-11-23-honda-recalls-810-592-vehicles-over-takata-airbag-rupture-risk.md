## Honda recalls 810,592 vehicles over Takata airbag rupture risk

**Subtype:** fact

- Honda is recalling 810,592 vehicles because certain Takata front and passenger airbag inflators may rupture.
- The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration said the recall covers Honda Civic and CR-V vehicles with model years 2012 to 2013, plus Honda Accord, Acura ILX and Acura RDX vehicles with model year 2013.
- The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration said propellant degradation after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity and high temperature variation may rupture the affected Takata inflators.
- The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration said a ruptured inflator could send sharp metal fragments toward a driver or another occupant and cause serious injury or death.

## Dealers will replace affected inflators free; interim owner notices expected Dec. 18

Honda is recalling 810,592 vehicles because certain Takata front and passenger airbag inflators may rupture. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration said the affected vehicles include Honda Civic and CR-V models from 2012 to 2013 and Honda Accord, Acura ILX and Acura RDX models from 2013.

The agency said the affected population includes Honda Civic vehicles with model years 2012 to 2013 and Honda CR-V vehicles with model years 2012 to 2013. It also includes Honda Accord 2013 models and Acura ILX and RDX vehicles with model year 2013.

NHTSA said the affected vehicles carry certain Takata front and passenger airbag inflators. The agency said the inflators may rupture when propellant degrades after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity and high temperature variation.

According to NHTSA, a rupture could send sharp metal fragments hitting a driver or another vehicle occupant. The agency said such an impact could result in serious injury or death.

Under the recall, dealers will replace the front driver or passenger airbag inflator at no cost. The replacement covers the component identified by NHTSA as vulnerable to rupture after prolonged exposure to high humidity and temperature variation.

Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk are expected to be mailed Dec. 18. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available.

The broader Takata airbag recall has covered tens of millions of airbags and is one of the largest product recalls ever. Honda’s latest action adds specific Civic, CR-V, Accord, ILX and RDX vehicles to the vehicles affected by the Takata inflators.

Owners may contact Honda’s customer service at 1-888-234-2138 for more information. Individuals may also contact the NHTSA vehicle safety hotline at 1-888-327-4236 or visit www.nhtsa.gov.
