## Murdaugh’s lawyers focused on Owen’s blood-spatter account

A South Carolina judge on Friday refused to dismiss the original murder indictments against Alex Murdaugh in the 2021 killings of his wife, Maggie, and younger son, Paul Murdaugh. Judge Debra McCaslin’s ruling leaves the charges in place and clears the way for an April retrial; prosecutors said Murdaugh killed them on the family’s sprawling property in the South Carolina Lowcountry.

McCaslin’s decision keeps the original murder charges in place after the South Carolina Supreme Court threw out Murdaugh’s convictions and life sentence in May, ruling that a court clerk improperly influenced jurors during his 2023 trial. The ruling clears the way for an April retrial on the murder charges.

The decision came after a Nov. 13 hearing at which Murdaugh’s attorneys argued for nearly an hour that chief investigator David Owen lied to a Colleton County grand jury in 2022 about blood spatter being found on Murdaugh’s T-shirt. Owen, a former State Law Enforcement Division agent, was central to Murdaugh’s arguments.

Defense lawyer Jim Griffin said at the hearing that, when prosecutors sought the indictments, Owen told the grand jury that blood spatter had been found on the T-shirt Alex Murdaugh was wearing the night of the killings. An earlier report from state agents said there were no blood stains, and no such evidence was introduced at trial.

Griffin said the false claim about the blood spatter was the only thing anyone had heard that could link Alex Murdaugh to the place and time of the killings. “A phone call doesn’t put him there. The video doesn’t put him there,” Griffin said. “The blood from his murdered son sprang through the air -- it’s raining back on him -- puts him there.”

Owen also told grand jurors that the same ammunition load of buckshot in one chamber and large birdshot in the other used to kill Paul Murdaugh had been found loaded in weapons all over the Murdaugh property. A denial followed, but the report did not identify who made it: “This was not the case.”

Prosecutors wrote that “a single mistake is not bad faith” and that other evidence supported the charges. Owen told grand jurors about a video Paul Murdaugh recorded in which Alex Murdaugh’s voice could be heard at the scene, contradicting Alex Murdaugh’s denials.

Owen, who abruptly retired in 2024, also told grand jurors about the family’s mounting debts and said Alex Murdaugh’s schemes to steal from clients and his law firm were close to being discovered.

Prosecutors said Murdaugh killed his wife, Maggie, and younger son, Paul Murdaugh, on their sprawling property in the South Carolina Lowcountry because he thought the sympathy generated by the killings would buy him time to cover up his thefts.

Murdaugh, 58, is serving decades in prison for stealing millions of dollars from clients and has admitted in court that he is a thief, insurance cheat, bad lawyer and longtime opioid addict. He denies being a killer.
