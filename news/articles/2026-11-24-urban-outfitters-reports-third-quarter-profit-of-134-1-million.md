## Retail same-store sales growth slowed to 4.4% from 8% a year earlier

Urban Outfitters reported third-quarter profit of $134.1 million, or $1.54 a share, up from $116.4 million, or $1.28 a share, a year earlier. The Wall Street Journal reported that total company sales rose 8.9% to $1.67 billion, meeting Wall Street expectations.

Retail segment same-store sales rose 4.4%, compared with 8% growth in the prior-year period. That comparison covers stores open long enough for the company to measure performance on a comparable basis, and it is the measure that slowed as the retailer reported stronger earnings and higher total sales.

Urban Outfitters' retail segment sales rose 6.1%, below the pace of the company's overall sales. The faster growth came in two other lines: subscription sales climbed 28% and wholesale sales increased 19%.

Chief Executive Richard Hayne said, "Every brand and segment contributed positively to our third-quarter performance, underscoring the strength of our portfolio and disciplined execution across the organization."

Urban Outfitters said its namesake brand, Free People and Anthropologie all performed well during the quarter. The retailer reports across three business lines — retail stores, subscriptions and wholesale — and each contributed a quarterly figure to the report.

Per-share earnings of $1.54 topped the $1.52 that analysts polled by FactSet expected.

Market data reported by The Wall Street Journal showed the shares of Urban Outfitters, which trade under the ticker URBN, up 0.34%.

The quarter closed with Urban Outfitters posting higher profit, higher total sales and per-share earnings above the FactSet expectation, while comparable-store growth of 4.4% trailed the 8% recorded a year earlier. Subscription and wholesale posted the fastest growth rates in the company's sales mix, and the company said every brand and segment contributed positively to the result.
