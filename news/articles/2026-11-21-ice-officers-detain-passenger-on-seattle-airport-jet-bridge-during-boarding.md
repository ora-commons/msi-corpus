## Alaska Airlines says ICE gave no advance notice of jet bridge arrest

Masked immigration officers detained at least one person on a crowded jet bridge at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport on Saturday as passengers were boarding a flight to Los Angeles, a scene Alaska Airlines called "distressing" for passengers and employees and one that delayed the departure.

Video of the incident showed officers dragging away at least one person at the airport as a woman screams that her daughter is being taken and that she has a work permit. It was not clear how many people were detained.

The arresting officers wore unmarked jackets and khaki pants. In the video, they are seen struggling with someone on the ground as passengers surrounded by rolling suitcases look on.

Online flight data showed that Alaska Flight 1306, which had been scheduled to depart at 8:10 a.m., was delayed.

Alaska Airlines said the Immigration and Customs Enforcement officers gave no advance notice of the enforcement action, which occurred during a busy travel weekend ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday.

"We are concerned by this morning's law enforcement action during the boarding of Alaska Flight 1306," the airline said. "We recognize how distressing this incident has been for affected employees and guests."

The airline added that it is committed to "treating every guest with dignity and respect."

The Department of Homeland Security, which oversees ICE, did not immediately respond to emails seeking comment.

ICE has stepped up arrests at airports since the summer, especially targeting people who overstayed visas in the past.

Alaska Airlines said it was working with the Port of Seattle, which manages the airport, to see if protocols were followed, and that it has raised concerns "directly with the appropriate authorities."

The Port of Seattle said in a statement that the arrest was distressing not only for the affected family but for other passengers, and that local authorities would "engage federal officials to understand more about what occurred."

Washington Gov. Bob Ferguson, a Democrat, called the incident "just the latest example of ICE's deeply un-American tactics."

"ICE is completely and totally out of control," Ferguson said in a statement. "Like many Americans, I'm outraged by masked agents, who do not identify themselves, dragging people off jetways as they board a flight, creating chaos in our communities."

Ferguson announced an afternoon news conference to address the incident.
