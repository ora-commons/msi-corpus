## State data show 220 measles cases in 2026, 40 in children four or younger

Virginia reported its first measles-associated death of 2026 on Friday, involving a child who tested positive for the infectious disease, the state's health department said.

The department said the child was four years old or younger. It did not disclose additional details, including the child's vaccination status, citing the family's privacy.

"Virginia has not had a measles-related death since 1978," said Frederic Bertley, an immunologist and chief executive officer of the Center of Science and Industry. Bertley said the infection was alarming and evidence that measles is gaining a foothold and becoming a growing problem for the United States.

The death adds to a worsening national measles outbreak that recently cost the United States its measles-elimination status after 26 years.

Pennsylvania, the hardest-hit state, reported an eighth measles-related death and confirmed 1,631 measles cases as of Friday. Wisconsin has also reported two deaths, while New York state recorded its first measles-associated death, involving a child.

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention has confirmed six measles-associated deaths so far in 2026, though it does not specify the states where the deaths were recorded. A discrepancy between state and federal death tallies arose after the CDC, under the Trump administration's health secretary, Robert F. Kennedy Jr., began using National Center for Health Statistics mortality data instead of state figures.

Within Virginia, the state health department counted 220 measles cases so far in 2026, including 40 among children four years old or younger and 75 among those age five to 12, in data shown as of Thursday. Of those cases, 179 involved unvaccinated individuals, 39 were associated with people whose vaccination status was unknown and two occurred with vaccinated individuals.

Virginia health officials have reported increased measles activity driven largely by outbreaks in the state's central region. Buckingham county has reported 127 cases, the most in the state, while neighboring Charlotte county has recorded 34 cases.


The Virginia death extends a national outbreak MSI previously reported in [U.S. measles cases top 5,400 so far this year, largely driven by Pennsylvania](/articles/2026-11-17-u-s-measles-cases-top-5-400-so-far-this-year-largely-driven-by-pennsylvania/), which traced the surge to Pennsylvania and to the loss of the country's measles-elimination status after 26 years.
