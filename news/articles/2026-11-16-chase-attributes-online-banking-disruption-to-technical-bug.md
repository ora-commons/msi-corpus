## Downdetector lists Bank of America, Chase, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo and Zelle

Customers reported difficulty accessing online accounts at some major banks Monday, according to user reports collected by the website downdetector.com.

Bank of America, Chase, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo and Zelle were listed by users as down or experiencing issues on the site.

Chase said the problem at Chase Bank stemmed from a technical bug, not a cyber incident. "Due to a technical issue, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete but customers still have access to their accounts and funds," a Chase spokesperson said in a statement. "We are working to resolve this as quickly as possible."

The spokesperson said Monday that Chase Bank's services were experiencing a technical bug issue, not a cyber issue.

Some Chase customers received a message that read: "Some services aren't available. We're working on them. Please try back later."

The reports of banking disruption follow a major outage at Microsoft that began Friday and lasted for hours. The Microsoft outage impacted services including Outlook, Teams and the AI assistant Copilot.

Downdetector aggregates user reports of service problems, so its listings reflect what customers said they were experiencing rather than a declaration by the banks themselves. The source material did not indicate when the banking issues would be resolved.
