## Giuliani lost his law license after promoting Trump's false claims

Donald Trump is expected to present Rudy Giuliani with the Presidential Medal of Freedom on Tuesday morning, according to The Guardian. Trump's schedule lists the presentation as closed press, meaning no media would attend.

Giuliani, 82, told Steve Bannon on Monday that Trump would give him the award on Tuesday morning. “I’m, of course, very, very humbled and very honored not only to receive it but to receive it from him,” Giuliani told Bannon. Trump had announced the award more than a year earlier.

The Presidential Medal of Freedom is one of the highest civilian awards in the United States. It is typically given to individuals who contribute to world peace, the national interest or significant cultural endeavors.

Ahead of the presentation, the White House posted a heroic compilation video of Giuliani, saying he left an “undeniable mark on American history.”

Giuliani served as the mayor of New York City during the 11 September 2001 terrorist attacks. The Guardian said his leadership made him a globally recognized figure and earned him the nickname “America’s mayor.”

After serving as mayor, Giuliani ran for the Republican presidential nomination in 2008 but dropped out after underperforming in early primaries, The Guardian reported.

The Guardian also reported that Giuliani's reputation suffered several blows in the decades after 9/11, in part because of his loyalty to Trump. Giuliani lost his license to practice law after promoting Trump's false claims of election fraud.

Giuliani settled a multimillion-dollar defamation suit filed by election workers Ruby Freeman and her daughter, Wandrea “Shaye” Moss, after he falsely accused them of election fraud, The Guardian reported.

Giuliani filed for bankruptcy in 2023 after settling the $148 million defamation suit, though a judge later dismissed the bankruptcy suit. In 2025, he also settled a long-running, billion-dollar lawsuit filed by voting machine manufacturer Dominion Voting Systems.

Giuliani pleaded not guilty to state charges in Georgia and Arizona stemming from efforts to overturn the 2020 election, though both cases were eventually dismissed.

In his second term, Trump has awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom posthumously to Charlie Kirk, the conservative activist and co-founder of Turning Point USA, and to Welles Crowther, a stock trader and volunteer firefighter who died during 9/11 while rescuing people.
