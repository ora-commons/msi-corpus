## Body remains unlocated as Coast Guard search was suspended

The Santa Cruz County Sheriff's Office confirmed that a beachgoer found the purse of Se Nah Park, 24, at 4 Mile Beach, south of Panther Beach, on Wednesday, three weeks after a wave swept her into the ocean. Park's body has not been located, and the Coast Guard suspended its active search for her on November 9.

The Guardian reported that the west coast saw record high king tides when the purse was found. Park's disappearance came on November 8, at Panther Beach in California.

According to a GoFundMe started by Park's friends, Park and five others had gathered around a bonfire beyond the keyhole at Panther Beach that night. Four members of the group, including Park, tried to make their way back through the keyhole when a powerful wave struck.

The wave swept Park into the ocean, the GoFundMe account said. Three people in the group managed to get back up the cliffslide; Park did not.

The Coast Guard suspended active search efforts on November 9, one day after Park was swept away. Lt Max Holloway, the Sector San Francisco search and rescue mission coordinator, said: "Given the extensive search effort of the surrounding area and environmental conditions, we have suspended active search efforts pending further developments."

The statement went on: "We worked closely with our partner agencies and exhausted all available resources in our attempt to locate Ms Park. Our deepest condolences are with her family and loved ones."

In the weeks since, Park's family and friends have not given up on their own search efforts. The friends wrote on the GoFundMe page that they continue to "hold on to the hope that Senah can be found and brought home to rest."

The Guardian said the discovery of the purse may lead Park's community closer to locating her body. As of Wednesday, Park's body remained unlocated, the Coast Guard's active search had been suspended since November 9, and Park's family and friends were still searching.
