## First-place majority resolved the race without ranked-choice calculations

Former Democratic Rep. Mary Peltola defeated Republican Sen. Dan S. Sullivan in Alaska's U.S. Senate race, according to The Associated Press, giving Democrats 56 senators in the upcoming Congress. Peltola secured a majority of first-place votes, so officials did not need to calculate the result through Alaska's ranked-choice voting system.

The victory flips a Senate seat in a state Trump won by 13 percentage points in 2024. The race's final days were marked by allegations that Peltola's team tried to recruit another candidate to the race to siphon votes from the incumbent. Peltola's campaign denied any involvement.

Nvidia board chairman Harvey Jones said on an earnings call that the board does not intend to reinstate former CEO Jensen Huang. Huang "voluntarily resigned" on Nov. 12 after disclosing "personal behavior inconsistent with the company's code of conduct," according to Jones. Huang acknowledged that he had been unfaithful to his wife. News reports tied Huang to a woman whom U.S. intelligence officials say was recruited by China's spy agency, and the board unanimously accepted his resignation.

Earlier, Huang said he would return as CEO if the board permitted it and agreed to testify before Congress regarding national security issues surrounding Nvidia. NPR's John Ruwitch said Jones clearly wanted to dispel rumors and showed support for interim CEO Colette Kress. He said Kress "has been at the center of the company's extraordinary growth." Kress also joined the call and maintained a business-as-usual demeanor, Ruwitch said.

Nvidia posted revenue exceeding $115 billion for the quarter, with the results secured before Huang's recent departure. Tech analyst Gaurav Gupta of Gartner told NPR that any impact from the new leadership would not be reflected in the numbers for several months. Jones said the board would try to decide on a permanent CEO as soon as possible without rushing the decision.

President Trump arrived in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen and was set to meet President Xi Jinping on Thursday. The meeting was expected to mark the third time the two leaders had met this year. They were expected to discuss trade, investment and tariffs, which were currently on hold.

NPR described 7-OH as one of the major psychoactive ingredients in kratom and said it acts more like an opioid in the human brain than kratom leaf products do.

Some people use kratom to maintain long-term recovery from opioid addiction, while others say it helps them feel energized or focused. Users of 7-OH and kratom can show signs of addiction, and unclear product labeling makes it difficult to determine how much they consumed.

Over the past decade, the U.S. government tried to restrict these products, but public opposition kept them legal. In August, the Drug Enforcement Administration restricted the manufacture and sale of three semi-synthetic opioids from 7-OH and other related compounds. The scribe uses "automatic speech recognition" technology to record the conversation between a doctor and patient. After the visit, the recording is transcribed and turned into a medical note about the exam's findings and the doctor's decisions. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act compliance is crucial for AI scribe services seeking to protect patient privacy. Software companies must safeguard data by encrypting it, controlling access and implementing systems to prevent data breaches. Most companies delete recorded conversations after a specified period.

AI scribes can make mistakes, which is why doctors must review AI-generated notes before signing them and adding them to the medical record. Most AI scribe software requires doctors to sign a user agreement, making them liable for all content in the medical record. Many AI scribes can only be used with English-speaking patients, and health equity advocates have raised concerns that this could widen health disparities. Patients can opt out of using an AI scribe during a doctor's visit, but opting out of AI in medicine more broadly could be difficult.
