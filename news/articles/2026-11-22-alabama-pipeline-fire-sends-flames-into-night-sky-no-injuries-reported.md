## Operator shuts down pipeline segment; customer deliveries unaffected

A fire on a natural gas pipeline burned in rural Alabama late Saturday, sending a towering column of orange flame above the tree line and prompting the operator to shut down part of the line, Southern Natural Gas Company said.

The company said in a statement Sunday that it “experienced a natural gas release and fire” on a segment of the pipeline at around 11:30 p.m. CST Saturday.

No injuries were reported, and customer deliveries were not impacted, the company said.

Emergency responders and company personnel secured the area and the blaze was extinguished, according to the statement. The impacted part of the pipeline was shut down and depressurized.

The cause of the fire is under investigation, the company said.

Photos and videos posted on social media appeared to show flames rising dozens of yards above the trees near Ward, with smoke billowing into the air.

People said on social media that the fire was visible from miles away across the state line in Mississippi.

The Sumter County Emergency Management Agency did not immediately respond to a voicemail seeking information.

The company’s account of the incident came in a statement issued the day after the fire, and it did not describe what preceded the release on the line.
