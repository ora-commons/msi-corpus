## Iran's seven conditions include Middle East ceasefire

Iran's IRNA state news agency reported Monday that the country's delegation to the United Nations General Assembly had no plans for negotiations with the United States, citing an unnamed source close to the group. The statement directly contradicted President Donald Trump's account to Axios one day earlier that he expected more negotiations to resume soon. Trump's expectation came a day after he publicly rejected an Iranian proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days.

According to IRNA, "Iran's views and positions were conveyed clearly and explicitly to the US side... via a Qatari mediator." IRNA added that the Iranian delegation would return to Tehran on Tuesday.

Axios reported that indirect talks mediated by Qatar could take place as early as Monday, less than a week after a separate round of discussions between US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner and Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi. That earlier session ended without a reported position shift on either side.

Iran says it has presented seven conditions for ending the conflict and reopening the strait, all of which Trump rejected on Saturday. The demands include a seven-day ceasefire across the Middle East — including in Lebanon, where Iran's ally Hezbollah is regularly bombarded by Israel — the unfreezing of Iranian foreign assets, the lifting of US sanctions on Iran's oil sector, and an end to the US naval blockade of Iranian ports.

The Strait of Hormuz lies at the heart of a conflict that began with US-Israeli strikes on Iran in February. Following that attack, Tehran placed a chokehold on the waterway, and Washington responded with a blockade of Iranian ports. Roughly 20% of the world's oil and gas flowed through the strait before the war.

The Monday IRNA statement did not address whether Iran had formally rejected new talks or was merely confirming that none had been scheduled during the General Assembly session. No party had reconciled the two accounts as of publication.
