## FEMA instructs families to file insurance claims before applying for assistance

President Donald Trump approved a major disaster declaration for Brevard and Broward counties in Florida for flooding and storms the state has seen since Nov. 16 and that were continuing.

Brevard had been included in Trump's declaration earlier in the week, while Broward had not. Broward's later addition brought both counties into the current federal designation.

Because of the declaration, grants are available for temporary housing and home repairs in Brevard and Broward.

Loans to fix uninsured property are also available under the declaration, along with other programs for people in the two counties.

FEMA instructed families to first file insurance claims and then apply for federal assistance. Residents may apply at www.disasterassistance.gov or call 1-800-621-3362.

According to a social media video from the state, Florida applied for the disaster declaration for flooding in eight counties: Brevard, Broward, Flagler, Indian River, Orange, Putnam, Seminole and Volusia.
