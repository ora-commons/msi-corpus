## Earlier conviction carried 10 years and six months in prison

A jury at Caernarfon crown court in north Wales found Blade Silvano, 44, guilty of six counts of sexual assault by penetration on Tuesday for conduct between November 2022 and the following June. The case involved a woman whom Silvano met on Plenty of Fish while using the alias “Hunter” and falsely claiming to be an army captain and surgeon. Silvano is already serving 10 years and six months in prison for a separate Cambridge conviction involving the same deception.

Both women said Silvano tricked them into believing she was male, and neither had any idea they were being deceived.

Using the alias “Hunter,” Silvano began messaging the woman before meeting her for sex, always keeping the lights very low and not allowing herself to be touched intimately. On one occasion, Silvano claimed that “his” condom had broken while they had sex. She assured the woman that pregnancy was “very unlikely” and told her: “Trust me, I’m a doctor.”

The woman said she noticed her partner sounded a “little feminine” and began to have doubts about their “odd” sex life. She eventually received a call from Silvano’s long-term partner, a woman, who said the person she had known as “Hunter” was female, the court heard.

After learning of the deception, the woman told police she felt sick, saying: “I thought it was some sort of joke.” In court, she said: “I believed I was in a relationship with a straight, heterosexual man. I would never go out with a woman.”

Philip Clemo, prosecuting, told the jury Silvano was “a gifted con artist” and suggested she was inspired by the myth of Zeus turning into a bull.

The earlier Cambridgeshire offenses took place in 2016 and 2017. As in the later case, Silvano met that woman on an online dating site. Using the fake name Blade Mendez, she presented herself as a male army veteran throughout a two-year relationship. She sent the woman photographs of herself apparently shaving her face and pretended to urinate standing up.

Silvano, of Shropshire, would not allow the woman to touch her genitals, claiming to be “body conscious due to injuries sustained in the army.” Silvano and the woman became engaged, and the woman was encouraged to try on wedding dresses and book a registrar. The woman accidentally uncovered the deception after coming across Silvano on Facebook in September 2018 “in a completely different living situation.”

Silvano was found guilty of two counts of assault by penetration in the earlier case. She was jailed in Cambridge in 2023 after a jury heard she carried out the deception using a blindfold, a sex toy and a “wholly fake persona.” She was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison and placed on the sex offenders register.

Silvano has not yet been sentenced for the six current counts. Judge Jones ordered a pre-sentence report so that the risk she posed could be assessed, with sentencing scheduled for next month.
