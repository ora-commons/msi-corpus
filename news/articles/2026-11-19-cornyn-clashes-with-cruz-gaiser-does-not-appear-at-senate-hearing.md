## Roberts criticizes Cornyn’s refusal; Cornyn responds by calling him “irrelevant”

Outgoing Republican Sen. John Cornyn and fellow Texas Republican Sen. Ted Cruz had a yelling match on the Senate floor over T Elliot Gaiser’s nomination, according to The Washington Sun. The report said the confrontation was loud enough for people in the press gallery to hear Cornyn tell Cruz: “I don’t find you credible. I’m mad at you.”

At one point, Cornyn told Cruz he was upset at someone “for ending my career.” It was unclear whether Cornyn was referring to Trump or Cruz.

Gaiser, a 37-year-old Justice Department official, did not appear before the Senate Judiciary Committee on Wednesday.

Kevin Roberts, president of the Heritage Foundation, the conservative thinktank behind Project 2025, publicly criticized Cornyn on social media for refusing to support Gaiser. Roberts wrote on X: “John Cornyn is going out as the Republican Texas Senator who helped get radical leftist James Talarico’s agenda started early. What a legacy.”

Cornyn responded by calling Roberts “irrelevant” and saying that Roberts’s “sycophancy has destroyed the credibility of a once great institution.”

Cornyn’s refusal came a day after the confrontation. He withheld his “blue slip,” derailing Republicans’ effort to approve as many of Trump’s judicial nominations as possible before Congress recesses for the rest of the year and Democrats take over in January.

Under a longstanding Senate tradition, the Judiciary Committee chair supports a president’s judicial nominee only when senators from the state approve. That approval is known as a “blue slip.”

On Wednesday, Cornyn told the Associated Press he would not support Gaiser for the northern Texas district court. Cornyn said: “He’s not from Texas. He hasn’t been vetted. And this is a lifetime job, and there’s better-qualified people to hold that job.”

Cruz, in a statement obtained by Reuters, said: “If the Senate Judiciary Committee does not move Gaiser forward, the people of Texas will pay the price because it will effectively give the judicial seat to the Democrats, who will keep it vacant in perpetuity.” Cruz said the White House indicated it would be “impossible” to get a new nominee confirmed because the FBI’s background check would take too long.

Cornyn told reporters he recommended Steve Fahey, a federal prosecutor in Texas, for the empty seat on the northern district of Texas court.

Cornyn is leaving the US Senate after nearly 24 years following his primary loss to a Trump-backed challenger. Trump had refused to endorse Cornyn’s reelection and instead backed Ken Paxton in the Senate race; Paxton ultimately lost to Democrat James Talarico.

The Justice Department said in a statement obtained by the Associated Press that it was “deeply disappointing that Senator Cornyn would choose political obstruction over advancing a highly qualified nominee.”
