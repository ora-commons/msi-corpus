## Comparable sales rose 6.1% as Best Buy gained market share

Best Buy raised its full-year outlook for adjusted earnings, revenue and comparable sales on Tuesday after reporting higher quarterly profit and revenue. The electronics retailer said the results reflected market-share gains and growth in several key categories.

Net earnings totaled $353 million for the three months ended Oct. 31, up from $140 million a year earlier. The results amounted to $1.66 a share, compared with 66 cents a share in the year-earlier period.

After excluding one-time items, Best Buy earned $1.62 a share, above the $1.49 a share analysts expected.

Revenue rose 5.8% to $10.24 billion, topping the Wall Street estimate of $9.89 billion. Comparable, or same-store, sales increased 6.1%, well above analyst expectations for a 2.3% gain.

Best Buy now expects adjusted earnings of $6.90 to $7.05 a share for the year, up from prior guidance of $6.70 to $6.90. The company projects revenue of $43 billion to $43.3 billion, compared with the previous forecast of $42.3 billion to $42.8 billion.

The retailer also lifted its full-year comparable-sales forecast to growth of 3.4% to 4%, compared with its previous forecast of 1.9% to 3%.

For the current quarter, Best Buy expects comparable sales to rise 2% to 4%. The midpoint of that range is above analysts' 2.3% forecast.

FactSet analysts were looking for adjusted earnings of $6.82 a share, revenue of $42.7 billion and same-store sales growth of 2.7% for the year.

“Disciplined execution, a compelling product cycle and continued market share gains helped drive broad-based top-line momentum across nearly all product categories, our services offerings, and both our digital and store channels,” Best Buy Chief Executive Jason Bonfig said.
