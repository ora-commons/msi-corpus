## Suspected gunman found dead after barricading in apartment

Two officers with the Co-op City Department of Public Safety were shot Monday at the Co-op City apartment complex in the Bronx, the New York Police Department said. Their injuries were not believed to be life-threatening, and both were taken to a hospital for treatment.

The New York Police Department confirmed that the two victims were officers with the Co-op City Department of Public Safety, a private security force for the complex. The suspected gunman was found dead of a self-inflicted gunshot wound after barricading himself in an apartment at the massive complex, police said.

Police revised the number of victims after initially saying three people had been wounded.

The shooting unfolded in one of the high-rises at Co-op City, which was built as middle-income housing in the 1960s and '70s and now has more than 15,300 cooperative apartments in high-rises and townhomes, counting 50,000 residents in all.

Mo Bee was working in a podiatry office in the building when authorities arrived and said it was being evacuated. He went outside and saw a mass of officers and emergency vehicles in the campus-like complex.

It was "a pretty crazy scene," said Bee, adding that he was allowed back in after about two hours. No patients were in the office at the time of the shooting.

The complex's emergency dispatchers said they had no information. The property's management referred calls to the NYPD.
