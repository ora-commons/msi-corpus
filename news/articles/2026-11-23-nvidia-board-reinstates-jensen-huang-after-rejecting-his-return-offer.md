## Colette Kress returns as CFO after serving as interim CEO

Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and chairman on Monday in a surprise move, shortly after rejecting his offer to return. Colette Kress had served as interim CEO and will return to her role as Nvidia's CFO.

Board Chairman Harvey C. Jones said in a statement Monday, "The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers."

Huang said in a statement that he was "honored" to return to the company and that he takes the responsibility "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment."

He thanked Jones and interim CEO Colette Kress, who will return to her role as CFO, as well as Nvidia's executive staff, for their leadership during the brief period of disorder.

Huang's statement continued: "I am also deeply thankful to our employees, shareholders, leaders across the industry and partners around the world for their extraordinary support." He added, "Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I can't wait to get back to work."

The Associated Press described the reinstatement as the latest twist in the fallout from Huang's admission of an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy.

President Donald Trump said the federal government had "no reason to believe" Huang's return to Nvidia posed a national security risk. Trump also said he would love Huang to be given a second chance.


Earlier in the leadership change, [the board said it would not reinstate Huang as CEO](/articles/2026-11-18-nvidia-holds-first-post-huang-earnings-call-petition-has-nearly-200-000-names/) after [Huang offered to return to Nvidia and agreed to testify to Congress](/articles/2026-11-17-jensen-huang-offers-to-return-to-nvidia-and-agrees-to-testify-to-congress/); Monday's unanimous decision reversed that position.
