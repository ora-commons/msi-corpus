## Updated filing puts annual revenue at $81 billion

Anthropic, the California-based developer of the Claude chatbot, submitted an updated U.S. regulatory filing for an initial public offering with a valuation of up to $1.4tn (£1.05tn). The filing, made Tuesday, disclosed the terms of the company's planned Wall Street stock market flotation.

The filing said Anthropic aimed to raise up to $64.4bn by selling 825m shares in the IPO, priced at up to $78 a share. At the upper end of those terms, the company would seek a $1.4tn valuation.

The filing said Anthropic would also raise a further $26bn through a separate private placement with cornerstone investors, including the Google parent Alphabet and chipmaker Nvidia. Trading in Anthropic shares could start as soon as next week.

The company had previously been widely reported to be targeting a $2tn initial public offering on Wall Street. The updated filing showed that its sights had been set lower, with Anthropic now seeking up to $1.4tn.

The Guardian reported that investor jitters over the AI boom crystallized last week when the US midterm elections produced victories for datacentre opponents. The result added to existing concerns about how quickly, or if at all, companies can generate returns on AI infrastructure spending.

Brian Hopkins, a principal analyst at Forrester Research, said the election gave investors an opportunity to correct valuations because of worries about the financial sustainability of the AI build-out. He said the results brought those concerns to a head. "The market needed an excuse to correct itself a little bit in terms of valuations," Hopkins said. "The US election spook is what has caused it." He added that Anthropic's valuation had been lowered from the anticipated $2tn.

The Guardian described Elon Musk's SpaceX as the largest-ever stock market listing. SpaceX joined the U.S. stock market with a $1.78tn valuation and was now worth just under $2tn. Its assets include Musk's rocket launch business and the X social media platform.

A Chinese spying scandal involving Nvidia, described as a cornerstone company for AI investors, threatened to overshadow the industry. The issue had not affected share prices so far, apart from Nvidia's.

The updated filing said Anthropic's annual revenue was running at $81bn as of 31 October, up $10bn from the previous month.
