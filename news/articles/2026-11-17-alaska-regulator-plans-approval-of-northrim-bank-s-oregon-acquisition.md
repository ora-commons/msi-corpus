## Stock deal gives Oregon bank owners more than 22% of enlarged company

The Alaska Division of Banking and Securities said in a public notice Thursday that it was planning to approve Northrim Bank’s takeover of People’s Bank of Commerce. The proposed purchase would extend the Alaska-based lender into Oregon for the first time.

Northrim is based in Alaska and has 21 branches spread across the state. People’s Bank of Commerce has 11 branches in southern Oregon and the Willamette Valley. The proposed acquisition would place both sets of branches under a single owner.

The purchase, announced by Northrim in July, would mark the company’s first expansion outside Alaska. It would establish Northrim’s Oregon presence through the acquisition of People’s Bank of Commerce and its existing branch network.

People’s Bank of Commerce shareholders approved the deal in October. The approval followed Northrim’s July announcement and came before the Alaska banking regulator’s public notice.

Under the terms of the purchase, Northrim would give stock in Northrim to the owners of People’s Bank of Commerce. Those owners would own more than 22% of the enlarged company after the transaction.

Northrim and People’s Bank of Commerce expect the arrangement to become final on Jan. 1. The public notice set out the regulator’s plan to approve the transaction; it did not announce that the purchase had already been completed.

The Knoll Bond Rating Agency analyzed the deal this summer. The agency concluded that the transaction would strengthen Northrim and was aligned with the bank’s efforts to expand outside Alaska.

At the time of the public notice, the purchase had shareholder approval but remained subject to the regulator’s planned approval and the companies’ expected Jan. 1 finalization.
