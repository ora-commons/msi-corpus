## HSBC expects 2% China passenger-car growth in 2027

General Motors is working with an executive search firm to identify possible outside candidates to succeed Chief Executive Mary Barra and is also considering internal candidates, according to people familiar with the matter. The Wall Street Journal reported the process on Nov. 18. Barra has led General Motors since 2014, making her its second-longest-tenured chief executive.

HSBC Global Research analysts said in a note that China’s domestic passenger-car demand will likely grow 2% from a low 2026 base, mainly because of support for electric vehicles. They said expiration of the remaining 5% EV purchase-tax relief at the end of 2027 will likely bring some purchases forward to late 2027.

China’s passenger-car exports also maintained robust momentum during the first 10 months of the year, and HSBC expects an export rush ahead of a VAT rebate cut in April 2027. The analysts said medium-term growth depends on localization to mitigate geopolitical trade risks. Among automakers, HSBC prefers BYD because of its overseas manufacturing footprint, proven brand recognition and scale advantages.

Rata Limsuthiwanpoom of TTB Wealth Securities said in a research report that SCGJWD Logistics is likely benefiting from strong export, import and foreign-direct-investment flows. Its general warehouse and dangerous-goods terminal and yard businesses are capturing those flows, particularly in data centers, artificial intelligence, electronics and manufacturing. The transportation and distribution business is capitalizing on logistics demand across the agricultural, electronics, electrical appliance, industrial and e-commerce sectors.

TTB Wealth Securities lifted its 2026-2028 earnings forecasts for SCGJWD Logistics by 29% to 33%. The brokerage raised the stock’s target price to 14.00 baht from 11.80 baht and left its buy rating unchanged. Shares were unchanged at 10.90 baht.

Barclays analysts said further disruption to the Strait of Hormuz in 2027 would be a headwind for Qatar’s GDP and would keep the country’s budget deficit large. Qatar recorded a cumulative budget deficit of 54.3 billion Qatari riyals, or $14.9 billion, through the third quarter, far exceeding the full-year target of 21.8 billion Qatari riyals. Its GDP contracted 11.5% year over year in the same period.

Barclays said a persistently higher deficit would increase Qatar’s issuance needs and the likelihood of tighter fiscal policy while weighing on non-hydrocarbon activity. The analysts nevertheless called 2027 a story of recovery as repair work gets underway at the Ras Laffan gas facilities.

Oil prices fell 0.5% as expectations grew for a widening supply surplus next year, despite persistent concerns over continued attacks in the Middle East. Brent crude traded at $91.93 a barrel and WTI at $85.29 a barrel, down around 16% and 14% on the month, respectively. ANZ analysts said “the supply flows are unsustainable over the longer term,” adding that “Logistics are under stress, while supply, inventories, refining and effective spare capacity are vulnerable.”

According to the latest Kpler data, Middle East oil flows, including shipments through Hormuz and alternative routes across the region, averaged more than 15.7 million barrels a day in the week through Nov. 15, below prewar levels of 23.3 million barrels a day.

Viking said historically low water levels hurt portions of its European river operations during the recent quarter, reducing third-quarter revenue and adjusted gross margin by about $100 million each. The cruise company attributed the reductions to guest cancellations and incremental costs tied to mitigation efforts. CEO Leah Talactac said on a call with analysts that some fluctuation in water level is typical. She described 2026 as unique: “2026 was unique due to the unprecedented combination of duration, severity and geographic breadth,” she said. “Multiple sections of the Rhine, Main and Danube rivers were affected simultaneously and remained impacted for an extended period.”

Talactac said conditions were beginning to improve and that water-level trends were expected to improve over the remainder of the year because of recent rainfall and forecasts for additional precipitation in the coming days. “As we have done in prior disruptions, we leveraged our experience, scale and operational flexibility to minimize the impact on our guests,” Talactac said. She said the company drew on nearly 30 years of river-cruising experience and its identical fleet of long ships to operate all scheduled voyages through ship swaps, itinerary modifications, alternative transportation and additional accommodations where needed.

UBS analysts said in a research note that lower jet-fuel costs and resilient demand created an increasingly favorable near-term setup for U.S. airlines. “Gulf Coast jet fuel is down 18% over the past month while our checks suggest demand remains strong and the industry continues to push through select price increases,” the analysts said. They saw upside to fourth-quarter EPS for most airlines despite the drag from the weekend’s FAA-mandated ground stop and moved their 2027 estimates meaningfully higher. UBS favored Allegiant, Southwest, Alaska and American in the near term, while picking Delta and United over the long term.

RBC Capital Markets analysts said FirstGroup’s stock looked cheap relative to earnings and cash flow and that the transport operator had plenty of spare cash and borrowing room to reward shareholders or buy businesses. After boosting its 100 million pounds buyback program by a further 50 million pounds, FirstGroup expects free cash flow of more than 500 million pounds over the next three years.

RBC lowered its FY 2027 EPS forecast by 2% because of higher upfront costs in the rail business, then raised forecasts for the following years by 1% to 2% as cost cuts and share buybacks would boost EPS. The analysts said upside from 2 pounds in bus fares beginning in January 2027 may be greater than their forecast. Shares fell 7% to 216.60 pence after FirstGroup reported lower adjusted EPS and operating profit margin for the first half.
