On Tuesday, Sen. Chuck Schumer accused newly reinstated Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang of shipping “extremely advanced chips to China” and violating U.S. export-control laws. Schumer said the move “amounts to the equivalent of selling rocket engines to the Soviets during the space race.”

Schumer added, “This is an unfathomable failure of enforcement by the Trump administration.” His comments put the administration's enforcement of export-control law at issue while questions remained over the chips-to-China accusations.
