## Both companies' boards approved the $175-per-share deal

Sanofi agreed on Nov. 24, 2026, to acquire U.S. drugmaker Insmed for $41 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal. The French pharmaceutical company said the transaction would complement its immunology and rare-disease portfolio.

Under the agreement, Sanofi will acquire all outstanding Insmed shares for $175 per share. Sanofi put the transaction's total value at $41 billion.

Sanofi said it expected to gain access to two first-in-class commercialized and pipeline products. The company said those products would strengthen its mid- to long-term growth outlook and its position in respiratory treatments.

The boards of Sanofi and Insmed approved the transaction, according to Sanofi. Sanofi also expects the acquisition to be earnings-per-share accretive by the end of 2028.
