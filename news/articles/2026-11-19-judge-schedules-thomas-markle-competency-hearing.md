## FBI filing describes seizure of approximately 78 items in California

A federal judge scheduled a mental competency hearing for Thursday afternoon in Tampa, Florida, for Thomas Markle, the 82-year-old estranged father of Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex. Markle was arrested in Lakeland two weeks before the hearing and charged in California with storing child sexual abuse material.

The hearing is the latest procedural step in a federal case involving suspected child sexual abuse images seized from California storage units. The prosecutor's office in the Philippines is separately assessing possible charges arising from digital devices found at Markle's former home in Cebu City.

Markle was released after his arrest at a senior care center. Four days later, on Nov. 10, a judge ordered him jailed after authorities accused him of cutting off his electronic ankle monitor. The judge also ordered Markle examined by a mental health professional.

The hearing is set for Thursday afternoon in federal court in Tampa. Prosecutors declined to comment Wednesday, and Markle's lawyer did not reply to an email seeking comment.

Markle's arrest followed the seizure of approximately 78 items from storage units in the Los Angeles area, according to an FBI court filing. The items included slides, film negatives and books that contained suspected child sexual abuse images. The FBI said some images appear to depict Markle with children.

Markle had a stroke earlier this year while living in the Philippines and had to have his left leg amputated. Those health problems forced him to return to the United States, where he settled in Florida.

The British tabloid The Sun reported that Meghan's half brother found child sexual abuse images while clearing out their father's home in the Philippines. The discovery led to a search of two U.S. storage units in California, which Markle had rented since 2008.

In the Philippines, Markle is under investigation for possession and intent to distribute images. The Women and Children Protection Center, a branch of the Philippine National Police, filed a formal criminal complaint against him on Monday, according to a written statement from police spokesperson Brig. Gen. Allen Rae Co.

The complaint was filed with the prosecutor's office following an investigation into digital devices found at Markle's former home in Cebu City. The prosecutor's office will assess the case for possible charges.

Markle did not attend Meghan's 2018 wedding to Prince Harry at Windsor Castle. Meghan said at the time that he needed to tend to his health. Meghan is an American citizen who starred in the U.S. television drama *Suits*.
