## Rubio says leader relationships shape trade and diplomacy

President Donald Trump suggested that Russian President Vladimir Putin and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy share equal responsibility for the continuing war in Ukraine. Speaking at a wide-ranging White House news conference in Washington on Friday, Trump made the suggestion when asked about the most recent attacks, hours after Putin met him in China.

“He’d love to end the war,” Trump said of Putin. He made the comment despite Russia's continued aggression following its invasion of Ukraine more than four years earlier.

Trump described the situation this way: “you have two guys that don’t know how to end it. We’re trying to help them. You have two people that hate each other, Zelensky and Putin. They don’t know how to end it. They want to end it. They’re both stuck in it.”

After returning from China, Trump praised both Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping. During the trip, Trump participated in bilateral and trilateral meetings with fellow heads of state, welcomed Putin and repeatedly referred to him as his “friend.”

Trump said his relationships with other world leaders were key to an eventual peace, telling reporters, “You know, you’re very lucky to have somebody that has a relationship with the heads of the most powerful countries.”

Trump also repeated longstanding grievances about the extended federal probe into Russian interference in the 2016 presidential election. The probe concluded that Russian agents sought to influence U.S. voters on Trump's behalf. Trump said he had been dealing with Putin since what he called the “fake Russia, Russia, Russia hoax,” referring to the Department of Justice investigation. He said, “It was a hoax made up by the Democrats.” He added that it “probably helped me win.”

Secretary of State Marco Rubio traveled with Trump to Asia, stood on stage during the news conference and made brief remarks. His comments responded to criticism that Trump was embracing autocratic leaders as trusted allies, the Associated Press reported.

“I want everybody to understand the relationships between heads of state are invaluable,” Rubio said. “It sets the tone for the entire relationship. It sets the tone for our trade negotiators. It sets the tone for our diplomats.”

Trump also maintained his description of Putin as a friend and made no effort in the White House briefing room to walk back the comments. Asked directly whether he viewed the Russian president as a friend to the United States, Trump said, “I can’t speak to America,” and did not elaborate.

Trump then described his personal relationship with Putin as “very good.” He said, “I consider him to be somebody that I have a very good relationship (with).” He added, “It’s actually very good.” Trump said the United States was trying to help the two leaders end the war, while his relationships with other world leaders were key to an eventual peace.

On Ukraine, Trump insisted that Putin wanted peace and suggested that Putin and Zelenskyy share equal responsibility for a conflict they wanted to end.

Associated Press author Bill Barrow reported from Atlanta.
