Internet-connected cameras — the doorbells on front porches, the traffic cameras at intersections, the security feeds inside homes — have become instruments of modern warfare, according to The Wall Street Journal. "From doorbells to traffic cameras, internet-connected cameras are becoming tools of modern warfare," the Journal wrote. "They are in our homes, on our doorsteps and along our streets."

The Journal said its reporters investigated the use of hacked cameras in conflicts from Ukraine to the Middle East. In one case they documented, hackers took control of a camera in Crimea and discovered a previously unknown Russian air base that Ukrainian forces later attacked.
