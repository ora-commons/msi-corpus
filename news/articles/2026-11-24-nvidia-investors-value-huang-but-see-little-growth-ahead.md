## Trump raises China concern as diversity-and-inclusion advocates call profit the only priority

Ricky Mulvey with The Indicator from Planet Money said investors have put the scandals aside and that Huang represents a significant portion of Nvidia’s value. Mulvey said the stock trades at about 10 times forward earnings, indicating investors expect to pay for 10 years of future profits now.

Investors pay more for other chipmakers, Mulvey said, implying those companies have more expected future growth. Despite Nvidia’s recent sales doubling over the past year, its investors are not anticipating much growth ahead.

NPR’s Danielle Kurtzleben told Up First that Trump’s involvement in a private business decision was significant, even though Kurtzleben said intervention in the economy is typical for the administration. In a CNBC interview, President Donald Trump said he was concerned that Chinese President Xi Jinping might lure Huang to China to enhance China’s chip business.

Some individuals advocating for diversity and inclusion in Silicon Valley argued that Huang’s return signals that profit is the only priority. The newsletter described Huang as a close ally of the president and said he had previously donated $25 million to the new White House ballroom and security complex.

The leadership change itself came the previous day. Nvidia reinstated Huang after his resignation two weeks earlier, which followed reports of extramarital affairs, including involvement with a woman U.S. intelligence agencies suspected of being a Chinese spy. President Trump claimed credit for facilitating the return.

Nvidia’s shares soared more than 30% when the company reinstated Huang, and the return triggered a rally in the entire stock market, the newsletter said. The newsletter described the scale of the reaction as showing that one man’s comeback was worth about a trillion dollars.

Despite mixed economic indicators, the newsletter said Trump frequently highlights stock-market performance. Trump said Huang’s reinstatement not only boosted Nvidia’s stock but could also uplift the entire market after a tough week.
