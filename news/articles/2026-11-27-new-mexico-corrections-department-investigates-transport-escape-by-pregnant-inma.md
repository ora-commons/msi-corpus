## Searchers say Taylor has ties to the Houston area and Albuquerque

The New Mexico Corrections Department is investigating how Rebecca Taylor, 26, took control of a corrections transport vehicle during a Whataburger stop with three correctional officers, spokesperson Brittany Roembach said. Taylor was not restrained during the drive because of her pregnancy, Roembach said. Taylor climbed from the back seat into the driver’s seat and drove away while an officer was standing nearby the vehicle, Roembach said.

According to court records, Rebecca Taylor pleaded guilty to four counts stemming from a series of vehicle thefts dating to 2024 and was sentenced to six years in prison.

Several law enforcement agencies searching for Rebecca Taylor said she has ties to the Houston area as well as Albuquerque. The agencies asked anyone who sees her to call 911.

Taylor fled Wednesday while being transported from a prenatal appointment in Albuquerque back to the Western New Mexico Correctional Facility in Grants, authorities said.

A second inmate exited the vehicle after Rebecca Taylor took control and remains in custody.

Police found the transport vehicle 3 miles (4.8 kilometers) away about an hour later, but Taylor was not inside.

Taylor’s orange prison jumpsuit and a transport cellphone issued by the Corrections Department were found in the same area Wednesday afternoon.
