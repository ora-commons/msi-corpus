## Nor'easter brought high winds and snow that toppled trees

In the PBS NewsHour broadcast, Nick Schifrin led with crews still working to restore power to hundreds of thousands of homes a day after a massive Thanksgiving Day storm forced some residents on the East Coast to dine in the dark. Most of the lingering outages were in Pennsylvania and Missouri, where the previous day's nor'easter brought not only high winds but also snow that toppled trees.

In Delaware, the rain had run its course, but trucks still moved through flooded streets.

In New York City, workers were preparing for the postponed Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, and tourists who had come in from across the world said they were grateful the show would go on. One woman interviewed for the broadcast said, "Well, at least it's going to happen on Saturday. So we cannot control the weather. We have to be resilient."

Even with improved conditions, the storms delayed thousands of flights. Airports were expected to get busier on Sunday, one of the peak travel days of the year, when travelers return from the Thanksgiving holiday.

On a shortened Black Friday trading day, stocks rebounded to end a volatile week. The Dow Jones industrial average rose by nearly 600 points, finishing its best week since 2022. The Nasdaq rose more than 2 percent, and the S&P 500 recorded its best week in more than a year. It was a choppy week, and stocks remain far off recent highs. Zelenskyy said Trump's calling Putin a friend was "strange" and added that Putin is an aggressor and a killer. "It's the same that somebody say about Hitler during the Second World War that he's a good friend. No, he's not a friend," Zelenskyy said.

At his Mar-a-Lago estate last night, Trump reiterated that Putin was welcome at next month's G20 summit, which he is hosting at his club in Doral, Florida, and defended his relationship with the Russian leader. "We want to make a deal. We want to stop killing. I could say he's not a friend, or we -- I want to make a deal. I got to get a deal done. I could walk in and say, I don't like you, and then we have the same problem that we have with Zelenskyy and Putin," Trump said.

Against the war's stalemate, nine Ukrainian children kidnapped by Russia as part of a campaign of abductions returned to their families in tearful reunions. One child had been displaced for nearly five years. Their release was brokered partly through first lady Melania Trump.

In the Persian Gulf, the U.S. military said it disabled a commercial ship trying to run the naval blockade of Iranian ports. Video shows the strike hitting the ship's stern, and CENTCOM said no one was hurt.

Separately, Kuwait lodged a formal note of protest with Iran after a drone strike in Kuwait killed two Italian nationals. Italy and Kuwait blamed Iran for the attack that hit an air defense station the previous day, and the victims were technicians working for a German arms manufacturer. Tehran has denied responsibility, and its foreign minister called the incident suspicious, saying it looked like "a precooked recipe for war."

Gaza saw some improvement in its food security since last year's cease-fire, but U.N. agencies warned that conditions remain fragile. The report cited armed conflict, soaring fuel costs, decreased humanitarian aid and extreme weather as leading drivers of food insecurity.
