## Few concrete outcomes beyond tariff truce, BBC reports

The summit followed multiple days of state-visit pageantry in Washington, during which Xi delivered pandas to the United States among other symbolic gestures, according to BBC News. The two leaders committed to extending their tariff truce for six months, though BBC News reported the meeting produced "few big movements" beyond that commitment.

BBC News characterized the state visit as combining "many symbolic gestures" — including the pandas Xi delivered to the United States — with what it called "few big movements" in policy terms.

The BBC's China correspondent Laura Bicker analyzed the outcome for both governments in a video report, according to the BBC. Her assessment, the BBC said, focused on how the trip "panned out for Beijing and Washington."
