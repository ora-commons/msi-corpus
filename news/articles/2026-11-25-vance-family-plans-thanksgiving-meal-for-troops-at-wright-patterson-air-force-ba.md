## Ohio lawmakers ask Trump administration to locate U.S. Space Academy

Vice President JD Vance, second lady Usha Vance and their four children are scheduled to serve a Thanksgiving meal to troops at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in southwest Ohio on Wednesday and join the troops for lunch, according to Vance’s office.

Vance’s office said the family plans to spend the holiday in Ohio. The base is about 60 miles (97 kilometers) from the Vances’ home in Cincinnati and close to where Vance was born.

The four children joining the trip are Ewan, 9; Vivek, 6; Mirabel, 4; and four-month-old Alec.

The visit comes just days after Ohio’s congressional delegation made a push for the Trump administration to locate the U.S. Space Academy close to the technologically advanced Wright-Patterson complex.

The delegation was led by Republican Sen. Bernie Moreno, a friend and former Senate colleague of Vance.

In the letter, Moreno wrote: “What West Point is to the Army and Annapolis is to the Navy, Wright Field should be to the United States Space Force.”

The letter was co-signed by GOP Sen. Jon Husted and all 15 of Ohio’s U.S. representatives.
