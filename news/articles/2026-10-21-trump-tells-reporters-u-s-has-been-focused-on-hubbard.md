## State Department classified Hubbard as wrongfully detained

President Donald Trump said Tuesday that the United States had been focused on Stephen Hubbard and that he had raised the American citizen’s case with President Vladimir Putin. “Watch what happens,” Trump told reporters, “we have been focused on Hubbard.”

BBC News reported that Russian state media said Hubbard had been taken to Pulkovo Airport during talks with U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff in St Petersburg. The state-media account said Putin had said Russia had agreed to release Hubbard, who had been jailed for allegedly fighting on behalf of Ukraine.

Hubbard was originally from Michigan. He was 72 when a Moscow court sentenced him in 2024 to almost seven years in a penal colony.

Russian prosecutors accused Hubbard of serving in a defence unit in the eastern city of Izyum when Russian forces captured him two years before the sentencing.

The U.S. State Department had previously classified Hubbard as “wrongfully detained.” The department’s classification and the Russian prosecutors’ allegation offered different official accounts of the basis for his detention.

A ReutersImage caption accompanying the BBC report showed Hubbard in a Moscow courtroom as he was sentenced in 2024.

The BBC said it had contacted the White House for comment.

The release agreement and airport transfer were attributed to Putin through Russian state media, while Trump’s remarks supplied his public account of U.S. efforts on the case. The differing official accounts of Hubbard’s detention remained unresolved.
