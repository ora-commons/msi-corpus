## PayPal’s stand-alone plans follow a stalled Stripe and Advent buyout

PayPal appointed Kartik Mani as president and head of consumer financial services and Venmo, bringing the American Express executive into a broader reshuffle of the digital payments company’s senior ranks. PayPal said Mani currently serves as president of global network and partner services at American Express.

The Wall Street Journal described Venmo as a popular payments app that generates meager profits. Under CEO Enrique Lores’s plan, turning the app into a one-stop money-management service is central to PayPal’s strategy, with proposed tools for consumers to keep budgets and invest.

That strategy is part of Lores’s effort to run PayPal as a stand-alone company after a proposed buyout from Stripe and Advent International, valued at more than $50 billion, stalled. Mani’s appointment places Venmo and PayPal’s broader consumer financial-services operation under a new leader.

PayPal also named Claire Bramley as its incoming chief financial officer. She is joining from Australian accounting-software provider Xero and will succeed Jamie Miller as finance chief the day after Miller leaves.

Miller, PayPal’s number-two executive, currently serves as executive vice president and chief financial and operating officer. PayPal said she will leave on Feb. 2 and receive severance payments and benefits related to a termination without cause. PayPal also said Miller briefly served as interim CEO in February after it ousted Alex Chriss, before Lores took the reins in March.

PayPal said Bramley will receive a $5 million cash signing bonus and a grant of $26 million in restricted stock units. Her annual base salary will be $875,000, and her annual bonus will carry a target of 125% of base pay.

In comparing the compensation packages, The Wall Street Journal said Miller had received a $6 million signing bonus and $14.5 million in restricted stock units. The report linked that package to a late-2023 hiring, but elsewhere said Miller joined PayPal as CFO in 2013.

The appointments put new executives in charge of Venmo, consumer financial services and PayPal’s finance function as Lores pursues the stand-alone plan. Venmo’s expansion from a payments app into a money-management service is a central part of that strategy.
