## Energy secretary resignation remains possible in first major row under Burnham

The Guardian's Politics Weekly UK podcast, published on 26 November 2026, said close to 100 Labour MPs had sent Andy Burnham a letter calling on him to reject the Rosebank oilfield licence. The episode described the dispute as the first major internal party row under Burnham.

The Guardian said potential resignations, including from energy secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh, were not off the table. The description treated those departures as possibilities rather than announcements.

Environmental campaigners said the Rosebank project would create massive greenhouse emissions and conflict with the UK's climate goals.

Supporters of the project said they believed it could bring investment and tax revenues. The episode presented those claims alongside the MPs' call for Burnham to reject the licence.

Politics Weekly UK was presented by Kiran Stacey and Peter Walker. Annie La Vespa produced the episode, Axel Kacoutié supplied the music, and Maz Ebtehaj served as executive producer.
