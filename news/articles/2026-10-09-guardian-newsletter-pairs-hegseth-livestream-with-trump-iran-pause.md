Hegseth said the broadcast would show "serious consequences" for Hasan's role in the November 5, 2009, Fort Hood shooting, in which the gunman killed 13 people and wounded dozens at a medical processing center. The Guardian described the planned broadcast as "a radical departure from the previous norm" and quoted Hegseth as saying: "We'll make sure that people are able to watch it, that it's public, because people need to understand there's serious consequences for these types of things." A military jury sentenced Hasan to death in 2013, and the Guardian reported the last known case of a U.S. military firing squad was in November 1945. Hasan has been held at Fort Leavenworth in Kansas since his conviction.
