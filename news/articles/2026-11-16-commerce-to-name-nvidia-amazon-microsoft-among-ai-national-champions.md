## Designated firms can receive expedited chip export licenses and federal financing

The Trump administration plans to name Nvidia and more than two dozen other companies as artificial intelligence "national champions," people familiar with the plans said. The designation is intended to help American AI firms compete for business abroad. Under the program, named companies can receive expedited approvals for licenses to export chips, government support for their overseas work and access to federal financing, according to the Commerce Department's website for the program. The program was initially announced about a year ago, but the companies had not been named.

The announcement, which is expected within the next week, indicates the administration is pressing ahead with its tech strategy despite the turmoil surrounding Nvidia, The Wall Street Journal reported. The national champions initiative is part of an effort to get foreign companies and countries to adopt U.S. AI technology over Chinese rivals.

Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic are among the companies set to be designated, people familiar with the matter said. The aim is to package U.S. chips, models and other AI products so that customers in other countries can purchase them together.

The Trump administration has been focused on exporting AI technology around the world to stay ahead of China since last summer, when it announced the concept as a pillar of its tech strategy. The national champions initiative sits inside that broader effort to steer foreign buyers toward U.S. AI technology rather than Chinese rivals.

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is expected to announce the plan, the people said. Lutnick has been under pressure for what many critics say is his agency's failure to crack down on loopholes that allow Chinese companies to access high-end Nvidia chips, and The Wall Street Journal previously reported that Lutnick was reluctant to anger China during trade talks. Lutnick is expected to join President Trump on his trip to China this week, while his agency is set to move forward with fresh rules restricting chip diversion to China. The national champions announcement date could still slip, some of the people said.

The revelation that former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang was having an affair with a suspected Chinese intelligence source who worked for a Singapore-based data-center company has fueled fresh calls from hawks to prioritize national security over market share, including by tightening export controls on chips.

Nvidia's chips power the most advanced AI models and are among the world's most valuable commodities, and President Trump has repeatedly praised Huang for building a homegrown champion powering the AI boom.

AI is the latest tech arena in which the United States and China are competing for global dominance. Chinese companies such as Huawei Technologies expanded aggressively overseas to markets including Europe and Latin America in an effort to dominate the global 5G ecosystem.
