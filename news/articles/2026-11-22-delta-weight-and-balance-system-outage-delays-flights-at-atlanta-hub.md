## 48% of Delta flights were delayed by midday Sunday, FlightAware says

Delta Air Lines said Sunday that an issue with its weight and balance automation system was causing delays at its hub in Atlanta and across the country, and that its operations teams were relying on manual workarounds to keep departures moving.

In a statement, Delta said its teams were using the manual workarounds to get flights off the ground while they continued to work to restore the automation system. The airline did not say in the statement when it expected the system to be back in service.

The Federal Aviation Administration said on X that Delta was working to resolve the issues, and that the delays were not related to any FAA air traffic systems. The statement placed the disruption with the airline's own systems rather than with the government's air traffic network.

The flight-tracking website FlightAware said that as of midday Sunday, 48% of Delta's flights were delayed. The effects concentrated at the airline's largest hub: FlightAware said 38% of flights out of Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport were delayed, while about 36% of flights destined for the airport were delayed.

The outage arrived as Americans begin traveling for the Thanksgiving holiday this week, one of the busiest stretches of the U.S. travel calendar.

It also extended a month of technology-driven disruption across U.S. aviation. Earlier this month, a malfunction in the FAA's network led to the longest nationwide ground stop in 25 years; the stop lasted for more than four hours, and carriers canceled and delayed swaths of flights as they worked to recover. Just days after that, United Airlines saw its own system outage, which forced it to delay departing flights.

Sunday's problem differed in origin and scope from those episodes. The FAA said the Delta delays did not involve its air traffic systems, and Delta described a single airline's internal automation as the source of the disruption rather than a nationwide halt ordered by regulators. The airline said it was still working to bring the system back online as departures continued under manual processing.
