## The preliminary price range implied a $1.26 trillion to $1.41 trillion valuation

Anthropic set the share price for its U.S. stock market debut at $83 on Wednesday, above the preliminary range of $70 to $78 it set last week. The San Francisco-based artificial intelligence company, founded in 2021, is scheduled to begin trading Thursday on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT. CEO Dario Amodei and his fellow co-founders plan to ring the bell to open the exchange.

With all shares counted, the preliminary $70-to-$78 range implied an Anthropic valuation of $1.26 trillion to $1.41 trillion. That range would rank Anthropic's IPO as the second-biggest market offering ever, behind Elon Musk's SpaceX, which debuted on Wall Street in June with a $1.77 trillion valuation, The Guardian reported.

Amodei plans to sell about $260 million worth of shares with the IPO, as do his six fellow co-founders. At the top of the price range, the co-founders' stakes would be worth more than $18 billion each.

Anthropic's investor prospectus lists computing power from datacenters as the company's main expenditure. It reports compute purchase commitments and lease agreements of more than $70 billion over the next 12 months, more than $450 billion by 2030 and a further $450 billion due by 2045.

Anthropic is the first company at the forefront of the generative AI boom to go public. The Guardian said its performance is likely to affect investor enthusiasm as the company makes its public-market debut.

OpenAI had been slated to reach Wall Street later this year, but CEO Sam Altman said the company delayed its IPO until it could overcome safety concerns.

The Anthropic IPO comes during a volatile period for AI and the stock market. Last month, the industry saw a broader slowdown, with share prices dropping at several companies amid growing concern about the technology.

The Guardian reported that Nvidia, which designs the chips AI companies use to power their chatbots, was hit by a scandal last week. The outlet said the episode led CEO Jensen Huang to resign after reports of multiple extramarital affairs.
