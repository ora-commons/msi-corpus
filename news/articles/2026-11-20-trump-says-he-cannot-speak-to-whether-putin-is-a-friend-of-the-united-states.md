## Trump says Putin and Zelenskyy both remain 'stuck' in the Ukraine war

Donald Trump, the Republican president, said at a Friday briefing that he could not speak to whether Russian President Vladimir Putin was a friend to the United States. Days earlier in China, Trump had repeatedly referred to Putin as his "friend," and he did not disavow that description on Friday. Asked whether the Russian president was a friend to the United States, Trump said, "I can't speak to America," without further explanation.

He then turned to his personal relationship with Putin. "I consider him to be somebody that I have a very good relationship (with)," Trump said. "It's actually very good."

Trump then raised his longstanding grievances over the extended federal probe into Russian attempts to influence U.S. voters in the 2016 presidential election. The remarks on the probe followed his answer about Putin's friendship to the United States and his description of their personal relationship.

Before the briefing, Trump had taken part in bilateral and trilateral meetings with Putin and China's Xi Jinping. After those meetings, he described his relationship with Putin as "very good" and said relationships with other world leaders were key to an eventual peace in Ukraine.

On the war in Ukraine, Trump said Putin would "love to end the war" but described the Russian president and Ukrainian leader Volodymyr Zelenskyy as "stuck in it." "You have two guys that don't know how to end it," he said. "We're trying to help them. You have two people that hate each other."

Trump said his relationships with other world leaders were key to an eventual peace. "You know, you're very lucky to have somebody that has a relationship with the heads of the most powerful countries," he said.

Trump's answer distinguished his own relationship with Putin from the question of whether the Russian president was a friend to the United States. He called the relationship "very good," said he was trying to help Putin and Zelenskyy, and said relationships with other world leaders were key to an eventual peace in Ukraine.
