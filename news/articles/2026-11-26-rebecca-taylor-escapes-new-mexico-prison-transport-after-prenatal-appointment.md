## Multi-agency search underway as Taylor remains on the run

Rebecca Taylor, 26, remains on the run after escaping from a prison transport vehicle in Albuquerque, according to the New Mexico Corrections Department. Local media reported that Taylor took control of the Dodge Journey and drove away when the transport team stopped to pick up food in Albuquerque on Wednesday morning. The corrections department said Taylor was being returned to prison from a prenatal appointment.

The other inmate in the car was able to get out as Taylor took control, local media reported. That inmate was also returning from a prenatal appointment. The New Mexico Corrections Department said the vehicle and Taylor's prison jumpsuit were later recovered.

Taylor had been imprisoned for multiple theft and burglary charges, the corrections department said in a Facebook statement.

The Albuquerque Journal reported that Taylor is in her second trimester of pregnancy. Department spokesperson Brittany Roembach said Taylor was not restrained during transportation because of the pregnancy.

Roembach said: "It is the understanding right now that one officer was with the vehicle but not inside of the vehicle when this occurred."

All prisons in New Mexico went into lockdown following the escape, and the lockdown was lifted at 06:00 on Thursday. The Western New Mexico Corrections Facility, where Taylor was being held, will remain on "modified movement" with no visitors currently allowed.

The public has been told not to approach Rebecca Taylor if they see her and to call police straight away. The BBC said it had approached the New Mexico Corrections Department for an update.

Taylor's whereabouts remained unresolved in the corrections department's account, which said she remained on the run while the multi-agency search continued.
