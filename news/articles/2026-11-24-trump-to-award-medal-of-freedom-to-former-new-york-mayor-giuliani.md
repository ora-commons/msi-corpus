## Giuliani was disbarred in New York and Washington for false election statements

President Donald Trump will award the nation's highest civilian honor, the Medal of Freedom, to former New York Mayor Rudy Giuliani on Tuesday, according to the Associated Press. Trump said last year that he would award the medal to Giuliani, shortly after he was seriously injured in a car crash.

Giuliani led New York through the Sept. 11, 2001 attacks and afterward. Trump, describing Giuliani as a fellow New Yorker, has called him the “greatest Mayor in the history of New York City, and an equally great American Patriot.”

Giuliani mounted a brief campaign for the Republican presidential nomination. The Associated Press described him as one of the country's most recognizable political figures after the campaign.

Giuliani later served as Trump's personal lawyer. In that role, he was a central figure in efforts to overturn Trump's 2020 presidential election loss to Democrat Joe Biden.

Courts rejected the fraud claims Giuliani advanced. Two former Georgia election workers won a $148 million defamation judgment against him.

Courts sanctioned Giuliani over his amplification of false claims about the 2020 election. He was disbarred in New York and Washington for repeatedly making false statements about the election.

In his second term, Trump has awarded the medal posthumously to Welles Crowther, an equities trader who became known as the “man in the red bandanna” after rescuing several people in the Sept. 11, 2001 attacks. Trump also posthumously honored Charlie Kirk, the assassinated activist who inspired a generation of young conservatives and pushed the nation's politics to the right.

During his first term, Trump awarded the medal to golf legend Tiger Woods, former football coach Lou Holtz, conservative economist Arthur Laffer, New York Yankees Hall of Fame closer Mariano Rivera and conservative radio host Rush Limbaugh. The Limbaugh honor came during the 2020 State of the Union.

Trump also awarded posthumous medals to Babe Ruth and Elvis Presley.
