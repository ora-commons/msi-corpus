## Garcia has taken steps ahead of January House Oversight chairmanship

Rep. Robert Garcia, a Democrat from California, will become chairman of the House Oversight Committee in January, according to a profile published Nov. 23 in The Wall Street Journal's Politics newsletter. The newsletter says Garcia has already taken steps to "hit the ground running, brushing aside White House warnings and putting people on notice."

The publication's characterization of the job Garcia is taking is direct: the House Oversight chairmanship is "an extremely powerful perch that could make him the de facto top prosecutor for Democrats" — the profile notes — while pointing out that Garcia is not a lawyer.

The profile is written as an introduction to the incoming chairman — a short guide to the lawmaker who will hold the committee's top seat in January and to the steps he has taken already.

Damian Paletta, The Wall Street Journal's Washington coverage chief, wrote the profile. In the newsletter's byline note, Paletta says he has covered Washington for 22 years as a reporter and editor, including the White House, Congress, national security, the federal budget, economics and multiple market meltdowns.
