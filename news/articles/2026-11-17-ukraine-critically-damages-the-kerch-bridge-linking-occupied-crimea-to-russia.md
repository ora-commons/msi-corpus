## Photos suggest a 227-metre road arch span fell into the Kerch strait

Sergey Aksyonov, the Kremlin-appointed governor of Crimea, confirmed that the bridge was no longer usable, and photos suggest that a 227-metre road arch span has fallen into the Kerch strait. Video released by the Ukrainian Security Service shows damage to the crossing.

The strike followed an earlier attack on Monday that targeted an arch across the bridge's central section, and the bridge was temporarily closed on Monday. Ukraine has declared the bridge illegal and has repeatedly attempted to destroy it. The crossing was built eight years ago after Vladimir Putin's 2014 annexation of Crimea.

Aksyonov's confirmation that the crossing is unusable suggests the damage outlasts Monday's temporary closure, while Ukrainian officials have framed the strike as a symbolic blow to the Kremlin.
