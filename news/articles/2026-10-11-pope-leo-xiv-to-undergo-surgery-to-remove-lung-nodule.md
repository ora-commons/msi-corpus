## Vatican sets surgery for early next week with weeklong recovery

"It was detected at a very early stage and is well localized," Leo said in an appearance from the Apostolic Palace. "The doctors believe it is best to intervene, so, following their advice, I have decided to undergo surgery to have it removed."
