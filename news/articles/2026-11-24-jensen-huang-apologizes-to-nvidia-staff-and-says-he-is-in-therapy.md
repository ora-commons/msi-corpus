## Trump administration intelligence informed Nvidia's assessment of Huang's return

Nvidia co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang apologized to employees and said he was in therapy during an all-hands meeting Tuesday at the company's Santa Clara, Calif., headquarters, according to people familiar with the matter. Nvidia had reinstated him Monday, 11 days after he resigned over two extramarital affairs.

Huang thanked the Trump administration for providing Nvidia with quick access to intelligence, according to the people familiar with his remarks. He said the assistance helped the board determine that the national-security risks of reinstating him were low.

The people familiar with the remarks said Huang was thankful for the second chance at the company and would work hard for “a second chance with my family.” Huang has been married to his wife for more than 40 years, and his children work at Nvidia.

Employees lined the stairs and balconies of Nvidia's office on Monday and shouted “We love you, Jensen!” as Huang returned. The welcome followed the board's reinstatement of Nvidia's co-founder as chief executive.

Huang admitted to violating Nvidia's code of conduct during Tuesday's meeting, the people familiar with the remarks said.

The Wall Street Journal reported that Huang made a similar disclosure to Nvidia's board on Sunday, admitting that he had carried on affairs beyond the two that had received widespread media coverage.

The Wall Street Journal reported that the two widely covered affairs included one with an employee and another with Alice Huang, an executive at a customer of Nvidia whom U.S. officials suspected was a Chinese intelligence asset.

Huang told staff that Nvidia had begun a process under which his direct reports would send reviews of his behavior to the board, the people familiar with the remarks said.

President Donald Trump had championed Huang's return, saying only Huang was capable of running Nvidia properly. Trump also said the stock market's exuberant response to the reinstatement proved it was the right decision.


The all-hands meeting followed Nvidia's reinstatement of Huang after [Vice President JD Vance's weekend lobbying of the board](/articles/2026-11-23-vance-helps-persuade-nvidia-s-board-to-reinstate-jensen-huang/) and [President Donald Trump's public support for his return](/articles/2026-11-23-jensen-huang-returns-as-nvidia-ceo-after-trump-s-intervention/). Tuesday's disclosures place Huang's conduct review alongside the government intervention surrounding his reinstatement.
