The Guardian reported that the New York congressman also promised to take on the Trump administration’s Maga approach to governing the U.S.

The 251-11 closed-door vote on Wednesday secured Jeffries’s nomination for House speaker, but a full House vote remained ahead. The New York congressman has served as the House Democratic minority leader since 2023; if the House elected him, his service would begin when the new Congress is sworn in next year.

The Guardian said Jeffries is set to become one of President Donald Trump’s most prominent opponents during the final two years of his presidency. The Guardian also said Democrats will control both the Senate and House of Representatives when the new Congress is sworn in next year and are expected to undertake aggressive efforts to check his powers.

Jeffries stood to become the first ever Black speaker of the House. He would also be the first Democratic House speaker since Nancy Pelosi, who will retire from Congress at the end of this year. Both distinctions remained contingent on the full House vote.

According to The Guardian, voters swept Democrats back into power in the House and Senate with significant majorities.
