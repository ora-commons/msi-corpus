## Direct-to-consumer sales rose 25% as all three regions set sales records

On Tuesday, On Holding, the Swiss running-shoe maker, reported third-quarter net income of 162.8 million Swiss francs ($198.5 million), up from 118.9 million Swiss francs in the same quarter a year earlier. Net sales increased 17% to 931.4 million Swiss francs.

Adjusted earnings excluding one-time items came in at CHF0.54 a share, above the adjusted earnings of CHF0.39 a share expected by analysts polled by FactSet. The Wall Street Journal reported that the sales result was in line with Wall Street models.

On Holding’s direct-to-consumer sales jumped 25%, while sales through wholesale channels climbed 12%.

By region, net sales rose 12% in the Americas, 18% in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and 31% in Asia-Pacific. On Holding said each region had achieved its highest quarterly net sales to date.

On Holding said it continued to hold full prices in a promotional market. Founder and Co-CEO Caspar Coppetti said, “In a promotional market, we stayed true to who we are and held full price, delivering record sales and record underlying margins.”

For the full year, On Holding continued to expect net sales of CHF3.47 billion to CHF3.56 billion. Analysts were looking for sales of CHF3.51 billion, within the company’s forecast range.

On Holding raised its projected gross profit margin to around 67.5%, or around 66% excluding U.S. tariff refunds. The company previously had guided for gross profit margin of at least 65%.

The company also reaffirmed its outlook for the margin on adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization to be in a range of 21% to 21.5%. Excluding U.S. tariff refunds, On Holding’s outlook was 19.5% to 20%.

On Holding shares slipped 4.1% to $35.98 in premarket trading.
