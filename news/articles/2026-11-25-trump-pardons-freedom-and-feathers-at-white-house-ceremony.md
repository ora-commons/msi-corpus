## Trump used the event to promote a new ballroom complex

President Donald Trump pardoned the Thanksgiving turkeys Freedom and Feathers at a White House ceremony on Tuesday, and both birds will live out their days at Texas A&M University. The event on the south lawn doubled as a showcase for a ballroom complex under construction, which Trump described as a ballroom military complex and said would be the strongest building ever built in the country.

The Guardian wrote that some people saw Trump's pardons of January 6 rioters or the deployment of masked ICE agents as actions that had gone too far, while others pointed to Elon Musk's chainsaw or the demolition of the White House East Wing. It said Trump's second term had repeatedly prompted “this is the last straw!” reactions, but that on Tuesday, even Trump had gone too far.

The Guardian described the Thanksgiving turkey pardoning ceremony as typically an occasion for levity, with dad jokes, bad puns, bipartisan spirit and a rare glimpse of presidential self-awareness and humility, but wrote that levity was in short supply at Trump's appearance. At his 2010 ceremony, after the Democrats' midterm losses, Barack Obama quipped: “It feels pretty good to stop at least one shellacking this November.” The Guardian wrote, “Perhaps he was still scarred by his humiliatingly unfunny turn at the White House Correspondents’ Association dinner.” Trump devoted part of his Tuesday remarks to his own record, his White House renovations and the ballroom construction visible from the lawn.

Guests sat on the White House's new circular granite helipad on the south lawn. Trump said the structure was “supposed to land helicopters,” called it “as good as you can get” and declared its location “locationally perfect.” He added that the helipad would be there “for hopefully hundreds of years” and that the White House used it for other purposes as well.

Welcoming guests, Trump claimed the White House had “never looked better than it’s looked right now.” He asked, “Do you mind if I turn around and look?” before turning toward the north face of the White House, which was dressed with seasonal wreaths. Trump said he had been criticized for fixing broken windows and moldings and for making the building look better. He said the repairs had been worthwhile — estimating that he had spent “10 minutes a day on it” — and called the White House “the symbol of our country.” The Guardian called the estimate generous and said Trump had once reportedly been found supergluing gold appliqués to the Oval Office fireplace.

A Guardian photograph showed Trump with Melania Trump and National Turkey Federation chair Christy Puffenbarger. The guest list additionally included outgoing House speaker Mike Johnson, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and Attorney General Todd Blanche. Trump called Bessent “central casting,” said of Hegseth: “who’s been fantastic,” and said of Blanche: “Todd has been very busy.”

When he introduced the farmers from West Virginia who provided the year's turkeys, named Freedom and Feathers, Trump said: “We won West Virginia by 45 points.” He added, “At least, I did when I was on the ballot.” Trump said he did not know what had happened and added: “Somebody please explain it to me.”

As in past years, the turkeys had stayed overnight at the nearby Willard Hotel, a tradition Trump said was coming to an end. He announced that next year the turkeys would be housed in “that rather small ballroom being built,” adding that the space would host “a lot of people and a couple of turkeys next year.”

The Guardian said Trump had made clear over the past two years that the project was what he “really cares about.” Construction of the complex was visible from the lawn: a giant structure of concrete and steel that dwarfed workers in helmets, overalls and yellow vests while hammers rang on pipes. Trump said the project would serve as a “ballroom military complex” and, while acknowledging that he expected criticism, said he believed it would be the strongest building ever built in the country. He also relayed what others had told him — that it was missile-proof, drone-proof, bulletproof “and everything else” — and predicted it would be “the most beautiful ballroom ever built.” He also said the project would represent the country and fit nicely.

The address continued in a rally-style register. Trump said the past two years had been “amazing” and “incredible” and claimed the stock market had added $25tn in wealth before losing $6tn because of the midterm election result.

The Guardian reported that Trump had just gotten Jensen Huang reinstated at Nvidia despite an alleged affair with a Chinese spy.

Trump placed his hand on Freedom's back and said “Congratulations.” Both turkeys received the ceremonial pardon, and both will live out their days at Texas A&M University. The Guardian said Trump might be a lame duck but still had the power to pardon a turkey or two.
