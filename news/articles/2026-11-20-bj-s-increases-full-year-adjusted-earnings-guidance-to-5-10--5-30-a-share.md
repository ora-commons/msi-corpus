## BJ's reported 14% comparable-club sales growth in the third quarter

BJ's Wholesale Club moved its full-year adjusted earnings forecast higher on Friday, lifting the range to $5.10 to $5.30 a share from $4.60 to $4.80. The Wall Street Journal said the higher outlook reflected higher profit and revenue in the recent quarter and accelerating same-store sales, as third-quarter adjusted earnings topped analyst expectations and quarterly revenue exceeded Wall Street estimates.

A market quote supplied with the source showed BJ's Wholesale (ticker BJ) shares down 0.12%.

The new earnings range was above the $4.74 full-year adjusted earnings analysts expected, according to FactSet. BJ's raised the outlook after a third quarter that Chief Executive Bob Eddy described as strong, with traffic and membership growth driving the results.

For the quarter ended Oct. 31, BJ's reported a profit of $219.5 million, or $1.73 a share, compared with $152.1 million, or $1.15 a share, a year earlier. After BJ's stripped out one-time items, adjusted earnings also totaled $1.73 a share, above the $1.26 analysts had expected.

Total revenue increased 17% to $6.28 billion, topping the Wall Street estimate of $5.97 billion. Comparable-club sales, which account for store openings and closings, rose 14% for the quarter, or 4.1% when gasoline sales were excluded. Analysts had expected a 2.7% gain in comparable-club sales.

For the full year, BJ's raised the low end of its comparable-club sales forecast excluding gasoline to growth of 2.5% from 2%, while maintaining the high end at 3%. FactSet analysts were looking for 2.6% growth.

Chief Executive Bob Eddy said BJ's delivered a strong third quarter driven by both traffic and membership growth.

“We seized a unique opportunity to invest in our stakeholders,” he said. “We’re excited about the momentum across our strategic priorities, and we remain committed to investing in growth for the long term.”

BJ's reported adjusted earnings of $1.73 a share and revenue of $6.28 billion for the quarter ended Oct. 31. On Friday, after the quarter had ended, BJ's raised its annual adjusted earnings range to $5.10 to $5.30 from $4.60 to $4.80, above FactSet's $4.74 estimate. BJ's full-year forecast for comparable-club sales excluding gasoline of 2.5% to 3% included FactSet's 2.6% growth expectation.
