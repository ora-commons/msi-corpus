## Trump: work with Democrats if they want common ground; veto what he opposes

Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress on Tuesday, taking the House and Senate in midterm elections that cost Republicans Senate seats in Maine, North Carolina, South Carolina, Texas, Ohio, Kansas and Iowa. The result leaves President Trump with two years left in office and a divided government. Damian Paletta, The Wall Street Journal's Washington coverage chief, wrote that Trump must decide quickly among three paths — cutting deals with Democrats, trying to use them as a foil, or continuing the way he has led since January 2025 — and likely won't have the same control over his own party that he had before.

Paletta also wrote that Trump is now officially a lame duck and will face a number of tough decisions almost immediately. Ahead of Tuesday's vote, Trump had cast the economy as being on solid ground, but voters disagreed and blamed him for higher prices, with close to two-thirds saying inflation was up more because of his policies.
