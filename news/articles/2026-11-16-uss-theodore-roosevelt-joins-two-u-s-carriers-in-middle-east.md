## Ford and Lincoln set modern-day records for deployment length and time at sea

Three U.S. aircraft carriers are now operating in the Middle East for the second time this year, after the aircraft carrier USS Theodore Roosevelt arrived in the region, The Wall Street Journal reported on Nov. 16, 2026.

The warship joins USS George Washington and USS George H.W. Bush. Its arrival gives the U.S. added capacity to fend off Iranian attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, enforce the blockade and give President Trump expanded options to strike Iran should he choose to do so, the Journal reported.

The U.S. has kept at least two carriers in the region since the war started, and so far five of the Navy's 11 aircraft carriers have deployed to support the war, according to the Journal.

The tempo has left its mark on the fleet. Two of those carriers, USS Gerald R. Ford and USS Abraham Lincoln, have set modern-day records for their length of deployment and their time at sea without a port call. The deployment of USS George H.W. Bush, which is in the Arabian Sea, has also been extended.

Journalists from The Wall Street Journal visited USS George Washington in the Arabian Sea in October to learn more about how these carriers are conducting operations, and how the war is testing the limits of the Navy's carrier strike groups. The carrier normally operates in the Western Pacific but was pulled into the Middle East in August to relieve the Lincoln.
