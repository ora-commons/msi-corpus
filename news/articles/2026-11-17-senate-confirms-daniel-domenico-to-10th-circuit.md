## Tymkovich’s move to senior judge status created the vacancy

The U.S. Senate confirmed Daniel Domenico to the 10th Circuit Court of Appeals on Monday in a 51-46 vote, making him the third Trump appointee on the Denver-based panel of 12 judges. Domenico serves as a judge on the U.S. District Court of Colorado, where he briefly served as chief judge this year. Colorado's two U.S. senators, Democrats Michael Bennet and John Hickenlooper, voted against his confirmation.

President Donald Trump nominated Domenico for the court of appeals in May. Trump had also nominated him to the U.S. District Court of Colorado during his first presidency in 2017.

A spokesperson for Hickenlooper said Domenico's judicial record and his responses during a Judiciary Committee hearing on his nomination raise concerns about his fitness for a lifetime appointment. Domenico declined to answer when a senator on the committee asked him who won the 2020 election. Hickenlooper's office said that refusal and his record on immigration “were troubling and inconsistent with the high standards we should expect from federal judges.”

The 10th Circuit hears appeals of district-court decisions from Colorado, Oklahoma, Kansas, New Mexico, Wyoming and Utah, as well as portions of Yellowstone National Park in Montana and Idaho. The Denver-based court's decisions can be appealed to the U.S. Supreme Court.

Judge Timothy Tymkovich announced earlier this year that he would retire as an active judge on the appeals court and serve as a senior judge, creating the vacancy Domenico filled. Domenico had clerked for Tymkovich in 2003.

Domenico joins Allison Eid and Joel Carson, the other 10th Circuit judges Trump appointed during his first term. He previously served as solicitor general for Colorado, arguing cases before the U.S. Supreme Court, the 10th Circuit and the Colorado Supreme Court on the state's behalf across three governors.

News From The States reported that in 2022 Domenico had the fourth-biggest backlog of pending motions among all U.S. district and magistrate judges across the country.

Trump will nominate a replacement for Domenico on the U.S. District Court of Colorado. U.S. senators are typically involved in the nomination process for district court judges in their state.
