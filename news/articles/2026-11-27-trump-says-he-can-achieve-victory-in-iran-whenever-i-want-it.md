## Trump keeps blockade and sweeping sanctions; Iran has not accepted U.S. demands

President Trump said Thursday that he could achieve victory in Iran “whenever I want it” and that he is giving Tehran “a chance to be smart.” The comments came as the United States continued to enforce its naval blockade on Iran, with efforts to diplomatically end the conflict stalled.

Trump in recent months has opted to maintain the blockade and sweeping sanctions on Iran to continue squeezing the country economically, rather than commit to heavy strikes.

Officials and analysts said the blockade has sapped Iran of vital oil revenue and pushed its economy to the brink of collapse.

Iran has so far not accepted the two U.S. demands — that it give up its nuclear program and allow free commercial navigation through the Strait of Hormuz, an important global energy maritime chokepoint. Both demands remain unmet, according to The Wall Street Journal, which reported that Iran “has so far refused to bow” to U.S. demands.

The United States is enforcing its naval blockade on Iran as efforts to diplomatically end the conflict have stalled, and the enforcement campaign has reached well beyond any single vessel. U.S. forces have redirected 158 ships and disabled six that refused to comply with warnings not to run the blockade, according to Centcom.

That campaign continued this week. U.S. Central Command, which oversees U.S. operations in the Middle East, announced on Friday that a U.S. aircraft had struck and disabled the Palau-flagged commercial vessel M/V Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman.

A U.S. aircraft on Thursday struck the stern of the Palau-flagged M/V Pacific Archer, a bulk carrier the Pentagon accused of attempting to run the U.S. blockade after departing the Iranian port city of Bandar Abbas.

Centcom said in a statement that it disabled the ship after multiple warnings and that the strike left the civilian crew unharmed.

Trump spoke on Thursday, the same day a U.S. aircraft struck the Pacific Archer. Neither development changed the terms of the standoff: Iran has not accepted the two U.S. demands, the United States continues to enforce the blockade and its sanctions, and diplomatic efforts remain stalled.


Earlier, MSI reported that U.S. forces disabled the Palau-flagged tanker M/T Settebello in the Gulf of Oman after nearly 60 warnings, according to a U.S. official, an incident that killed three Indian sailors, and documented a separate run of strikes on three tankers over three days.
