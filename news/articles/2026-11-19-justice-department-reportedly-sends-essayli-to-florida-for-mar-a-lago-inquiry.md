## Jason Reding Quiñones resigns as US attorney for southern district of Florida

Bill Essayli, a California prosecutor whom The Guardian describes as a "staunch ally of the president," is being dispatched by the US Department of Justice to Florida to handle a criminal inquiry into the 2022 search of Mar-a-Lago and Jack Smith's handling of the classified documents case, according to reports by Politico, the New York Times and Bloomberg Law. Politico first reported the assignment, and Justice Department spokespeople did not immediately respond to inquiries on Thursday.

The reported assignment comes as the US attorney's office for the southern district of Florida deals with a stalled criminal investigation into whether Obama and Biden administration officials violated Trump's rights. Jason Reding Quiñones, the US attorney for that office, resigned earlier this month, and other prosecutors working on the case have resigned.

Essayli's assignment concerns the 2022 search of Mar-a-Lago and Jack Smith's handling of the classified documents case. The broader criminal investigation now stalled in the Florida office asks whether Obama and Biden administration officials violated Trump's rights.

Trump has long railed about the raid, framing his 2024 presidential run in part as vengeance over what he asserts was political persecution under the Biden justice department. He has called the Mar-a-Lago search a "siege" and Smith a "sick son of a bitch."

News of the reported assignment comes weeks after a federal judge ruled that Essayli was "unlawfully serving" as head of the US attorney's office in Los Angeles and should be disqualified from overseeing two criminal prosecutions. Defense lawyers have fought for months to have him removed, and the judge's ruling is under appeal.

Essayli was not formally nominated by Trump. He was appointed "interim" US attorney in April 2025, a role that allowed him to lead the office without Senate confirmation, and the Trump administration has since kept him at the top of the office under the title "first assistant."

On 13 August 2026, Essayli announced the findings of a federal investigation into alleged sexual abuse of female inmates by staff.

The Guardian reported that over the last year Essayli's office had aggressively prosecuted protesters and local residents for alleged crimes against immigration officers, including "conspiring" against and "impeding" agents. The Guardian described Essayli as a Trump loyalist and rightwing ideologue who has made national headlines pursuing cases closely aligned with the Maga agenda.

The Guardian's recent review found that as of mid-September, the majority of more than 100 cases brought by his office had ended in dismissals, acquittals or misdemeanor pleas.

In the lead-up to the midterms, Essayli mounted a failed effort to force California election officials to set aside thousands of ballots, alleging widespread voter fraud. Courts repeatedly blocked the effort, and state officials said he was spreading misinformation and risked disenfranchising voters.

Before he headed the US attorney's office in Los Angeles, Essayli was a Republican state lawmaker who railed against "woke" California policies and fought to undo trans rights.

The reported assignment thus joins two unresolved threads: the appeal of the ruling disqualifying Essayli from two prosecutions in Los Angeles, and a broader case in southern Florida that has stalled in an office that has lost its US attorney and other prosecutors.
