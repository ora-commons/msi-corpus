## Zach Lahn's campaign drew scrutiny over Kansas voting and Koch family ties

Zach Lahn, a political newcomer backed by Robert F Kennedy Jr’s “Make America healthy again” movement, won Iowa’s Republican primary over a candidate endorsed by Donald Trump, The Guardian reported. Iowa Democrats accused Lahn of spending a disproportionate amount of time in Kansas despite his campaign for Iowa governor.

The New York Times reported in October that although Lahn voted in Iowa in the 2024 general election, he had his ballot mailed to him in Kansas. The Guardian reported that Lahn also faced scrutiny for his complicated ties to the Koch family, whom it described as Republican billionaire mega-donors.

## Sand cited bipartisan government and one-party control

At a rally on Oct. 28, Sand said, “I think a big piece of that was because we elected both Democrats and Republicans, and they had to work together to get anything done.” He also said, “We have had 10 years of one-party control in the state of Iowa, and that is what led us to where we are today.”

Sand, 44, is a native Iowan who has cast himself as a unifier who would restore balance to Iowa politics. At campaign events, he led crowds in singing “America the Beautiful” and talked about the need to return to Iowa as a “common sense state.”

A photograph accompanying The Guardian’s report showed Sand speaking at a campaign rally on Oct. 31 in Ankeny, Iowa. The image was credited to Scott Olson/Getty Images.

## Projected Iowa result restores a Democratic governorship

The Associated Press projects that Democrat Rob Sand has been elected governor of Iowa, defeating Lahn. Before the election, Sand was the sole Democrat holding statewide office, and The Guardian reported that he had been leading in the polls ahead of election day. His projected victory makes him the first Democrat to win the governor’s office since 2006.

Iowa twice backed Barack Obama but has voted for Donald Trump all three times Trump was on the presidential ballot, while the GOP has come to dominate state politics. Sand’s projected victory was a milestone for Democrats seeking to rebuild their political standing in the Midwestern state.

The Guardian reported that Sand’s projected victory came as Democrats netted historic victories in gubernatorial elections nationwide, including in states where they had struggled in recent years. In Georgia, former Atlanta mayor Keisha Lance Bottoms has become the first Black woman elected governor in the country.
