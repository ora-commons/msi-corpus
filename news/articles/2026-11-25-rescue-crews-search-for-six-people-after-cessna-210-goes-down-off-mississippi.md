## Responders find three life jackets, a life raft and the cabin

Rescue crews searched the Gulf of Mexico on Tuesday afternoon for six people, including two children, after a small plane went down off the coast of Mississippi, authorities said.

The aircraft was a Cessna 210. It went down between Pascagoula, Mississippi, and Horn Island, which is part of the Gulf Islands National Seashore.

Coast Guard spokesperson Juan Ros said responders found three life jackets, a life raft and the plane's cabin floating on the water.

Rescue crews had not located the four adults and two boys aboard, Ros said. The boys were 8 and 10 years old.

FlightAware listed the Cessna as en route from New Orleans to Fort Meyers, Florida.

The Federal Aviation Administration and the National Transportation Safety Board said Tuesday that they were aware of the crash. The National Transportation Safety Board said it would investigate.

At the time of the report, the rescue search was still underway, and responders had not located any of the six people aboard.
