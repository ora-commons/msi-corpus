## Ultrasound showed the hat passing through Fiona’s small intestine

Fiona, a hippopotamus at the Cincinnati Zoo in Ohio, died after veterinary teams were unable to remove a knitted hat she swallowed, the zoo said. The zoo said Fiona initially showed no symptoms but later required emergency care as her appetite declined.

The Cincinnati Zoo said in a Thursday afternoon statement: "It is with extreme sadness that we share with you the devastating news that Fiona did not make it after multiple attempts from our amazing vets and keeper team to help her through what ended up being a medical emergency."

Fiona swallowed the hat about 10 days before her death. Zoo spokesperson Michelle Curley said the hat got into the enclosure by accident after a visitor tossed it into the air and it caught on a ceiling fan, which flung it through a small opening. Curley added, "We probably couldn't make [it] happen again if we tried."

At first, Fiona was asymptomatic. A few days later, her appetite began to decrease, and staff tried to encourage her to eat to help push the hat through her digestive system. An ultrasound before her death showed the hat passing through Fiona's small intestine.

A zookeeper said surgery was not an option for full-grown hippos and "our best chance for success with Fiona would be if she could pass this on her own." The zoo said its vet staff "tried everything they could to help her" but "despite multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system, it was ultimately too much to overcome."

Cincinnati Zoo director Dave Jenike said, "We are in shock at the moment. We did not expect this outcome. She fought until she couldn't anymore." He also said, "Our animal care team was heroic in their efforts to save her. They did everything possible, but her body couldn't fight anymore."

Fiona was born premature at the Cincinnati Zoo in 2017 and at first struggled to survive. At birth, she weighed 13 kg (29 lbs), about half the previous lowest recorded birth weight for her species, the zoo said.

Her story inspired an ice cream flavor and a character in Cincinnati's ballet.
