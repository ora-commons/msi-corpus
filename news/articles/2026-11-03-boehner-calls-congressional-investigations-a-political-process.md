## Public pressure could shape Trump's Russia policy

Risk Journal reporter Richard Vanderford said corporations were likely to face a wave of government investigations after the midterm elections if Democrats take control of Congress.

Polling suggested Democrats were likely to gain control of the House of Representatives and could gain control of the Senate. On Monday, prediction market Polymarket pegged the odds of both chambers flipping at about 74%. U.S. Senate seats were up for election Tuesday.

John Boehner, the House speaker from 2011 to 2015, characterized congressional investigations in political terms. “These investigations are not a legal proceeding…This is a political process. This is the Wild West.”

President Trump's moves to ease sanctions on Russia were likely to come under Congress's magnifying glass after the midterm elections, Risk Journal reported. Criticism of Trump's recent diesel deal with Russian President Vladimir Putin showed Congress already asserting itself and could hint at what might come next.

Legal and political experts said Congress would have difficulty forcing changes by passing sanctions legislation that Trump could veto. They said Congress could nevertheless have some success in shaping Trump's approach to Russia by marshaling public criticism and pressure placed on the private sector.
