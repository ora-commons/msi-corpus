## USS George Washington conducts routine operations after leaving the Middle East

The U.S. military has returned to having two aircraft carriers supporting Iran war operations after the USS George Washington departed the Middle East, the Associated Press reported Wednesday. The Japan-based ship is now under U.S. Pacific Fleet control, spokesperson Cmdr. Katie Koenig said, and is “conducting routine operations” in the Asia-Pacific region.

The George Washington departed in the same week the USS Theodore Roosevelt arrived, briefly putting three carriers in the waters off Iran. The move left the USS George H.W. Bush, which arrived in the region in April, and Roosevelt as the two carriers there, alongside more than a dozen destroyers, cruisers and a littoral combat ship.

During the Iran war, the carriers have served as the linchpin of a U.S. flotilla blockading all merchant shipping to and from Iran, according to the Associated Press. The report said the Trump administration has sought to squeeze Tehran's economy by preventing its oil from reaching global markets, and the carrier presence is helping maintain the blockade.

The United States has also carried out some limited strikes on Iranian targets in the Strait of Hormuz. The Associated Press described the strait as a critical waterway through which nearly a third of the world's oil passed before the war began Feb. 28. Disruptions to shipping through the strait have raised global energy prices.

The unpopularity of the war and its economic effects contributed to major losses for President Donald Trump's Republican Party in this month's midterm elections, according to the Associated Press. Trump has offered mixed signals about whether he plans to return to major combat with Iran, and any negotiated deal to end the fighting appears to be a long way off.

Trump recently warned Iran that the U.S. military was “locked and loaded” and said the United States was keeping an eye on Pickaxe Mountain, a facility linked to Iran's nuclear program, for signs of activity. He also said it was highly unlikely he would need to order new major strikes on Iran, declaring that the war “militarily is effectively over.”

The George Washington had arrived in the Middle East in August before its departure. It had been pulled from a patrol in the South China Sea shortly after reports of mental health and supply issues emerged aboard another long-deployed aircraft carrier in the Middle East, the USS Abraham Lincoln. The Lincoln set a record for uninterrupted time at sea, at more than 240 days.
