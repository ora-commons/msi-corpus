## Prosecutors alleged Gupta was directed by an Indian government official

A federal judge sentenced Indian national Nikhil Gupta to 235 months, more than 19 years, for his role in an attempted plot to kill Gurpatwant Singh Pannun, a leading Sikh political figure in the US.

Gupta pleaded guilty in February to murder-for-hire, conspiracy to commit murder-for-hire and conspiracy to commit money laundering. His guilty plea came before the judge imposed the 235-month term.

US prosecutors alleged that Gupta was directed by an Indian government official to kill Pannun. India has denied involvement in the alleged plot.

Pannun is a leading figure of the Khalistan movement, which advocates an independent homeland for Sikhs. India's government has accused Pannun of being a terrorist, which he denies.
