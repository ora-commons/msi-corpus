## Hosts examine Republican losses and Trump's response to the result

The Nov. 4 episode of The Guardian's Decoding America podcast centered on the scale of participation in the 2026 United States midterms. Hosts Reged Ahmad and Jonathan Yerushalmy examined the high turnout around a Democratic victory many had predicted and what went wrong for Republicans.

The Guardian characterized the Democratic result as a historic “resounding win” after the party took control of the Senate and House of Representatives. That congressional outcome supplied the setting for the podcast's full title, “US midterm elections 2026: is this the end of Trump?”

The Guardian's account paired the turnout with the anticipated outcome: voters turned out in droves to deliver a Democratic victory so many had predicted. Ahmad and Yerushalmy's examination focused on why voters turned out in droves.

The episode framed its Republican analysis around the direct question of what went wrong for Republicans. Democrats' control of both chambers supplied the setting for the hosts' examination of the party's losses.

The Guardian described Donald Trump's acceptance of the 2026 midterm result as “perhaps the biggest surprise of the night.” According to the podcast, Trump accepted the outcome and vowed to work with Democrats.
