## Nvidia sets one-day record for market capitalization added

Jensen Huang returned to Nvidia on Monday as chief executive after the chipmaker reinstated him 11 days after his ouster. Colette Kress, who had served as interim CEO, relinquished the title and returned to her role as chief financial officer, ending a brief period in which the finance chief ran the company.

Three other sitting CFOs at S&P 500 companies have followed the same path: Intel’s David Zinsner, Lululemon’s Meghan Frank and PayPal’s Jamie Miller have each served as interim chief at their current companies and then returned full time to the CFO role. Kress’s case differs because she will once again work alongside Huang instead of his successor.

According to Dow Jones Market Data, Nvidia added nearly the amount of Walmart’s market capitalization on Monday. The move set a new record for market capitalization added in one day.

Before the reinstatement, a major selloff erased more than $2 trillion from Nvidia’s market capitalization. President Trump had advocated for Huang’s return, and employees had pleaded for his comeback before Nvidia reversed course.

Huang received cheers from his own employees when he returned, while business leaders who had supported him responded with quips of satisfaction. At the same time, another group of American executives, board members and advisers was grappling with what precedent the episode set for corporate governance and leadership amid the scandal.

Former Baxter International CEO Harry Kraemer Jr., who teaches management at Northwestern University’s business school, said the episode quickly brought calls and text messages from former colleagues. He said he kept returning to one question: “What is the message that’s sent to every other CEO in the U.S.?”

After 11 days, Nvidia had Huang back as chief executive and Kress back as finance chief. The corporate-governance questions remained active as executives, board members and advisers continued to weigh the precedent set by the leadership reversal.


MSI previously reported that [Nvidia’s board reinstated Jensen Huang after rejecting his return offer](/articles/2026-11-23-nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-after-rejecting-his-return-offer/). Huang has now returned to the company, Kress is returning to finance work, and the questions about CEO accountability raised by the leadership reversal remain unresolved.
