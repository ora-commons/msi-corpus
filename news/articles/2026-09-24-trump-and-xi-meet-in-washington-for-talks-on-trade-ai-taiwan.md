US District Judge Tim Kelly issued a 14-day temporary restraining order restoring White House press passes to CNN, Politico, and MS Now, three outlets Trump had banned on September 18 over their coverage of his administration.

Kelly issued his order in a lawsuit the three news organizations filed contesting the ban, finding it "likely unconstitutional" and ordering the Trump administration to immediately return the outlets' press passes.
