## Reported cap left some Ukrainian drones virtually useless

The Wall Street Journal reported that SpaceX founder Musk capped the speeds at which Ukrainian hardware could remain connected to Starlink last week. Citing people familiar with the matter, the newspaper attributed the order to Musk.

People familiar with the matter said Musk's speed restriction rendered some offensive drones and interceptor drones virtually useless. The restriction applied to Ukrainian hardware connected to the satellite-based network.

Two of the people said Musk and President Trump discussed the decision in a call soon after the slowdown became public.

Musk's public response came hours after the restriction became public. He tweeted, “any limits are decided by the U.S. military and the president.”

SpaceX did not respond to The Wall Street Journal's request for comment. People familiar with the matter attributed the cap to Musk, while Musk's public statement said the U.S. military and the president decided any limits.

The Wall Street Journal also reported that Musk and Trump had expressed anger at Ukraine for attacking Russian diesel refineries in recent weeks. Trump blamed the Ukrainian attacks for high energy prices.
