## Unannounced Navy removals draw pushback from Congress

The Senate Armed Services Committee is holding a confirmation hearing on Hung Cao's nomination to become the permanent Navy secretary. The hearing is scheduled for 9:30 a.m. EST on Tuesday, November 17, 2026.

Cao is serving as acting Navy secretary and is seeking the position permanently. The committee's consideration of his nomination comes as the Navy faces congressional criticism over its handling of several senior personnel changes.

The Navy has quietly fired or removed several top officers in recent months. The service broke with its longstanding practice of announcing major removals of commanders and other highly visible officials, and the changes drew pushback from Congress.

Two Democratic lawmakers said last week that the Navy failed to inform Congress that it had fired the entire leadership team of a nuclear-powered attack submarine last month after an underwater collision.

Separately, the Navy fired the commander of a destroyer deployed to the Middle East in September, according to a defense official who spoke on condition of anonymity to describe sensitive personnel matters.

Dr. Brian Monahan, a Navy rear admiral and attending physician for Congress and the Supreme Court, also departed his post during a Navy investigation. His departure came with no public announcement or confirmation.

The submarine leadership dismissal, the destroyer's commander's firing and Monahan's departure each departed from the Navy's policy of announcing major removals of commanders and other highly visible officials. They also followed increasing secrecy over military operations, especially in the Middle East.

For Cao, the hearing is the formal step toward becoming the Navy's permanent top civilian official. The personnel cases form the congressional backdrop: two Democratic lawmakers have said the Navy failed to inform Congress about the submarine's leadership team, while the service's broader departure from its announcement practice drew pushback from Congress.
