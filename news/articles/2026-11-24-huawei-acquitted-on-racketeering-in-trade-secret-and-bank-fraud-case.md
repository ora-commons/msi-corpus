## Defense said prosecutors began by deciding Huawei was a criminal organization

Huawei was found guilty of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud in a U.S. trial, BBC News reported, citing AP. The Chinese technology and telecommunications giant was also acquitted of the overarching racketeering charge on November 24, 2026.

The two-month trial produced the split verdict in the United States. BBC News described the proceeding as a landmark trial and the racketeering allegation as the more serious overarching charge.

The guilty findings covered conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud. Prosecutors accused Huawei of lying, cheating and stealing to gain any competitive advantage.

Huawei's defense argued that prosecutors had decided the company was a criminal organization and built the case from that premise.

The defense also said Huawei's success was due to its own innovation.

In a statement, Huawei said the racketeering acquittal "confirms that these allegations lack factual basis and vindicates our consistent position over the past eight years".

The result left Huawei with convictions for conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud and an acquittal on racketeering, separating the specific charges from the broader allegation.
