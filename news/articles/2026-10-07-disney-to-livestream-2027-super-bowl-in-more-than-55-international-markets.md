## Disney+ to add two Monday Night Football games ahead of Super Bowl

Disney said its streaming platform, Disney+, will livestream the 2027 Super Bowl. All U.S. subscribers will be able to stream the traditional game presentation airing on ESPN and ABC, the company said, as well as the Spanish-language telecast on ESPN Deportes.

Outside the United States, Disney+ will stream the Super Bowl in more than 55 international markets, Disney said.

The 2026 Super Bowl was broadcast on NBC, Telemundo and Peacock, a different combination of broadcast and streaming outlets than the arrangement Disney announced for 2027.

Disney also said Disney+ will stream two Monday Night Football games during the regular season in the lead-up to the final game. The first regular-season game on Disney+ will be the Dallas Cowboys versus the Philadelphia Eagles on Oct. 26.

Both regular-season games will continue to air as scheduled on ESPN and ABC, as well as on ESPN Deportes, Disney said.
