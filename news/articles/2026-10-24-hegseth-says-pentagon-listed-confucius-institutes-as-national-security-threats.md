## Hegseth alleges "80 new propaganda cells" appeared across 30 universities

In a video posted on social media Friday, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth alleged: “It turns out that 80 new propaganda cells across 30 universities had popped up using different names and different charters, but with the same mission: to steal intellectual property and to infiltrate the minds of American students.”

Hegseth alleged that Confucius Institutes were operating under disguise at U.S. universities. He said the U.S. Defense Department had designated Chinese-funded cultural centers on university campuses, known as Confucius Institutes, as a threat to U.S. national security and had added them to its list of institutions that threaten the country.

Hegseth said the Defense Department had "systematically dismantled every single one of these organizations" over the previous two months.

The Guardian reported that Washington has long accused Confucius Institutes of being "Chinese propaganda tools" and has taken steps against them.

The Chinese embassy in Washington did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
