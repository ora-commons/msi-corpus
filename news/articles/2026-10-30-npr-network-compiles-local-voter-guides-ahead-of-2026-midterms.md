## Guides span governor races, abortion and LGBTQ+ rights measures

The NPR Network published a state-by-state directory of voter guides and local election resources on Oct. 30, with the 2026 midterms days away. The compilation directs readers to guides from NPR member stations across the states the directory lists, with resources covering races from statewide contests down to local offices.

Beyond key Senate and House races, the network said it is following governor races and ballot measures on abortion, LGBTQ+ rights, billionaire taxes and more. NPR has published a look at the 36 races for governor this year and a list of the House races to watch in the Nov. 3 election.

NPR said its member stations are gathering information for voters — from voter guides down to local races — offering guides, resources and news reporting from communities across the country to help voters be informed for the midterm election. The directory lists resources state by state. Beyond statewide guides, the directory includes local reporting from individual communities.
