## Merged utility serves 2.2 million customers across eight states

South Dakota-based utility companies Black Hills Corp. and NorthWestern Energy have completed their merger, forming Bright Horizon Energy, the company announced Monday. The combined utility serves 2.2 million electric or natural gas customers across eight states, and filings with the South Dakota Public Utilities Commission list the new company's value at $15.4 billion.

The company said customers will see no immediate changes to their billing or online account access.

"Today marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter as Bright Horizon Energy," said Brian Bird, the new CEO who formerly led NorthWestern. Black Hills CEO Linden Evans is to retire.

The merged company has about 4,500 employees, about 39,000 miles of electric lines and about 60,000 miles of natural gas lines. It is headquartered in Rapid City; NorthWestern was headquartered in Sioux Falls.

Bright Horizon Energy will serve 195,705 customers in South Dakota, and 2 million more in Montana, Wyoming, Colorado, Nebraska, Kansas, Iowa and Arkansas.

Two state regulators had cleared the transaction before the merger closed. The South Dakota Public Utilities Commission approved the merger on June 24. Montana was the final state to do so, with regulators there signing off on Nov. 10.
