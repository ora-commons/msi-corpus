## Trump appeared to close his eyes for roughly 10 seconds

The White House denied allegations that President Donald Trump fell asleep during a televised speech by Chinese President Xi Jinping at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in China. Trump appeared to close his eyes for roughly 10 seconds; his head tilted downward, and his hands remained clasped in front of him. On the same day, Trump and Vladimir Putin took part in a trilateral meeting with Xi in Shenzhen, China.

Several Trump critics posted allegations on social media that the president had fallen asleep. Those making the allegations included Republicans Against Trump, the press office of California governor Gavin Newsom and conservative lawyer George Conway.

White House officials said Trump was looking down at papers in front of him. White House director of communications Steven Cheung wrote on X in response to a Newsweek article: “Fake News! President Trump was looking at papers in front of him while the speaker made remarks. You can clearly see his eyes move back and forth.” Cheung added: “This is why the people despise media so much. They constantly lie.”

Trump often faces similar accusations at Oval Office press conferences, cabinet meetings and sporting events. A photograph published with The Guardian’s report shows Trump with his eyes closed during an NBA finals game between the Spurs and the Knicks at Madison Square Garden in New York on 8 June 2026, in a photograph credited to Saul Loeb/AFP/Getty Images. He sometimes closes his eyes for several seconds while listening, appearing as though he is falling asleep, and footage of those instances has gone viral. The White House and Trump’s allies have consistently denied that he falls asleep during meetings.

Administration officials consistently attribute Trump’s apparent drowsiness to blinking or to an example of Trump listening intently, and they consistently say Trump “doesn’t sleep”.

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio testified to lawmakers earlier this year that he had “never seen” Trump fall asleep. In June, he said: “On the contrary, the guy doesn’t sleep, which is a big problem because he calls me at two in the morning.”

A photograph published with The Guardian’s report shows Trump apparently asleep next to Pete Hegseth at the White House on 2 December 2025, in a photograph credited to Carolyn Van Houten/The Washington Post/Getty Images. JD Vance said last year that Trump hardly ever sleeps, even while traveling overnight. Vance told Fox News: “If we go on a 20-hour trip somewhere, he doesn’t sleep the entire time. And of course, if he’s not sleeping, if he’s working, he expects everybody else to be working too.”

Trump, 80, became the oldest U.S. president ever inaugurated when he was sworn in nearly two years ago. Despite his age, Trump often claims he is in near-perfect health, has cited “perfect” cognitive test results and says he has a lot of energy.
