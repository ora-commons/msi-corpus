## Blyth said Albanese’s government was “running the country into the ground”

The Guardian reported, citing The Australian, that federal Liberal senator Leah Blyth for South Australia is defecting to One Nation. Under the reported move, Blyth would become the party's first upper-house member for the state.

Blyth is a member of Angus Taylor's shadow cabinet. The Guardian said the reported departure would cause recriminations in Coalition ranks.

Blyth told The Australian that she no longer felt the Coalition was capable of opposing the Albanese government. She described the government as “running the country into the ground.”

Blyth said, “I can no longer in good conscience tell Australians that the Liberal party can save this country.”

Blyth also said: “Australia is a country worth fighting for … I want our children to grow up proud of their country, with the freedom and opportunity to build a better life.”

In defending her choice of One Nation, Blyth said: “One Nation has never shied away from confronting the uncomfortable truths facing our nation.”

She added: “They are the only party with the bold ideas needed to turn this country around.”

The reported switch would give One Nation its first upper-house member for South Australia and remove a senator from Angus Taylor's shadow cabinet. The Guardian carried the account in its Australian politics live coverage and attributed the report to The Australian.
