## Certain stockholders sold shares that brought the total haul above $81.3 billion

Anthropic said on Wednesday that it sold more than 956 million shares at $83 each in a record-breaking initial public offering. The company raised around $79.4 billion through the sale.

The $79.4 billion raise exceeded the $75 billion IPO haul of Elon Musk's SpaceX earlier this year, according to NPR.

Anthropic said in a statement that "certain stockholders" also sold more than 23 million shares as part of the IPO. Including those sales, the total haul reached more than $81.3 billion.

Anthropic's shares will start trading publicly Thursday on the Nasdaq under ticker symbol ANT.

The listing was oversubscribed despite coming at a time of growing concern about a possible AI bubble and volatility in tech stocks.

The number of shares sold and the price exceeded Anthropic's original expectations. The listing prospectus targeted a sale of 825 million shares at $70 to $78 per share.

The IPO made the five-year-old artificial intelligence company one of the most valuable companies in the world, according to NPR.

NPR disclosed that Anthropic is a financial supporter of NPR.
