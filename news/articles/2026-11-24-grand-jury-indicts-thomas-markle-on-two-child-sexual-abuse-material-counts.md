## US prosecutors say charges involve children under 12

A federal grand jury in Los Angeles indicted Thomas Markle on Tuesday on two counts of possessing child sexual abuse material, U.S. prosecutors said. The 82-year-old father of Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, was arrested by FBI agents at a Florida senior care facility earlier this month and had already appeared in federal court in Tampa in connection with possession of child sexual abuse material in California.

The charges accuse Markle of possessing child sexual abuse material in the Philippines, where he previously lived, and keeping illegal images and other material in storage facilities in Los Angeles. U.S. prosecutors said the material involved children under the age of 12.

Tuesday’s indictment doubles the number of charges Markle faces in the case. That case also accuses him of posing in photographs with at least one naked underage girl and with other children in various stages of undress.

FBI agents arrested Markle at the senior care facility in Florida where he was living. He then made a brief appearance in federal court in Tampa, where the court initially released him.

Federal authorities said Markle cut off an ankle monitor and later jailed him.

Markle is estranged from his daughter, Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, and from Meghan’s husband, Prince Harry.

A Douglas A Land photograph, credited through AP, shows Markle during a federal court hearing in Tampa, Florida, on 19 November. The Associated Press and Reuters contributed reporting to The Guardian’s account.


The indictment advances the federal case after [Markle’s arrest and jailing following the ankle-monitor incident](/articles/2026-11-10-thomas-markle-cuts-off-ankle-monitor-after-arrest/) and [a ruling that he was competent to face the charges](/articles/2026-11-19-judge-finds-thomas-markle-competent-to-face-child-sexual-abuse-material-charges/).
