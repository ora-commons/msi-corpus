## Alí will conduct four science projects in biotechnology and materials

China presented the three-person Shenzhou 24 crew in Jiuquan on Tuesday before the spacecraft's planned launch this week. The China Manned Space Agency, in an announcement carried by Xinhua, named Muhammad Zeeshan Alí, 40, Tang Hongbo, 51, and Wu Yi, 39. The launch is scheduled for Wednesday morning from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwestern China.

Alí's assignment would make him the first foreign astronaut to board the Tiangong space station.

The Shenzhou 24 flight is set to be the first joint mission between Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of Beijing's crewed-spaceflight program. It forms part of an agreement signed by China and Pakistan in February 2025.

At a press conference, Alí thanked the Chinese government for helping him realize his dream. Speaking Mandarin, he said he liked a Chinese verse about climbing another floor to see farther and compared the preparation with his daily training and reaching new heights. He called the mission a long path rather than a finish line and said he hoped cooperation would expand from one astronaut on one flight to multiple joint flights and work extending to the Moon, Mars and beyond.

Alí also asked children watching the launch to look at the sky and remember that he was up there with their dreams.

CCTV reported that Alí would serve as a payload specialist for a short-duration mission. During the orbital phase, his principal duties would be experiments for China-Pakistan cooperation and daily operations. The Pakistani astronaut would return to Earth aboard Shenzhou 23 after the mission, CCTV said.

CCTV said the scientific work would include four projects in biotechnology and materials. Alí was also scheduled to conduct an orbital experiment involving photosynthetic microorganisms.

Tang had flown on two previous space missions and was set to become the first astronaut to board Tiangong on three occasions. Alí and Wu were preparing for their first spaceflights.

Tang said learning to work together was more challenging on this mission because of linguistic, cultural and psychological differences. He said the efforts of both sides had produced results.

Tang explained that labels and operating instructions aboard the spacecraft and the space station were all in Chinese. Alí had begun training with virtually no foundation in the language, Tang said, while Tang and Wu intensified their English study.

China is preparing its first crewed Moon landing for 2030. The United States is considered China's principal space rival, and NASA aims to send astronauts to the lunar surface in 2028.
