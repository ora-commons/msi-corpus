## Journal described AI trade as battered last week amid Anthropic's bumpy post-IPO ride

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent appeared to ease his stance on Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia, saying Sunday, “Jensen is on the outside now.” The Wall Street Journal reported that Bessent had urged Nvidia's board to hire an outsider. The newspaper also described the artificial-intelligence trade as battered last week amid Anthropic's bumpy post-IPO ride.

Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as chief executive. The company said the board had “received input from senior levels of the United States government.” Based on that input, the board said it was “confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.”

The Wall Street Journal said Huang's scandal-driven resignation had reframed the outlook for AI and that every analysis needed to be reconsidered. The paper noted that the U.S. week was holiday-shortened as it turned to what Huang's return meant for the trade.

The announcement came 11 days after Huang resigned following revelations that he had at least two extramarital affairs, The Wall Street Journal reported. One involved a woman U.S. officials believed was working with Chinese intelligence, and another involved a senior direct report.

Board chair Harvey C. Jones said the board believed Huang's return was “in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers.” The chair's statement placed employees, partners and customers alongside stockholders in the board's justification.

President Trump, who had a close relationship with Huang and had pushed for his return since his ouster, praised the board's decision Monday, The Wall Street Journal reported.

Nvidia shares surged nearly 30% Monday morning, while the Nasdaq was up 6.6%, according to The Wall Street Journal. The [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NASDAQCOM) Nasdaq Composite series recorded a raw index level of 23,879.2 for Nov. 23, 2026, providing a broad-market benchmark for the session.

The Wall Street Journal listed Hewlett Packard Enterprise, Zoom Communications, Dell Technologies, HP Inc., Autodesk and Workday among the enterprise tech providers on deck to report earnings this week.
