## Encanto operates 180 restaurants in Puerto Rico, including Pizza Hut and IHOP

Officials said Monday that every KFC and Taco Bell restaurant in Puerto Rico had closed temporarily after Yum! Brands, the Kentucky-based parent company, ended its relationship with Encanto Restaurants, an operator based on the island. Yum! Brands said Encanto could no longer operate in a manner that met the company's standards, and KFC Puerto Rico and Taco Bell Puerto Rico said they were working to find a new partner.

Yum! Brands said it had made the "difícil decisión de poner fin a su relación," the difficult decision to end its relationship with Encanto Restaurants. The company said it took that step because Encanto "ya no puede operar de una manera que cumpla con nuestros estándares," meaning the operator could no longer operate in a manner that met Yum! Brands' standards.

In a statement shared with The Associated Press, Yum! Brands said, "Seguimos profundamente comprometidos con Puerto Rico y estamos trabajando lo más rápido posible para volver a recibir a nuestros clientes en nuestros restaurantes." The company said it remained deeply committed to Puerto Rico and was working as quickly as possible to receive customers again at its restaurants.

KFC Puerto Rico and Taco Bell Puerto Rico indicated that they are working to find a new franchise partner.

KFC Puerto Rico wrote on Instagram, "Esperamos poder volver a servirles muy pronto," indicating the brand hoped to be able to serve customers again very soon.

Encanto Restaurants did not respond to requests for comment.

According to Encanto Restaurants, the first KFC restaurant in Puerto Rico opened in 1967, and the company began operating Taco Bell in 1985.

Encanto Restaurants also operates Pizza Hut and IHOP restaurants in the U.S. territory of Puerto Rico. In total, the company operates 180 restaurants on the island and employs more than three thousand people.
