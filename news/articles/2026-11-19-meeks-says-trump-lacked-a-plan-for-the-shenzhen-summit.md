## Meeks feared spheres of influence could sideline U.S. allies

"I wish he had a plan and a strategy if he was going to meet with them and goals. Apparently, he does not," Meeks said Thursday, speaking on NPR's Morning Edition in an interview conducted by A Martínez.

## Meeks questioned alliances and summit agreements

Meeks questioned what President Trump accomplished at his summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin and warned that the meeting could undermine U.S. alliances. NPR reported that the meeting in Shenzhen, China, ended with few signs of progress on nuclear arms control or the war in Ukraine.

Meeks said he feared the three leaders were discussing a division of the world into spheres of influence. He said the approach could sideline U.S. allies. Meeks also questioned the lack of meaningful agreements on nuclear weapons and artificial intelligence safety.

## Meeks called for clear goals on Chinese espionage

Meeks argued that U.S. diplomats should have raised concerns about Chinese espionage and intellectual property theft before the summit to establish clear goals for the talks. His criticism was directed at both the summit's preparation and its limited results.

## Meeks called for lower tariffs and pressure on Russia

In a "Consider This from NPR" segment, "Where did U.S. support for Ukraine go," Meeks asked where U.S. support for Ukraine had gone. In his conversation with A Martínez, he called for reducing tariffs on U.S. allies while pressing China to end its support for Russia's war in Ukraine.

## Meeks outlined a response to the Nvidia scandal

Asked about the Nvidia scandal, Meeks said he would confront Xi over Chinese espionage and intellectual property theft and warn of possible retaliation. "When it comes to our national security, that's where we draw a red line," he said.
