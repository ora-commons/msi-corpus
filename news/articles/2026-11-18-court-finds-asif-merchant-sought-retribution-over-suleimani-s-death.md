## DOJ said Trump was target; Merchant testified no one was ordered killed

A Brooklyn court found that Asif Merchant, who had alleged ties to Iran, sought to hire a hitman to assassinate a politician or government official in the United States in retribution for the U.S. killing of Revolutionary Guards commander Qassem Suleimani. The Department of Justice said the alleged plot targeted Donald Trump in retaliation for that killing, while Attorney General Todd Blanche said Merchant came to the United States to coordinate an assassination attempt against President Trump.

Suleimani headed Iran’s foreign military operations and was killed in a U.S. drone strike in Baghdad in January 2020. Iranian officials have repeatedly vowed to avenge his killing.

After the sentencing, Blanche said the plot was against Trump. “An Iranian agent who came to the United States to coordinate an assassination attempt against President Trump has been justly sentenced for his criminal conduct,” Blanche said. “The Department of Justice will not tolerate foreign terrorist plots against our leaders and will not stop until those responsible are held accountable.”

Merchant, a Pakistani man, was sentenced Tuesday to 330 months in prison — more than 27 years — after the court finding.

U.S. officials previously said Merchant had “close ties to Iran” and described the alleged plot as “straight out of the Iranian regime’s playbook.”

Merchant testified that he was never ordered to kill a specific person and said his Iranian contact had mentioned three people in connection with the plot: Donald Trump, former U.S. president Joe Biden and former UN ambassador Nikki Haley.

According to multiple media outlets reporting on his testimony, Merchant said he was forced into the plot to protect his family in the Iranian capital Tehran from the Guards. He added that he thought he would get caught before anyone was killed.

Merchant was arrested on July 12, 2024, as he planned to leave the country.

The Brooklyn court’s findings referred to a politician or government official in the United States, while Blanche said the plot targeted Trump and Merchant testified that no specific person had been ordered killed.
