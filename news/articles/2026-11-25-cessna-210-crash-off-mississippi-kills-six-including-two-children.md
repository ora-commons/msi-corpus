## Cessna 210 was en route to Fort Myers when contact was lost

The US Coast Guard said six people, including two children, were found dead after a Cessna 210 crashed off the coast of Mississippi on Tuesday. The aircraft went down in the Gulf of Mexico.

According to FlightAware, the Cessna 210 was on its way from New Orleans to Fort Myers, Florida.

Air traffic control lost touch with the aircraft at about 15:45 (21:45 GMT), officials said.

The aircraft later went down in the Gulf of Mexico between Pascagoula and Horn Island, officials said.

US Coast Guard rescuers spent hours searching for the group aboard.

Early in the operation, rescuers initially found only three life jackets, a life raft and the plane's cabin floating on the water.

Coast Guard spokesperson Juan Ros said divers found the bodies of four adults and two boys in the fuselage. The boys were aged 8 and 10.

Cmdr Trevor Blount, the Coast Guard rescue mission co-ordinator, said: "While this is not the outcome any of us had hoped for, we are grateful for the strong inter-agency co-ordination that enabled us to swiftly locate those aboard the aircraft and provide closure to their families."
