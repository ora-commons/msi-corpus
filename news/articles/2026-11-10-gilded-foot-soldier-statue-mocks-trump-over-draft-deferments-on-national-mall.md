## White House spokesperson says artists suffer 'Trump Derangement Syndrome'

A gilded statue mocking Donald Trump's Vietnam war draft deferments was installed on the National Mall in Washington on Nov. 9, two days before Veterans Day. Titled The Foot Soldier, the work was erected by the Secret Handshake, a group of anonymous artists, near the National Gallery of Art's Sculpture Garden. Photographs published by The Guardian were credited to Mehmet Eser/Anadolu/Getty Images.

The statue depicts Trump sitting down while rubbing his bare foot. A plaque on the work reads: "We honor the service and sacrifice of our commander in chief, Donald J Trump. He's a soldier in spirit who marched to the front lines of dodging the draft, armed with a perfect diagnosis of bone spurs."

The plaque also reads: "We salute a leader who knows you can't spell sacrifice without 'I,' and a warrior who reminds us that sometimes when you're called to stand up, it's best to just sit down and rest your feet."

The art refers to allegations that Trump dodged the Vietnam war draft in 1968 by receiving a diagnosis of bone spurs in his heels after he graduated from college. Trump has faced draft-dodging allegations since his first term.

In 2018, the New York Times published an article accusing a foot doctor in Queens of doing "a favor" for Trump's father by diagnosing Trump with bone spurs. In 2019, Michael Cohen, a former lawyer for Trump, told Congress that the president deliberately avoided the draft.

Next to the installation stood Toey, a 6ft-tall mascot of a toe suffering from bone spurs, which the Secret Handshake said in a statement served as an interactive element. A person dressed as Toey told the Washington Post: "Draft dodger? I hardly know her." The person added: "I'm the biggest toe. The hugest toe. Huge. Hugest toe. Largest toe that anyone's ever seen."

David Ingle, a spokesperson for the White House, said in a statement that "Trump Derangement Syndrome is a sick and incurable disease that has rotted the brains of these Democrat activists. President Trump will never waver in fighting for and protecting the American people."

The Secret Handshake has also erected statues making fun of Trump's relationship with Jeffrey Epstein and Trump's remodeling of the Lincoln bathroom during a government shutdown. Last year, the anonymous group of artists created Best Friends Forever, a statue that showed Trump holding hands with Epstein. In March, the Secret Handshake installed a statue called King of the World, which depicted Trump holding Epstein in a Titanic-inspired pose.

The Secret Handshake said The Foot Soldier and Toey will be at the National Mall through Veterans Day.
