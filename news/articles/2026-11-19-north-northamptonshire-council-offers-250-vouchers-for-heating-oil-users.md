## Heating oil remains outside Ofgem’s energy price cap

North Northamptonshire Council has offered more financial support to households that rely on heating oil as their main energy source. The Reform UK-led council said residents can apply for a £250 voucher if they meet its emergency criteria.

Residents can apply online when low oil levels would put health, safety or basic living conditions at risk. The council encouraged residents to check whether they were eligible.

The council said rural areas, including parts of North Northamptonshire, are among the most affected by rising heating costs.

Heating oil is not protected by Ofgem's energy price cap. That means households relying on the fuel feel price increases faster than those using mains gas or electricity.

Heating oil prices have been volatile since the conflict between the US and Iran began in March.

The government announced a £53m support package in response to rising costs.

Greg Wilcox, North Northamptonshire Council's executive member for communities and rural affairs, said: "We know that times are hard for many households and we want to do all we can to support as many residents as possible."
