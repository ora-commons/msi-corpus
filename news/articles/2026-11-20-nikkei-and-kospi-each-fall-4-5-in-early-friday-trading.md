## Nikkei and Kospi each fall 4.5% in early Friday trading

## S&P/ASX 200, Hang Seng and Shanghai Composite post early losses

Asian shares dropped in early Friday trading as a broad selloff hit issues related to artificial intelligence and computer chips, following declines on Wall Street. Japan's Nikkei 225 shed 4.5% to 56,775.04, while South Korea's Kospi also shed 4.5% to 4,905.20.

Australia's S&P/ASX 200 dropped 1.6% to 8,594.10. Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell nearly 2.0% to 22,393.93, while the Shanghai Composite lost 1.7% to 3,515.87.

AI-related shares among those declining included Advantest Corp. and SoftBank Group Corp. in Tokyo, Samsung Electronics Co. in South Korea and Tencent in China.

The Associated Press reported that global markets had been weighed for some time by concerns about the sustainability of the AI boom, despite Anthropic's 17.1% first-day gain on Wall Street.

## Wall Street indexes fall as Nvidia reverses course

On Wall Street, the S&P 500 fell 2% for a sixth consecutive loss. The Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1,101 points, or 2.2%, while the Nasdaq composite sank 2.3%. The S&P 500 finished at 7,175.83, down 147.80 points; the Dow closed at 49,097.06, down 1,101.11 points; and the Nasdaq composite ended at 23,879.20, down 550.69 points.

Nvidia erased an early gain of 4.7% and reversed to a 3.3% loss. The Associated Press said Huang's sudden resignation as Nvidia's chief executive contributed to the decline in Nvidia shares. Huang helped build interest in the AI industry and used some of Nvidia's profits to fund the company's further growth.

## Anthropic closes 17.1% higher after trading above $115

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI chatbot, sold shares of its stock at $83 apiece in its initial public offering. Its stock began trading on the Nasdaq shortly before noon and quickly surged above $115. The S&P 500 jumped as much as 1.3% after the offering. Anthropic later pared its gain and finished Thursday at $97.17, up 17.1%.

The Associated Press said Anthropic's IPO appeared to show that investor interest in AI remained. The Associated Press also said the AI industry needed a boost after sharp recent declines in AI-related stocks on concerns that the stocks' prices had risen too high.

## Treasury yield falls as oil and currency markets move

The yield on the 10-year Treasury fell to 5.04% from 5.07% Wednesday.

Benchmark U.S. crude rose 0.38% to $83.53 a barrel. Brent crude, the international standard, edged up 0.40% to $92.35 a barrel.

In currency trading, the U.S. dollar slipped to 159.22 Japanese yen from 159.60 yen. The euro cost $1.0970, down from $1.0973.
