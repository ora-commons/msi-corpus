## Analysts expect early volatility because Anthropic has no direct public peers

Anthropic's shares fell to near $88 on Friday, retreating toward the $83 price of its initial public offering after the artificial intelligence company closed Thursday 17% higher at $97.17.

The move came a day after shares began at $111.50 and peaked at $115. BBC News described Thursday's debut as a crucial test for the industry, and the debut was largely seen as a success. Anthropic, the maker of the Claude chatbot, also raised a record $81bn in the IPO.

Friday's decline did not occur alone. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite Index opened down 1.3%, the S&P 500 opened down 0.4%, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average opened slightly higher. BBC News said other technology stocks also suffered as their declines continued into Friday.

Analysts said volatility was expected in the first days or even weeks of Anthropic's trading because the company was the first frontier AI firm to go public and had no direct peers.

BBC News reported that some observers had noted similarities with Elon Musk's SpaceX, which made its debut in June as the largest IPO on record at the time. SpaceX shares experienced large swings during their first few weeks of trading.

"There's going to be some volatility like we had with SpaceX," Rob Hackel, chief executive of financial advisory and brokerage firm RF Lafferty & Co, told Reuters.

Hackel also said: "A lot of people are going to have FOMO [Fear Of Missing Out]. I don't think there's any need to get on it on the first trade."

Friday's fall left Anthropic trading above its $83 offering price but below Thursday's $115 peak. The $81bn offering had raised a record sum, and Friday added another price decline to the company's first two sessions.
