## First Deputy Mayor Dean Fuleihan oversaw city operations Friday

New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani underwent surgery Friday to repair a right Achilles tendon he tore last week during a soccer match against local journalists, his press secretary said. Joe Calvello said the roughly 70-minute procedure went well and that Mamdani, 35, was resting comfortably Friday evening at Gracie Mansion, the mayoral residence.

“The mayor is expected to make a full recovery,” Calvello said in a statement. City Hall thanked the healthcare workers involved in the operation but did not respond to a question about where the surgery was done.

The injury occurred on Nov. 19, when Mamdani, a Democrat and an avid soccer fan, took part in a friendly match between his office and the City Hall press corps. His team won, with the mayor notching a goal.

Mamdani explained the next day that soon after scoring, he ruptured the tendon. He said ruefully that he hadn't stretched before playing.

Since the injury, Mamdani has been wearing a medical boot and using crutches while going about his work, including public appearances.

Speaking Monday about the surgery, the mayor said he was apprehensive but quipped: “I just keep telling myself: Jayson Tatum did it.”

That reference was to Jayson Tatum, a Boston Celtics star who ruptured an Achilles tendon in a 2025 NBA playoff game against the New York Knicks. Tatum had surgery and returned to competitive play about 10 months later.

First Deputy Mayor Dean Fuleihan oversaw day-to-day city operations Friday.

The details released about the operation were limited. The mayoral statement said the procedure went well and that the mayor was expected to make a full recovery. City Hall did not say where the surgery was performed.
