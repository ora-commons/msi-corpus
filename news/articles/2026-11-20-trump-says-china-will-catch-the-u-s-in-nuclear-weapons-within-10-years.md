## Pentagon says China is on track for more than 1,000 nuclear warheads by 2030

At a White House news conference Friday, Trump said of China’s nuclear buildup, “They’ll catch us within 10 years,” before adding, “They’re going at it hot and heavy, but they have a lot.”

Trump’s Friday remarks were his first public remarks since the trilateral summit, which took place Thursday in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen and brought Trump together with Xi and Putin.

After Thursday’s meeting, the three leaders issued a joint statement saying they had “affirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.” The statement said the leaders “will conduct a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing the proliferation of nuclear weapons.”

The Pentagon’s annual review of China’s military buildup said China’s stockpile of nuclear warheads remained in the low 600s through 2024. The review also said China was on track to have more than 1,000 warheads by 2030.

China is rapidly expanding its nuclear arsenal, while Russia and the United States each possess thousands of nuclear weapons and lead the world by that measure.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio, who spoke to reporters alongside Trump on Friday, said it was imperative for the three nuclear powers to talk. “These three countries possess the overwhelming majority of nuclear weapons on this planet,” Rubio said. “The idea that these three leaders should not engage on a regular basis is not just absurd, it’s dangerous.”

Before the meeting, observers had doubted whether China would agree to nuclear talks. With a much smaller nuclear arsenal, Beijing had resisted joining nuclear arms control talks with the United States and Russia.
