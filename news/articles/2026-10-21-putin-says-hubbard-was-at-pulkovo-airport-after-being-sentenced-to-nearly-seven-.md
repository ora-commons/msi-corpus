The disclosure came as Putin and White House envoy Steve Witkoff held talks focused on U.S. efforts to end the war in Ukraine.

NPR described Saint Petersburg as Russia's former imperial capital. As the talks with Witkoff got underway there, Putin announced that Hubbard had been released and was already at Pulkovo Airport in Saint Petersburg.

Stephen Hubbard was 74 and a resident of Michigan. NPR reported that Russian forces detained Hubbard in eastern Ukraine in 2022.

A closed trial in 2024 imposed a nearly seven-year prison sentence on Hubbard for serving as a mercenary in the Ukrainian army.

Putin said the release followed Trump's request. The U.S. had formally designated Hubbard wrongfully detained under the Biden administration.
