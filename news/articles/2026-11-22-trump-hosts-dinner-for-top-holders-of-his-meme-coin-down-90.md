## Trump hosts dinner for top holders of his meme coin, down 90%

**Subtype:** fact

- President Trump will host an exclusive dinner Sunday for the top owners of his $TRUMP meme coin, which has fallen about 90% in value since May 2025.
- Trump made $635 million in meme-coin royalties last year, part of a $1.4 billion windfall in crypto income in 2025.
- The dinner is struggling to fill 185 seats at Trump's golf club in Sterling, Va., after would-be guests competed for 220 open seats at a May 2025 gathering.

## Trump collected $635 million in royalties as the coin slid

President Trump will reward top owners of his declining meme coin with an exclusive dinner Sunday near Washington, featuring himself and a number of celebrities, including Kevin O'Leary, the entrepreneur best known for his role on "Shark Tank."

A similar dinner in May 2025 was sought after by crypto moguls and others seeking an audience with Trump, with would-be guests competing for 220 open seats. But since that gathering, the coin's value has plummeted by around 90%, and the event is struggling to fill 185 seats despite it being cheaper to buy the meme coin.

Other advertised guests at the dinner, slated for Trump's golf club outside Washington in Sterling, Va., include boxer Floyd Mayweather, former NBA player Tristan Thompson and mentalist Lior Suchard.

The $TRUMP cryptocurrency briefly commanded a paper valuation exceeding $14 billion in January 2025, around the president's second inauguration. Its per-coin peak was around $74. It now trades below $1, leaving hundreds of thousands of small investors with steep losses while Trump-affiliated entities collected hundreds of millions of dollars in fees and token sales.

Trump himself made $635 million in meme-coin royalties last year, part of a $1.4 billion windfall in crypto income in 2025. The White House didn't respond to a request for comment. Trump has previously said he knows little about the coin besides launching it.

Trump advertised the meme coin as his own in a January 2025 X post. "My NEW Official Trump Meme is HERE!" he wrote. "Join my very special Trump Community. GET YOUR $TRUMP NOW."

At the dinner, the top 29 holders of his coin will also get access to a VIP reception with Trump. The top four will get 18-karat gold Trump watches, provided they hold at least one million Trump coins — worth roughly $850,000. The dinner's website says guests won't be able to privately meet with the president.

Among those who will attend the VIP reception is Morten Christensen, the founder of crypto firm AirdropAlert.com. The $TRUMP investor found a way to qualify for the event's VIP leaderboard while hedging his financial risk.

Christensen said he bought 75,000 $TRUMP tokens at an average price of $1.70 per token to secure his spot, while simultaneously betting against a roughly equal amount using perpetual futures on the decentralized exchange Hyperliquid. Perpetual futures allow traders to bet on an asset's price movements without owning it, with the majority side paying fees to the minority side. Thanks to those fee payouts, Christensen calculated that his plan to attend the dinner netted him a $6 profit. He said he paid roughly $1,200 to qualify for the May 2025 dinner.

The Sunday dinner would mark Christensen's third $TRUMP memecoin-affiliated event, and he used the same trading strategy to help his sister qualify to attend the dinner as well. Even though crypto assets have struggled over the past year, he said he was still looking forward to having a good time.

"I think the vibe will be a lot different," he said. "But it's always a unique experience to be in a room with the president."

Justin Sun, a crypto billionaire who was the largest holder of $TRUMP at last year's dinner, isn't attending this year's event, a person familiar with the matter said. He has since sued Trump's crypto company, World Liberty Financial, accusing it of freezing valuable digital tokens because he refused to invest more money with the company. World Liberty has called Sun's claims "entirely meritless."

The dinner follows losses for the GOP in the midterm elections, which awarded the Democrats majorities in both chambers of Congress come January. Top Democrats are preparing for investigations into Trump's business dealings, including his crypto-related ventures.

In addition to Trump's namesake coin, first lady Melania Trump promoted $MELANIA, which peaked early at a value past $8 but now trades at around 4 cents.

The crypto landscape has shifted dramatically since Trump's inaugural meme-coin dinner. Bitcoin, the largest cryptocurrency, recently dropped below $60,000, more than 50% off the peak above $126,000 it reached in October 2025. The industry also suffered a legislative setback in September when the Clarity Act failed to advance in the Senate; the bill, backed by the crypto industry, was meant to usher digital assets into the mainstream financial system. Meanwhile, surging interest in artificial intelligence and prediction markets has drawn away the retail traders who once fueled the crypto frenzy.
