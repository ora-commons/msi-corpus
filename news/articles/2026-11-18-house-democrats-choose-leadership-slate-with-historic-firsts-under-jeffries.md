## American Dream and Promise Act would offer DACA pathway and TPS protections

House Democrats have selected a leadership slate under Rep. Hakeem Jeffries for the 120th Congress, with historic representation among the team's most senior roles. Rep. Ted Lieu of California would become the first Asian American to chair the Democratic Caucus. Rep. Katherine Clark of Massachusetts is poised to become the first woman to serve as Democratic majority leader.

Rep. Pete Aguilar of California will serve as Democratic whip, and Rep. Joe Neguse of Colorado will be assistant speaker. Rep. Lauren Underwood of Illinois will be the next Democratic Caucus vice chair; her selection would make her the highest-ranking Black woman elected to House Democratic leadership.

At a press conference Tuesday, Jeffries identified the American Dream and Promise Act as one of the early pieces of legislation he plans to bring to the House floor. The immigration bill would provide a pathway to citizenship for people enrolled in Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals, or DACA, and would reissue legal protections for more than 1 million immigrants stripped of their Temporary Protected Status by the Trump administration.

Although Jeffries will have a Democratic Senate to work with, getting President Donald Trump's sign-off on legislation that runs counter to his agenda remains a hurdle amid divided government.

In the weeks since the midterm elections, Jeffries has laid out a handful of priorities for Democrats, including addressing healthcare costs, changing immigration enforcement policy and addressing the war in Iran.

At a press conference following the vote, Jeffries said, “We know millions of people are drowning in this failed Republican economy, and House Democrats are committed to turning things around.”

He added, “Our message to the American people who are struggling in this failed economy is a simple one: We hear you, we see you, we feel you and House Democrats are committed to making life better for you.”

The Associated Press had called 257 House races for Democrats and 171 for Republicans, with seven races not yet called. Democrats will enter the 120th Congress with a comfortable majority following the midterm elections.

In Washington, House Democrats' Wednesday closed-door nomination of Jeffries as the party's leader for the next Congress drew a 251-11 vote, lawmakers said as they left the meeting, where cheers could be heard. His support was well above the 218 votes he will need on the House floor in January to become speaker, which would make him the first Black speaker of the House.

House Republicans last week voted for Ohio Rep. Jim Jordan to lead them in the minority.
