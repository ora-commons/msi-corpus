- The contract has an initial five-year term with two additional option years, the company said.

## SM-6 used in anti-ship, anti-air, and ballistic missile defense

The Standard Missile-6, a multi-role interceptor, is used in anti-ship warfare, anti-air warfare, and ballistic missile defense operations, the company said. The agreement runs an initial five-year term and includes two additional option years, Raytheon said.

Raytheon, an RTX business, added that it has invested heavily to keep pace with rising demand for the weapon.
