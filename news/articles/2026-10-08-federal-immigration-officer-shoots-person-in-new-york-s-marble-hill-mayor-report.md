## Federal immigration officer shoots person in New York's Marble Hill, mayor reports

- A federal immigration officer shot and injured a person in New York City's Marble Hill neighborhood around 4 p.m. on Thursday, Mayor Zohran Mamdani said.
- Mamdani said early reports indicate agents surrounded the person and a 5-year-old child in the car before firing multiple rounds.
- Mamdani said the child was physically unharmed and that the person shot was conscious and at a hospital.
- NYPD said it was not at the scene and referred questions to the Department of Homeland Security, which did not respond to a comment request.
- Mamdani said he plans to ask President Trump to end all ICE enforcement actions in New York City.

## Mamdani plans to ask Trump to halt ICE in New York City

A federal immigration officer shot and injured a person on Thursday afternoon in New York City's Marble Hill neighborhood, near the Bronx, according to Mayor Zohran Mamdani. The New York Police Department said the shooting occurred around 4 p.m.

The person who was shot remained conscious and had been taken to a hospital, Mamdani said in a social-media post. "Early reports indicate agents surrounded him and a 5-year-old child in his car before firing multiple rounds," Mamdani wrote. "We believe the child was physically unharmed."

The NYPD, in its own statement, said it was not involved in the incident and was not at the scene at the time of the shooting. A police spokesman referred questions to the Department of Homeland Security, which oversees Immigration and Customs Enforcement. DHS did not respond to a request for comment.

Mamdani said he planned to speak with President Trump and ask that he end all ICE enforcement actions in the city.
