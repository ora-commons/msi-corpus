## Trump says the board requested national security assurances

Nvidia Corp announced Monday that Jensen Huang was returning as chief executive after resigning earlier in the month. Donald Trump said during a CNBC phone interview that he had spoken with the company’s board “directly and indirectly” and had “convinced” the directors to restore Huang.

Trump said his concern strengthened as he watched what was happening at the company. “I’ve been speaking to the board directly and indirectly, and I felt very strongly about it,” he said. “And I felt more strongly as I saw what was happening with the company, because that was, to me, a very important part of our country.” He added, “I convinced them.”

Huang had resigned from Nvidia earlier this month after reports of extramarital affairs, including with a woman who US intelligence believes was a Chinese spy. Trump said he spoke with Huang on Sunday night, before the company’s board announced the reinstatement on Monday.

Trump said Nvidia’s board asked for assurances about national security risks before reinstating Huang. “They did ask for assurance on the national security threat,” he said. “And we gave him assurance at least with everything we know, and we know a lot.”

Trump said losing Huang would have been a far bigger threat. The board had “acted really responsibly” in deciding to reinstate him, he said, adding that he was “very, very happy” the company “had the courage to do the right thing.”

Nvidia shares jumped 33% after the company announced Huang’s reinstatement.

Trump said he has a close personal relationship with Huang. A photograph accompanying The Guardian’s report showed Trump and Huang at a White House meeting with technology executives on artificial intelligence on 29 September 2026.
