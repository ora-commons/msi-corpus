## Anthropic's offering expected this month amid post-midterm volatility and AI spending doubts

Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude chatbot, plans to raise up to $64bn (£48bn) by selling shares on New York's Nasdaq stock exchange. The loss-making company said it will offer the shares for between $70 and $78 each in an IPO expected to go ahead this month. The listing is expected to place a public-market value on the business.

Fiona Cincotta, a senior market analyst at City Index, said the amount Anthropic wants to raise is "fairly bullish" within "the volatility [and] concerns towards AI that we've seen in sort of particularly last week". Cincotta told Reuters: "It's showing that they are still sort of upbeat about the outlook as far as AI is concerned and that obviously that they think that the demand is going to be there."

The listing arrives in a volatile stretch for AI businesses. Following last week's US midterm elections, the Nasdaq fell sharply on concerns that a Democrat-led Congress could slow development of the technology and the buildout of infrastructure such as data centres.

The Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis' FRED series records the [NASDAQ Composite Index](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NASDAQCOM) at 25,253.14 as of 10 November 2026, a raw reading for the day of the announcement.

Beyond the index move, there are wider fears over the billions of dollars being spent on AI and whether the spending will pay off.
