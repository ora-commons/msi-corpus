## Sheriff says Strathmore shooting may be gang-related; no arrests

Nine people were shot at a concert in Strathmore in central California on Saturday evening, the Tulare County Sheriff's Department said in a statement Sunday.

One of the wounded, a 26-year-old man, was shot in the chest and required surgery, the department said. He remained in critical condition.

Two other victims, ages 19 and 22, also required surgery. Six people sustained minor injuries, according to the department's statement.

The shooting broke out around 7:30 p.m. Saturday at a concert attended by about 150 people, the department said. An unspecified number of suspects opened fire on the crowd.

No arrests were announced.

Strathmore is a small town of roughly 3,000 people in Tulare County, a mountainous region in the center of the state. The show featured performers from neighboring states, the department said.

Authorities said they believe the shooting "may be gang-related" but did not elaborate. The department did not name any suspects or describe how many people it was seeking.

The department said there was no longer any immediate threat to the public. It did not immediately respond to emailed questions seeking further details.
