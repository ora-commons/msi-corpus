## Quarterly profit reached $164.4 million as comparable sales rose 6%

Abercrombie & Fitch raised its full-year earnings and sales outlook after the clothing company reported higher profit and sales in the fiscal third quarter. The quarter ended Oct. 31, and the announcement came before the holiday shopping season.

Abercrombie & Fitch reported profit of $164.4 million, or $3.82 a share, for the quarter ended Oct. 31, up from $113 million, or $2.36 a share, in the comparable period. Quarterly adjusted earnings also came to $3.82 a share, ahead of the $3.08 expected by analysts polled by FactSet.

Quarterly sales climbed 11% to $1.44 billion, topping Wall Street models for $1.37 billion.

Comparable sales, which account for store openings and closings, rose 6% across the company. The namesake brand recorded a 6% increase, while Hollister gained 5%. Analysts had expected same-store sales to rise 3.5%.

Chief Executive Fran Horowitz said the quarter marked Abercrombie & Fitch’s 16th consecutive quarter of growth. She said consumers responded well to new product launches and that marketing helped drive results above company expectations. “Growth was balanced across regions and brands, with each seeing growth rates accelerate from second quarter levels,” she added.

For the full year, Abercrombie & Fitch now expects earnings, excluding some tariff-related items, of $13.70 to $13.90 a share, raised from a prior outlook of $13.10 to $13.60. The company now projects sales growth of around 6%, compared with its previous forecast of 5% growth.

Analysts were looking for adjusted earnings of $12.90 a share for the year on sales of $5.53 billion, marking a 5% increase from last year.

For the current quarter, Abercrombie & Fitch guided earnings, excluding some tariff-related items, of $4.40 to $4.60 a share and sales growth of about 7%. Analysts were looking for earnings of $4.83 a share and sales of $1.78 billion, up 6.8% from last year.

The latest quarter also included a roughly $20 million benefit tied to tariff refunds, which the company added. Abercrombie & Fitch’s current-quarter and full-year earnings guidance exclude some tariff-related items.

“We’re energized by the momentum across our business as we enter the holiday season,” Fran Horowitz said.

Abercrombie & Fitch shares jumped 12% to $157 in premarket trading Tuesday following the announcement. The raised full-year earnings range was above the analyst estimate, while the company’s current-quarter range was below the corresponding estimate cited for the period.
