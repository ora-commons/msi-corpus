## Coalition primary vote sits at 22%, behind One Nation and Labor

Anthony Albanese returned from a series of overseas summits to negotiations on multiple proposed changes, with the new polling showing his popularity taking a further hit.

The Newspoll, published in The Australian on Monday and summarized by Australian Associated Press, put the opposition leader Angus Taylor ahead of Albanese as preferred prime minister for the first time.

The survey also recorded Albanese's net approval rating at a new low of minus 31.

The Coalition clawed back some voter support in the latest survey, with its primary vote standing at 22%. That left it behind One Nation and Labor, which were tied on 27%.

The figures place the three largest blocs on the primary vote within five points of one another, with the Coalition trailing both rivals.
