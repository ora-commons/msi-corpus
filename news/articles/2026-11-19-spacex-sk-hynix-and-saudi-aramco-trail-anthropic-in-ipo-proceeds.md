## Eleven S&P 500 companies were valued above $1 trillion at Wednesday's close

Anthropic's IPO now stands at the top of Wall Street's proceeds rankings. Three earlier offerings sit behind it: Elon Musk's SpaceX raised about $75 billion in June, South Korean chip maker SK Hynix raised $26.5 billion in July and oil giant Saudi Aramco raised $25.6 billion in 2019. Anthropic's $81 billion take made its debut the biggest ever.

Before trading began Thursday, Anthropic offered 980 million shares at $83 each. The initial share price valued the entire company at $1.5 trillion. The San Francisco company and maker of Claude became the first major artificial intelligence laboratory to become publicly traded.

When Anthropic stock began trading late Thursday morning, shares spiked more than 30% above the IPO price, reaching $111.50 a share. That pushed the company's total valuation to $2.01 trillion in early trading. The Associated Press said such a number was once unheard of for a company's first moments on the stock market, but that an acceleration in corporate wealth — and most recently the AI boom — had changed that.

Anthropic was the second business, after SpaceX, to debut with a value over $1 trillion, following SpaceX just months ago. The report said Anthropic would join a handful of other technology giants whose wealth was measured by figures reaching 13 digits.

SpaceX ended its first day of trading on the public market in June with a valuation of $2.1 trillion. Its market capitalization has since fallen closer to $1.95 trillion.

At the market close on Wednesday, 11 companies in the S&P 500 had market values above $1 trillion. Apple led the group at $5.1 trillion, followed by Microsoft at $3.8 trillion and Nvidia at $3.7 trillion, among others. The count did not include SpaceX or Anthropic, which had also joined the trillion-dollar club but were not yet in the S&P 500 index.

Only in recent memory have major corporations been valued above $1 trillion. iPhone maker Apple was the first to pass that threshold, in 2018.

The Associated Press said Anthropic proceeded with the IPO despite jitters among some investors over market volatility, fears of a possible AI bubble and growing unease among the public about the technology's risks overall. How Anthropic ultimately fares in the public market remained uncertain.

A trillion dollars is a thousand times greater than $1 billion and a million times more than $1 million.

More than 8.2 billion people were living on Earth, according to the U.S. Census Bureau. Dividing $1 trillion among the entire population would yield almost $122 for each person.

In physical cash, a trillion U.S. dollar bills laid end to end would stretch nearly 97 million miles, or 156 million kilometers. That would exceed the roughly 93 million miles, or 150 million kilometers, between Earth and the sun.
