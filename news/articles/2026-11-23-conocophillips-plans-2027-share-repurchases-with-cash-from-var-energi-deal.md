## Norwegian oil and gas company Var Energi to pay remaining 2026 tax liability

U.S. oil company ConocoPhillips said Monday that it had agreed to sell its Norwegian oil and gas business and a stake in a U.K. oil terminal to Norwegian oil and gas company Var Energi for about $3.9 billion.

ConocoPhillips said the sale includes its Norwegian oil and gas business and a 40.25% interest in, and operatorship of, the U.K. Teesside oil terminal.

According to The Wall Street Journal, the Norwegian assets produced around 120,000 barrels of oil equivalent a day in 2025.

Under the agreement, ConocoPhillips will receive around $2.5 billion in cash. Var Energi will pay the remaining 2026 tax liability of approximately $1.4 billion, due in the first half of 2027.

ConocoPhillips said the cash will be used for general purposes and share repurchases in 2027.

In a separate release, Var Energi said the deal was accretive to earnings and dividend per share.

The transaction is expected to close by the end of the first quarter next year.
