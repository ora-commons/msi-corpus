## Dec. 11 deadline gives Democrats leverage in the Republican-controlled Senate

Congress faces a Dec. 11 deadline to keep federal agencies operating as a fight over funding for the Iran war raises the risk of a shutdown before Christmas. Republican Sen. Ron Johnson of Wisconsin, the Senate Budget Committee chair, said Friday that he opposes tying government funding to an end to the conflict. NPR reported that Johnson had defended President Trump's war with Iran.

## Funding deadline divides both parties over military spending

The deadline gives Democrats leverage in the Republican-controlled Senate, where most legislation requires 60 votes to advance. Both parties face divisions: Democrats disagree over whether to use the deadline to force an end to the war, while Republicans are weighing bipartisan negotiations against a party-line approach to securing additional military spending.

What remains unresolved is whether Democrats will use the Dec. 11 deadline to press for an end to the conflict and whether Republicans turn to reconciliation to secure defense funding without Democratic votes.

## Johnson says shutting down the war is Trump's power

In the Morning Edition interview, Johnson said, "We're not going to shut down the war. That is President Trump's power. He's exercising his constitutional authority." His remarks opposed tying government funding to an end to the conflict.

## Johnson says Republicans would prefer a spending agreement over budget reconciliation

Johnson said Republicans would prefer to negotiate a spending agreement with Democrats rather than use budget reconciliation, a procedure that could allow Republicans to approve defense funding without Democratic support. He also said, "I think we'll give them one more chance," referring to negotiations before the Thanksgiving recess.

## Shutdown could furlough federal workers before Christmas

A shutdown could furlough hundreds of thousands of federal workers or delay their pay just weeks before Christmas. Johnson said he hoped to avoid one but warned that Republicans would need Democratic support.

## Johnson warns temporary spending measures would hinder military modernization and weapons procurement

Johnson, who previously supported military aid to Ukraine but later opposed additional funding, warned that temporary spending measures would hinder military modernization and weapons procurement. He pointed to the changing nature of warfare in Ukraine as an example.

## Iran war is a point of contention in the American midterm elections

NPR reported that the war launched by the U.S. and Israel against Iran has killed thousands of people, threatened U.S. military bases across the region and become a point of contention in the American midterm elections.

Asked what he wanted to see happen in Iran, Johnson said he hoped Iranians would rise up against their government and that Iran would never acquire a nuclear weapon. "I want to see the Ayatollahs on the dustbin of history," he said.
