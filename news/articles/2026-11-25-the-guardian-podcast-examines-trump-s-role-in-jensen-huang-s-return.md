## Episode considers precedent and possible motives behind Trump's involvement

The Guardian's Decoding America podcast, published Nov. 25, asks: “Can Trump really claim the Nvidia CEO’s return?” The episode examines Donald Trump's claimed role in Nvidia's decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive.

Reged Ahmad and Jonathan Yerushalmy frame the discussion around what many have described as Trump's extraordinary intervention before Nvidia decided to reinstate Huang. The Guardian says the episode will look at how the U.S. president is taking credit for Nvidia's move.

That makes attribution the episode's central issue: the podcast asks how much responsibility Trump can claim for the corporate decision. The announcement does not announce a conclusion on that question.

The podcast also asks why the intervention was unprecedented, focusing attention on the scale of presidential involvement rather than on Nvidia's personnel decision alone.

Another strand is motive. Ahmad and Yerushalmy consider what might have motivated Trump to get involved, and the Guardian's description leaves that question open rather than supplying an answer.

The Guardian published the episode Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 06:00 CET. It is available through Apple Podcasts, Spotify and an RSS feed.

Reged Ahmad and Jonathan Yerushalmy are listed as presenters. Lu Hill produced the episode and handled sound design and mixing, Rachel Herron worked on video production, Joe Koning composed the theme music, and Taylah Strano and Hannah Parkes served as executive producers.
