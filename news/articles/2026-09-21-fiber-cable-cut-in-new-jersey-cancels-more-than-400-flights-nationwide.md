## Fiber cable cut in New Jersey cancels more than 400 flights nationwide

**Subtype:** fact

- Construction contractors accidentally cut a Verizon fiber optic cable in New Jersey on Monday, leading to more than 400 flight cancellations nationwide, including 280 at Newark Liberty International.
- Philadelphia International, LaGuardia, and John F. Kennedy International also experienced delays and cancellations; airspace above the New York City airports has since reopened.
- Philadelphia air traffic control's TRACON facility suffered a separate blown circuit, and its backup line was discovered to have been cut in New Jersey as well.
- The damaged section of communications line spans roughly 600 feet between New Brunswick and Newark.
- The disruption coincides with the UN General Assembly meeting in New York City, which begins Tuesday.

## Philadelphia TRACON's backup line cut in New Jersey

Airspace above the New York City airports has reopened after the disruption.

Verizon said in a statement: "We have a fiber cable running adjacent to an Amtrak rail line in New Jersey. Construction contractors working in the area dug up and cut our cable. We and our partners are actively working to repair the damaged cable and restore connectivity as quickly as possible."

The damaged stretch of communications line spans roughly 600 feet between New Brunswick and Newark.

Separately, Philadelphia air traffic control was dealing with a blown circuit at its TRACON facility on the same day. When its backup system kicked in, controllers discovered that the backup line had also been cut in New Jersey.

The disruption coincides with the United Nations General Assembly meeting this week in New York City, which begins Tuesday.
