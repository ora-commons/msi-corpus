## Hormuz tanker attack, carrier presence and Nvidia earnings round out global news

The Harvard University-led assessment found that cellphone bans in some U.S. schools were leading to "modest improvements" in academic growth among students. The report said strict bans, such as those using lockable pouches, could restore the equivalent of one to two weeks of learning each year. The study, the first nationwide assessment of its kind, analyzed math and reading scores from more than three million students at some 4,600 schools.

That amounted to only about half of the annual decline in test scores seen over the past decade or so, the report said.

In PBS News' coverage of the war in Iran, maritime monitors said a gas tanker went up in flames in the Strait of Hormuz on November 17 after it was hit by multiple projectiles. The crew abandoned the ship after it was engulfed by an inferno off the coast of Oman. Separately, Bahrain said it intercepted a flurry of Iranian drones headed toward a U.S. radar facility. U.S. Central Command reported no damage or injuries.

A third carrier group, the USS Theodore Roosevelt, was also in the region as concerns persisted over long deployments. Acting Secretary Hung Cao, President Trump's pick to lead the Navy, appeared before the Senate Armed Services Committee on November 17 to address the USS Lincoln's prior record-setting time at sea, during which a number of suicide attempts occurred.

Cao said: "I did answer those inquiries right away, especially with the mental health issues. You see it as eight suicide attempts. I see it as eight suicide mitigations because of the chaplains we have on board, the deployment resiliency counselors, as well as mental health specialists." He also acknowledged the Iran war's strain on Navy resources but insisted that joint operations with other U.S. military branches had sent a message to adversaries like China.

In Ukraine, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukrainian drones and missiles hit the Kerch Bridge, which links Russia with the occupied Crimean Peninsula, calling the attack unprecedented. The 12-mile bridge is a vital artery for moving Russian military supplies to the front line. Video released by Ukraine's security service showed drones hitting the bridge. Military officials said part of its road link was severed, while a railway line remained intact, and a Kremlin spokesperson said the bridge would be restored.

Russia meanwhile fired missiles and drones at Kyiv, hitting two bridges and killing at least two people.

In corporate news, Nvidia beat expectations in its first earnings report since CEO Jensen Huang stepped down. The company reported nearly $116 billion in revenue for the last quarter, more than double what it made a year earlier, and expected even higher revenue in the current quarter. The results came after the closing bell on Wall Street.

Before Nvidia's results, stocks struggled again. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell nearly 850 points, the Nasdaq declined almost three-quarters of 1%, and the S&P 500 posted its lowest close since July. On Australia's Christmas Island, tens of millions of red crabs have begun their annual migration from the forest to the ocean to breed. The event is known as the Ruby Red Rush Hour. Local authorities said the phases of the moon determine the exact timing and speed of the migration. Officials clear roads and even build bridges to help the crabs complete the journey. Naturalist David Attenborough described the scene as "great scarlet curtain, moving down the cliffs and rocks towards the sea."

In background reporting on public health, international health officials said the United States was no longer considered a measles-free nation. At a virtual briefing on November 17, the Pan American Health Organization said endemic measles outbreaks in the United States, Mexico and Bolivia signaled the reemergence of a disease once seen as controlled. Dr. Jarbas Barbosa, speaking through an interpreter, said: "Measles is one of the most contagious diseases in the world. Measles' elimination is not a trophy that is won once and placed on a shelf. It is an achievement that must be safeguarded every day by closing immunity gaps and ensuring we reach every community."

PBS News described the measles elimination status as largely symbolic. PAHO nevertheless expects the three countries to submit plans in the coming weeks on how they will try to stop measles transmission for at least a year. In the United States, measles were spreading at the highest rate in more than 30 years, mostly among unvaccinated people, and CDC Director Dr. Erica Schwartz stressed the importance of vaccination.
