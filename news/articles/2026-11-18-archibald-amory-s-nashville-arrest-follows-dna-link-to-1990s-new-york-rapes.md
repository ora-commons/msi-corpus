## Tennessee court papers identify Amory as a wanted fugitive

Archibald Amory, 58, was detained Thursday in Hermitage, a neighborhood of Nashville, after investigators linked him by DNA to two rapes in Queens. The Daily News, citing sources and Nashville authorities, reported that Amory is being held in Tennessee pending his return to New York City.

Sources cited by The Guardian said recent advances in DNA technology and investigative methods helped investigators identify Amory. The DNA connection involved the two Queens rapes, while sources separately connected him to several other uncharged sex assaults. The Guardian described the cases as part of a series of unsolved New York City rapes from the 1990s.

Court papers filed in Tennessee said special victims investigators in Nashville and with the NYPD “identified Archibald Amory as a wanted fugitive in connection to a series of rape investigations originating in the jurisdiction of New York City”.

Amory appeared in Nashville court Monday, according to a spokesperson for the district attorney of Davidson County. He is being held on an $800,000 bond.

Separately, the report said Dereck Rivera-Deleon, 30, was arrested Tuesday at a Miami Burger King where he had been working. Police said he will also be charged in two other Central Park rapes.

Police said Rivera-Deleon will be charged with the sexual assault of a 39-year-old woman who was walking just outside Central Park on 24 October, near E 66th Street and Fifth Avenue. The report alleged that Rivera-Deleon pulled a knife on the woman and dragged her into the park, where he raped her.

A bystander who witnessed the attack called 911. Investigators identified Rivera-Deleon as the attacker through surveillance film and linked him to a Central Park rape in 2023 and another in 2021.

The report also directed readers affected by rape or sexual abuse to Rainn, which listed 800-656-4673 in the US; Rape Crisis, which listed 0808 500 2222 in the UK; and 1800Respect, which listed 1800 737 732 in Australia. It pointed to ibiblio.org/rcip/internl.html for other international helplines.
