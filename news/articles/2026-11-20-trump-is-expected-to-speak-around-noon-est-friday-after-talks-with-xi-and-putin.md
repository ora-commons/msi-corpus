## Trump's expected news conference would follow the first-ever sit-down of all three leaders

President Donald Trump is expected to speak at a news conference around noon EST on Friday after returning from meetings with China’s Xi Jinping and Russia’s Vladimir Putin. PBS News reported that Thursday’s meeting was the first-ever sit-down involving the leaders of all three countries.

The “peace declaration” released after the meeting said the leaders agreed on the need for reforms to the United Nations, on increasing oil supplies and on reducing nuclear risks. The statement did not spell out the type and extent of the United Nations reforms the leaders had agreed to.

The declaration also made no mention of Russia’s war in Ukraine or the U.S.-led war in Iran. The omission left both conflicts outside the public commitments listed in the joint statement.

Post-meeting reports said the three sides agreed that state officials should enjoy immunity from prosecution and opposed what they called “jurisdiction abuse” by the International Criminal Court.

The three leaders started with talk of a shared responsibility for keeping peace, with the United States, China and Russia presented as World War II victors and global powers.

PBS News characterized the post-meeting readouts by the three nations as reflecting “more symbolism than substance.”
