## Trump called Nvidia's oversight panel a good idea

Nvidia said Tuesday that its National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee has three members from the company's board of directors and will work with independent third-party experts. The committee will meet at least once every quarter and oversee national security policies and compliance with export controls. Its work with outside experts will include overseeing efforts to enhance Nvidia's existing national security and global trade compliance team.

The committee's creation followed revelations that former CEO Jensen Huang had an extramarital affair with an alleged spy for China. Huang was forced to resign as Nvidia's CEO last week after scrutiny of his relationship with a Chinese businesswoman who used to lead Singapore-based cloud computing company Megaspeed International.

The Associated Press reported that Megaspeed International is suspected of diverting advanced Nvidia chips to China in violation of U.S. export controls.

Nvidia's advanced chips are in high demand because they are essential for cloud computing and data centers that underpin the artificial intelligence boom. The chips face restrictions on their sale to China.

Chairman Harvey Jones said in a statement, “We are committed to working constructively with government and industry partners to help establish and advance a robust framework that uses every tool at our disposal to safeguard national security while supporting continued technological innovation.”

President Donald Trump said it was “good” that Nvidia had created a national security and export control oversight committee shortly after arriving in the Philippines on Tuesday evening.

“Yes, that’s a good idea. It’s a great company,” Trump said during a photo opportunity in Manila with the leaders of Australia, India and Japan. “It’s a great idea.”

AP writer Darlene Superville in Washington contributed to this report.
