## Earlier Trump-Xi readout warned on Taiwan and urged trade, AI communication

Xi Jinping told Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin that “every effort must be made to prevent nuclear war” and that nuclear weapons should be comprehensively prohibited and completely destroyed. At the same Apec summit session in Shenzhen on November 19, Vladimir Putin said the three countries shared “many converging interests” in resolving conflicts, including the full-scale war in Ukraine that Russia started in 2022. Chinese state media said the trilateral meeting lasted roughly an hour and a half.

Trump said the three countries were “uniquely positioned” to discuss nuclear weapons, placing his comment alongside Xi’s nuclear-prevention and disarmament statements. The Guardian described the leaders’ discussion as covering international peace and nuclear weapons.

Putin told Trump and Xi that their countries shared “many converging interests” when it came to resolving conflicts. The full-scale war in Ukraine, which Russia started in 2022, was among the conflicts he cited.

China released a readout of an earlier Trump-Xi meeting. The Guardian described the readout’s tone as largely positive. The document included a warning on Taiwan and a call to strengthen communication on trade and AI.

Those subjects belonged to the earlier bilateral meeting, while Putin’s remarks and the leaders’ nuclear statements came in the trilateral session. The trilateral meeting took place at the Apec summit in Shenzhen after the earlier Trump-Xi session and before Trump left China.

Trump said the three leaders were “really making history.” On arrival in China, he described Putin as “my friend.” Trump had departed China by the time The Guardian published its account. Across the two meetings, the reported agenda covered Ukraine, nuclear weapons, Taiwan, trade and AI.
