## OnStar AI agent expected to debut on 2027 Cadillac Lyriq EV

General Motors said Wednesday that it plans to use artificial intelligence and new software to develop new vehicles in less time and expand its in-car AI systems. Company officials said they hope to cut the time to develop new vehicles in half, to about two years, using AI and new software. The company did not share a timeline for cutting development time Wednesday.

Those officials said the shorter development cycle could allow GM to better compete with Chinese automakers, which roll out new models more quickly.

"All of these developments are building us the car of the future," CEO Mary Barra said Wednesday during a "GM Forward" event in New York City.

GM also said it will overhaul the computer systems in its cars and add a proprietary AI agent through its OnStar brand.

The automaker said it wants "eyes-off highway driving" available by 2028, with the initial rollout slated to start in 2028 with the $130,000 Cadillac Escalade IQ EV.

"The faster we can iterate, the better our products become," Sterling Anderson, GM's chief product officer, said in an interview with The New York Times. "We are developing better products faster at lower costs."

Anderson said GM's 100-year-plus history in the automotive industry and its experience with its OnStar systems make it better positioned to use AI.

"In the age of AI, data is currency," Anderson said. "For decades, GM's engineering, manufacturing and product teams have generated [an] unparalleled body of data that we're now using to build industry-leading AI tools."

The company's new AI agent is expected to debut on the 2027 Cadillac Lyriq EV. It will be available by subscription and is meant to help customers be more productive while in the car.

Officials said upcoming vehicles, both electric and traditional, will have 40 times the computing power and up to 50% faster cellular connectivity.

GM also said Wednesday that it will cut the cost of electric vehicle batteries and that it is working on new electric vehicles that will cost not much more than gas and diesel vehicles.
