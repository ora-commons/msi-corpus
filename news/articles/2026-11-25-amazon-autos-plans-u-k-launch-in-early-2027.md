## Hyundai will be the first new car brand in the U.K. rollout

Amazon said on Wednesday that Amazon Autos will launch in the U.K. market in early 2027, marking the platform’s first overseas market expansion. Hyundai will be the first new car brand available when the service opens, according to the company.

Customers in the U.K. will be able to buy cars on Amazon.com early next year. According to the statement, Amazon Autos will let shoppers browse real-time dealer inventory, see upfront pricing, obtain an instant trade-in valuation, connect with a local dealer and reserve a car.

Amazon said Hyundai’s IONIQ series, i20 and KONA models will be available on the platform. The company also said more brands and dealer partners will be announced before the launch.

Amazon Autos was first launched in the U.S. in December 2024. The platform has since expanded to more than 150 cities, including Los Angeles, Dallas and New York.

Amazon said the U.S. platform sells vehicles from 35 brands. That footprint gives the company an established base for its first overseas launch.

The size of the U.S. dealership market provides additional context. Around $1.3 trillion of cars were sold by dealerships in the U.S. last year, according to the National Automobile Dealers Association.

The planned U.K. rollout would move Amazon Autos beyond its U.S. operation and place several stages of vehicle shopping on Amazon.com. U.K. customers would be able to move from browsing dealer inventory and viewing upfront pricing to obtaining a trade-in valuation, contacting a local dealer and reserving a car. The initial brand lineup will begin with Hyundai before Amazon adds other brands and dealer partners.
