## DNC will cap each 2028 primary debate at 20 qualifying candidates

The Democratic National Committee announced Monday that it will hold 12 presidential primary debates for the 2028 election, with the first scheduled for June and the final planned for March 2028. The party has set five debates for 2027 and seven in 2028. The DNC said more details, including specific times and locations, are set for announcement in January.

The DNC said participation in each debate will be capped at 20 qualifying candidates, with two-night debates possible if needed. The party set the limit while anticipating a large number of presidential hopefuls.

Qualification standards will resemble those used in 2020, when there was also a large field and 12 debates. The DNC said qualifying candidates will have to meet polling criteria and other standards such as grassroots fundraising.

For the first time, candidates must pledge to disavow dark money as a condition of making the debate stage, a requirement Ken Martin has pushed for. Corruption in government was a top concern for voters in the midterm elections.

The debates could play a key role in generating attention for candidates and narrowing the field of possible nominees. The 2028 field is expected to be crowded.

The announcement comes before major candidates have launched campaigns to succeed term-limited Republican President Donald Trump. It follows the midterm election in which Democrats flipped both the House and Senate and secured their biggest majority in the new Congress in decades.

DNC Chairman Ken Martin said the party is aimed at staying united and helping the eventual nominee win the White House. In an emailed statement, he said: “The DNC is keeping our foot on the gas as we head into the 2028 cycle.”

The Associated Press reported that the debate schedule is “one of the most closely watched decisions the DNC makes,” alongside the party’s selection of a city for the 2028 convention. Earlier this year, the DNC narrowed the list of cities to Boston, Denver and Philadelphia.
