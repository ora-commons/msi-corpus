## Treasury says MEXC facilitated at least $16 billion in illicit transactions

The Treasury Department said Friday that MEXC facilitated at least $16 billion in illicit transactions connected to sanctions evasion and cybercrime from May 2021 to May 2026. The department said MEXC facilitated transactions for the Central Bank of Iran, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, sanctioned Russian entity Garantex and the Russian sanctions-evasion network known as A7.
