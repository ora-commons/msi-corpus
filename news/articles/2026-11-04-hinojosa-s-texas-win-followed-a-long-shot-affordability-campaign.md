The Guardian said she gradually moved from a candidacy overshadowed by James Talarico's campaign to a contest that some polls showed was neck-and-neck against three-term Gov. Greg Abbott, who maintained a large financial advantage. Hinojosa's Nov. 3, 2026, victory will make her Texas's first Latina governor, but the campaign behind that result began with little fanfare after other prominent Democrats declined to challenge Abbott.

Hinojosa's win ended Abbott's bid for an unprecedented fourth term and returned Texas's highest executive office to Democrats for the first time since voters elected Ann Richards in 1990. The Guardian characterized the result as delivering Democrats their most consequential victory in the state in a generation. The Guardian said Democrats had struggled since 1990 to convert a growing urban population and changing demographics into statewide victories; even Beto O'Rourke's 2022 attempt to unseat Abbott ended in defeat.
