## Cook says seeking her biological mother led to a German birth certificate

Federal prosecutors have charged Marva Cook, a 66-year-old Syracuse resident, with voting by an alien. The single federal count stems from Cook's vote in the 2022 midterms.

Cook starred on Syracuse University's women's basketball team decades ago. According to the criminal complaint, she told Homeland Security investigators earlier this month that she had been adopted as a toddler and had always assumed she was a U.S. citizen.

Cook said she had searched for her biological mother and discovered that she had a German birth certificate. Her account says she learned of her lack of citizenship only recently.

Federal prosecutors say Cook had held a green card for several years but cast ballots in recent elections despite not having citizenship. The federal case is based on her vote in the 2022 midterms.

The charge is a misdemeanor. If convicted, Cook could receive up to one year in prison and a fine of up to $100,000. She can also face deportation under federal law.

The arrest is among the latest cases highlighted by the Trump administration as it searches for noncitizen voters ahead of the midterm elections.
