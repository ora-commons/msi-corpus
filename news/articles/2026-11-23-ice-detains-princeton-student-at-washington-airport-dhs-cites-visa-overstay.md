## Doyle says pending application permits Xie to live, work and study

Xie, a Chinese neuroscience researcher and third-year doctoral student at Princeton, was detained by ICE officers at Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport on Thursday as she tried to fly back to New Jersey from a conference in the nation's capital, Carlos Brody, her adviser at Princeton, said in a social media post.

The Department of Homeland Security, which oversees ICE, said Xie overstayed her visa and did not have authorization to study in the United States. The agency said Xie came to the United States on a temporary visa on March 16, 2016, but “chose to violate our nation’s laws by refusing to depart by the required date of September 15, 2016.” DHS said Xie did not have valid permission to study in the United States and that “a pending application does not confer legal status.”

Kerry Doyle, Xie's lawyer, said Xie came to the United States at age 14 with her family from China and has had an asylum application pending for years. Doyle said Xie has permission to live, work and study in the United States while her case is pending, and that Xie is being held in ICE's Caroline Detention Center in Virginia.

The Department of Homeland Security said “Xie will receive due process and remain in ICE custody pending her removal proceedings.”

Rodney Priestley, dean of Princeton's graduate school, said in a statement that “News of Eva’s detention is distressing for me and many members of the Princeton University community.” Priestley said, “We hope Eva will soon be able to resume her studies at Princeton.”

On her professional website, Xie said her research at Princeton Neuroscience Institute “seeks to understand the neural basis of decision-making.” She said she graduated from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2024.

The detention marked another instance of federal enforcement at airports. ICE has ramped up detentions at airports since the summer, especially targeting people it says have overstayed their visas. Two days after Xie was taken into custody in Washington, masked immigration officers detained a father and daughter on an airport jet bridge in Seattle.

The two accounts turn on the same record: Xie came to the United States on a temporary visa on March 16, 2016, was required to depart by September 15, 2016, and has had an asylum application pending for years. DHS treats the stay as unauthorized and says a pending application does not confer legal status; Doyle says the application has permitted Xie to live, work and study in the United States throughout. Xie remains in ICE custody in Virginia pending removal proceedings.
