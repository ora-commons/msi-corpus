## Ship investigator reported woman found dead with multiple facial injuries

According to authorities, surveillance video showed Jason Watford entering the woman’s room aboard the MSC Seascape at 7:46 p.m. The woman entered about 25 minutes later. At 9:15 p.m., video showed Watford leaving the room wearing a different shirt and pants, according to the complaint, and then sitting on the hallway floor looking at his phone.

A ship investigator contacted authorities to report that the woman was found dead in her room with multiple facial injuries. A housekeeper who knocked to deliver towels saw the woman lying motionless on the floor and what looked like blood on Watford’s hands and pants when he opened the door, according to the affidavit.

Watford, 48, of Tyler, Texas, was set to make an initial federal court appearance on a murder charge Tuesday in Galveston. He was being held at a county jail. Whether he had a lawyer who could respond to the allegations was not immediately clear.

The woman, who was not immediately identified, was killed Sunday after the vessel left Galveston, according to an FBI affidavit filed in federal court.

In an interview with FBI agents after the ship returned to Galveston on Monday morning, Watford said he and the woman boarded the vessel together Sunday and began arguing while having dinner at one of the ship’s restaurants, according to the affidavit. The argument continued in their room, it said. Watford told agents the argument caused him to “snap.”

Watford told agents he struck the woman several times and “applied pressure” to her neck “for approximately four to five minutes” until he became exhausted, the affidavit said. He said the woman lay motionless for about 10 minutes until the housekeeper arrived.

A man who said he is Watford’s son declined to comment Tuesday when reached at a phone listing in public records for relatives.

MSC Cruises said authorities told the ship to return to Galveston after the crew reported the passenger’s death, and that the vessel resumed its voyage Monday. The company said it was cooperating with authorities and providing support to those impacted.

The MSC Seascape is a towering white vessel with 2,270 cabins and capacity for nearly 5,900 guests on five- and seven-night trips to the Caribbean, according to the company’s website.

Homicide investigations on cruise ships are uncommon, according to the Associated Press. Earlier this year, a teenager was charged with murder and aggravated sexual abuse in the death of his 18-year-old stepsister on a Carnival Cruise ship out of Florida.
