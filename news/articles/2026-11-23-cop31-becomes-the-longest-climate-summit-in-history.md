## The U.S. stayed away because the Trump administration withdrew from climate talks

COP31 in Antalya, Turkey, ran two days longer than expected, making it the longest-running climate summit in the history of the meetings. The United States was absent from the discussions because the Trump administration had withdrawn from international climate negotiations.

Wealthy nations agreed to provide funding under the Paris Climate Agreement, but COP31 did not finalize the financial-aid targets sought by developing nations.

Many smaller and developing nations are strong defenders of the talks, viewing them as a vital platform to ensure their voices are heard alongside those of larger countries, Waldholz said.

The additional time did not produce a final package. Several elements of the summit's outcome remained unresolved. Nearly 200 nations failed to finalize targets for electrification and financial aid for developing countries, or to address the impacts of climate change.

NPR's Up First newsletter identified fossil fuels as the main cause of climate change and said they contribute about 73% of global emissions. The negotiations centered on a plan to address fossil fuels over the weekend, with disagreement over whether a final outcome had to include a transition away from fossil fuels.

The European Union rejected any conclusion that did not advocate a transition away from fossil fuels. Saudi Arabia and other oil-producing countries opposed that position. Rachel Waldholz said the European Union had concluded that no COP deal was better than a deal that did not address fossil fuels.

Waldholz said money was another major issue. Developing nations sought increased support to tackle climate change, noting that they had contributed very little to climate change but often dealt with its worst impacts.

COP31 therefore ended without a final agreement, and the European Union's rejection of any conclusion that omitted a transition away from fossil fuels, together with oil-producing countries' opposition, left the central dispute unresolved.
