## Brooks says a political risk premium could weigh on SpaceX and Tesla shares

She also said Democratic control of Congress could slow a potential SpaceX-Tesla merger through extra scrutiny. “While Congress cannot stop a merger between SpaceX and Tesla, they could slow it down via extra scrutiny,” Brooks said. The Journal reported that Brooks said Democratic control of Congress “could derail a potential merger between SpaceX and Tesla.”
