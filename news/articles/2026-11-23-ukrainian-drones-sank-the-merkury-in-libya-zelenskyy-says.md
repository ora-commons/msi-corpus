## Satellite images capture the port before and after the explosion

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Monday that Ukrainian-made naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at the Benghazi naval base in Libya during a Sunday attack. He said the operation was part of Kyiv's response to Russia's continuing aggression.

Zelenskyy said Ukrainian warriors deployed seven Ukrainian-made naval drones.

He said the Merkury was sunk despite Russian efforts to counter the attack.

In a post on X, Zelenskyy wrote, “An exemplary Ukrainian operation in response to Russia’s war,” and included a video that The Guardian described as showing the moment the corvette was hit by Ukrainian drones.

Zelenskyy described the Merkury as one of Russia's newest ships and said it was commissioned in 2023 during the full-scale war.

Satellite imagery published by The Guardian shows a Russian corvette and the northern port area before and after an explosion aboard a Russian ship docked at the Benghazi naval base. The image caption credits VANTOR/Reuters.

The caption described the docked vessel as a Russian ship, while Zelenskyy's account identified the corvette as the Merkury and claimed its sinking.

Zelenskyy also said the Russian dry cargo vessel MV Angara was sunk. He said the MV Angara was used for transporting weapons.
