## Trump calls three-day Asia trip great and highly consequential

President Trump said in a social-media post that he planned to discuss trade deals with Thailand, the Philippines and other countries. Trump wrote in the post, "It was a Great and Highly Consequential Trip," The Wall Street Journal reported.

The Wall Street Journal reported that the post announced a White House press conference for Friday afternoon following Trump's return from a three-day trip to Asia.

The trade plan and trip assessment were stated in Trump's post. In its report, The Wall Street Journal said Trump was expected to discuss his trilateral meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese leader Xi Jinping during the press conference.

Natalie Andrews and Philip Wegmann reported the planned press conference for The Wall Street Journal. Andrews is a White House correspondent covering domestic policy and Trump's legislative agenda, while Wegmann is a White House reporter who previously covered the White House and presidential elections for RealClearPolitics beginning in 2019.
