## Marin County issues Stinson Beach warning; Humboldt County orders evacuations

King tides and powerful waves destroyed part of Pacifica's 1,140-foot L-shaped pier in Northern California on Wednesday as officials warned that more beachfront homes were at risk of flooding. A video showed a massive wave tearing down a portion of the structure and slamming it into the ocean.

Officials had closed the pier in the summer after finding cracks in the structure. Beachfront homes in Pacifica, a city south of San Francisco, began flooding this week as king tides, the year's highest tides, arrived in the area. High tides also flooded several communities along California's coast during the week.

The National Weather Service issued a coastal flood warning for the Pacific Coast and bay shorelines along San Francisco Bay and San Pablo Bay through 4 p.m. Wednesday. The service also issued a high surf warning in the Bay Area for Wednesday. Breaking waves of 20 to 30 feet (6 to 9 meters), about two to three stories high, could overpower swimmers and sweep people into the ocean.

“Rising tides will bring water and waves farther up the beach than normal, restricting escape from beaches and coastal area,” the weather service said.

North of San Francisco, Marin County officials issued an evacuation warning for residents of beachfront properties in Stinson Beach. They said large waves expected through the afternoon could send water and debris into roads and homes.

Some homes and streets in the coastal Marin County cities of Mill Valley, Larkspur, Sausalito and Corte Madera also flooded. The flooding came as officials warned that more beachfront homes were at risk.

Farther north, the Humboldt County sheriff's office ordered evacuations in a small coastal community near the Oregon border. Most of the buildings in that community flooded.

King tides occur when the gravitational pull of the moon and sun aligns to raise water levels. Their effects this year are being amplified by the ongoing super El Niño, which warmed the ocean and increased the volume of seawater.
