## Markle is in custody pending a federal hearing in Tampa

Thomas Markle, the 82-year-old estranged father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, cut off an electronic monitoring device at 1:45 a.m. Tuesday, days after his arrest over suspected child sexual abuse images stored in California. First Assistant U.S. Attorney Bill Essayli said authorities arrested Markle shortly afterward and that he remained in custody awaiting a hearing in federal court in Tampa.

Markle had been arrested Friday. Hours later, a judge allowed him to be released on the condition that he be electronically monitored, which typically means wearing an ankle tether. The judge also ordered a mental competency exam and a report by 20 November.

Markle cut off the bracelet early Tuesday, and authorities arrested him shortly afterward. DoJ announced the development while Markle remained in custody.

Essayli said Markle's arrest followed the seizure of approximately 78 items from storage in the Los Angeles area. The items included slides, film negatives and books containing suspected child sexual abuse images.

Markle's arrest followed reports by The Sun, a British tabloid newspaper, that Meghan's half brother found a collection of child sexual abuse images while clearing out their father's home in the Philippines. That discovery led to information about two U.S. storage units in California, which Markle had rented since 2008.

During the Friday arrest, Markle was taken from a senior care center in Lakeland by ambulance. He had been living in the Philippines this year when he suffered a stroke and had his left leg amputated, forcing him to return to the US.

Markle's daughter Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, married Prince Harry at Windsor Castle in 2018. The couple has two children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet. Meghan is an American citizen and actor who starred in the US television drama "Suits".

Meghan recently said she was "deeply distressed" by allegations that a collection of child sexual abuse images had been found at her father's former home. She also disclosed that a letter containing "troubling allegations" about him had been sent to police in London in 2019.

"It made troubling and disturbing allegations against my father and I was given assurances that these were being investigated," Meghan said of the letter. "I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications' distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children was for one reason: to protect them."

Markle remains in custody pending the hearing in Tampa. The ordered competency report is due by 20 November.
