## Western allies funded interceptors as Zelenskyy warned of urgent need

The Financial Times reported that the Pentagon is delaying delivery of about 60 Patriot air-defense interceptors to Ukraine, in reporting carried by The Guardian on November 24. Three people told the newspaper that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth needs to sign off on the interceptors' re-export and that his action is holding up the delivery.

Ukraine's western allies paid for the interceptors. The re-export still requires Hegseth's sign-off, according to the three people who spoke to the Financial Times.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has repeatedly warned of the urgent need for more air-defense missiles to protect the skies above Ukraine. His warnings have come as Ukraine braces for a difficult winter, with Russia continuing to strike critical civilian infrastructure.

A photograph published alongside The Guardian's report showed Ukrainian service members walking beside a Patriot launcher in an undisclosed location in Ukraine. Reuters photographer Valentyn Ogirenko took the image, which was captioned as showing the launcher amid Russia's attack.
