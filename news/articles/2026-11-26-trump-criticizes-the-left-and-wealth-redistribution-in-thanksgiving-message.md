## Trump defends White House repairs and thanks "criminals" sarcastically

In a Truth Social message on Thursday, Donald Trump attacked the “Radical Far Left” and wealth redistribution, defended his focus on White House renovations and offered sarcastic thanks to people he described as murderers, drug dealers and criminals. The Guardian described the holiday post as tinged with bitterness. Chuck Schumer and Hakeem Jeffries, whose party emerged victorious in the midterms, marked the holiday with conventional greetings.

A photograph in The Guardian’s report, credited to Kylie Cooper/Reuters, showed Trump at the annual Thanksgiving turkey pardoning at the White House on 24 November 2026.

Trump went on to wish “a very Happy Thanksgiving to all of those people that have worked so hard to bring our Country to the Radical Far Left” and to “those who want to redistribute Property, so that all of the hardworking people that have built up, over a lifetime, a certain Wealth, will now be forced to distribute it throughout the Country, even to those who refuse to work.”

Trump also revisited his dispute with the news media over White House renovations and decorations. He wrote, “I want to commiserate with those reporters and citizens who don’t understand filth, dirt, lack of maintenance, and corruption, and are upset with me because I took time out of my very busy schedule to fix the 17 broken windows, numerous chipped and decaying columns, broken, dilapidated, and missing moldings, and lack of a proper plaster and paint job on the White House.”

The message continued with sarcasm, thanking “the murderers, drug dealers, and criminals of all shapes, sizes, and colors” whom Trump claimed were “mostly gone now.” He wrote, “Happy Thanksgiving to them anyway, even though from faraway lands,” and concluded, “We miss you dearly, but you will never be back, and America will never allow what you have done to us to happen again!”

Democratic Sen. Chuck Schumer, who was vying to become majority leader in the chamber following his party’s election victories, took a more conventional approach. He wished “everyone in New York and across America a joyous and restful Thanksgiving holiday with your friends and family.” Hakeem Jeffries, whom Democrats had nominated to be the first Black speaker in the House of Representatives, also took a conventional approach. On X, Jeffries wrote, “Happy Thanksgiving to everyone in the community and throughout our great country! Grateful for the civil servants on the job who are working through the stormy weather to keep everyone safe,” followed by, “May God bless you all.”

The contrast followed a midterm election that The Guardian described as a Democratic “blue wave,” with Democrats unseating Republicans after voters roundly rejected Trump’s Republican party. The Guardian said many Americans had been grappling with a higher cost of living, exacerbated by the economic fallout from Trump’s war on Iran, and had backed Democrats in the midterms. Since taking office in January 2025, Trump had repeatedly claimed that an economic “golden age” was taking hold on his watch.

Within the broader holiday message, Trump alleged that economic figures published by the US federal government before the month’s midterms were “fake.” He wrote that he wanted to wish a happy Thanksgiving “to even those who issued fake Economic Numbers, just prior to the Election, and corrected them yesterday, three weeks late, showing that the GDP was actually 4.3%, versus 3.4%.” The US Bureau of Economic Analysis, an agency within the US Department of Commerce, calculates the gross domestic product figures he challenged.

The Guardian said revisions to official economic figures were commonplace. Trump wrote, “Why wasn’t the correct number issued before the Election? I am asking for an immediate investigation of this situation,” adding, “This is not a small error, this is a massive, and purposeful, error.” The Guardian said Trump supplied no evidence for the allegation and had repeatedly challenged trust in economic data that did not support his claims of a historic US boom.
