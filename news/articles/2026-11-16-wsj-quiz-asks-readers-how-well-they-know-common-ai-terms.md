## Quiz probes vocabulary beyond chatbots and hallucinations

The Wall Street Journal put a simple question to its readers on Nov. 16, 2026: how well do you actually understand the language of artificial intelligence? The quiz, published in the Journal's tech coverage and written by Wall Street Journal contributor Simon Constable, tests readers on whether they can keep up with AI talk without asking for help from AI.

The Journal opens by noting that "It seems as if artificial intelligence is everywhere and everyone is talking about it."

The passage then separates the terms readers have already absorbed from the ones described as increasingly common. "But sometimes the conversation is hard to decipher—at least without asking for help from AI. 'Chatbots' and 'hallucinations' are familiar lingo, but do you understand these other AI terms that are increasingly common?" Constable writes.

The exercise is a quiz rather than a reported survey — there is no poll result or data set attached to it, and the Journal does not report how readers scored.
