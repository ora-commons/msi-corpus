## At Terminal 3, Broward Sheriff Fire Rescue personnel identified people reporting injuries

Five people were injured when severe turbulence struck a JetBlue flight coming in from Puerto Rico during its landing at Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport on Tuesday morning, according to The Guardian. The aircraft landed safely at its Florida destination.

In a statement to WRAL, Broward Sheriff Fire Rescue said the aircraft “landed safely at approximately 6:58 a.m. and proceeded to Terminal 3,” where personnel were standing by to evaluate those reporting injuries.

The agency said fire rescue personnel “identified five individuals reporting injuries or requiring evaluation.” The Guardian described five people as injured and said some required hospitalization.

Turbulence has been a risk of air travel since flight began, The Guardian said. The outlet also reported that experts believe the risk is likely to get worse as the climate crisis plays out.

The report cited an analysis of atmospheric data collected between 1979 and 2020 by scientists at the University of Reading in the UK.

That analysis found severe clear-air turbulence increased by 55%. It also found that moderate turbulence had increased by 37% and light turbulence by 17% over the period.

The Guardian did not identify what caused the turbulence on this flight or label the incident clear-air turbulence. Its report cited the atmospheric figures as context on changing turbulence patterns rather than an explanation for this particular landing.
