## Air force reported hits at 20 locations; crews work to restore power across six Ukrainian regions

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Thursday that Russia was preparing another major attack on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure, logistics and critical services. Ukrainian intelligence indicated that Moscow was “completing preparations for a new massive strike,” Zelenskyy said on social media.

Zelenskyy said Russia launched ballistic missiles and 113 drones overnight. Ukraine’s air force reported hits at 20 locations. Zelenskyy said crews were working to restore electricity in six Ukrainian regions.

Ukrainian officials said the strikes hit an apartment building and damaged a hospital, a sports facility, businesses and a gas station. Zelenskyy said, “The Russians continue to target substations, overhead power lines, and the entire power grid.” Kyiv said the attacks aim to deprive civilians of light, heating and running water as winter approaches. Russian missile and drone attacks have battered Ukraine’s power grid, killed civilians and choked the economy since the invasion.

Zelenskyy said Ukraine was pressing its partners to provide more air-defense supplies, especially interceptors for U.S.-made Patriot systems that can stop Russian ballistic missiles but have been in short supply since the Iran war.

U.S. envoy Jared Kushner, President Donald Trump’s son-in-law, is mediating Russia-Ukraine talks with envoy Steve Witkoff. Kushner said Russian President Vladimir Putin had “shown restraint” in the war by agreeing not to use nuclear weapons. On the MD Meets podcast by Axel Springer Creators, Kushner said Putin “has agreed not to use nuclear weapons, which obviously is a big thing, and he's reassured us that that's not his intention.”

After talking at length with Putin, Kushner said he believed the Russian leader wanted peace, almost five years after Moscow invaded Ukraine. Kushner said Putin’s talk of peace depended on the terms: “what (Putin) is saying is peace, but I think it's always peace on what terms.” Kushner said the talks had reached “a little bit of a deadlock” over the fate of Ukrainian territory.

According to Kushner, Putin wants a new relationship with Washington as Russia seeks to keep NATO away from its borders. Zelenskyy and European leaders have criticized Putin for what they describe as Moscow’s refusal to compromise and determination to press on with an invasion that has killed hundreds of thousands of troops and thousands of Ukrainian civilians.

Trump said Thursday on social media that he would like to see Putin “pull back all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe, in the hopes that a Deal can be made between Russia and Ukraine, and made quickly.” Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov said Thursday that the United States was approaching talks on Ukraine with an understanding of the need to eliminate what Russia called the “root causes” of the conflict, including “the creation of threats to Russia's security right on our borders.” Lavrov, in remarks posted on the Russian Foreign Ministry’s website, said Washington was “open to dialogue” and interested in diplomatic contacts.

Ukrainian forces struck a Russian military airfield and an oil pumping facility in two overnight attacks, Ukraine’s General Staff said Thursday. A Ukrainian drone strike on a parking lot in the Russian-occupied part of Ukraine’s Zaporizhzhia region killed five people and wounded three, Moscow-installed officials said. In Russia’s Krasnodar region, a drone attack wounded four people and damaged four apartment buildings, local officials said.

The Russian Defense Ministry said its forces intercepted 343 Ukrainian drones overnight over 15 Russian regions, illegally annexed Crimea, and the Azov and Black seas.

Ukraine had used its domestically produced long-range weapons to hit targets in Russia and had struck a Russian naval vessel docked at a port in Libya with sea drones last weekend. The attack suggested the war was expanding beyond Ukraine and alarmed some European leaders.

Italian Premier Giorgia Meloni said Thursday that she supported Ukraine’s fight against Russia but that the war “should not and cannot be expanded to the Mediterranean.” Speaking at a European Union summit in Brussels, Meloni said: “I think that we must be very clear about this, that we must be, as we have been in recent days as well, clear with our Ukrainian friends and we must also be clear with our partners.” Trump said in another post that Ukrainian and Russian forces alike should avoid “any actions that intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the War.”
