## García Cuerva named to lead service under Vatican development and charity office

ROME — Pope Leo XIV created a global initiative on Wednesday to coordinate the Catholic Church’s spiritual care for inmates, naming Jorge Ignacio García Cuerva, the archbishop of Buenos Aires, as its first coordinator. The new service for prisoners’ pastoral care reports to the Vatican’s development and charity office.

The service is intended to coordinate church activity at the parish, diocesan and national levels. It aims to help each level develop pastoral care for inmates, promote prison ministry globally and assist local churches in creating programs for people behind bars.

Training is another stated function. The service also aims to train workers and help churches communicate with civil authorities to arrange prison visits and ministry.

The initiative follows a priority associated with Pope Francis. Francis ministered at Devoto Prison in Buenos Aires for years while serving as archbishop and scheduled prison visits throughout his pontificate, including during foreign trips and in Rome.

A few weeks ago, García Cuerva welcomed Leo to Devoto Prison on the last day of the pontiff’s visit to Argentina. The visit placed Leo at the same Buenos Aires prison where Francis had long ministered, with García Cuerva serving as the archbishop who hosted the pontiff.

Leo has continued the prison visits associated with his predecessor. He visited inmates at a prison in Equatorial Guinea that the Associated Press described as notorious and later visited inmates in Buenos Aires.

Leo’s prison messages have emphasized hope and belonging. The report said the aim has been to encourage inmates and show them they remain beloved children of God. In Buenos Aires, Leo urged inmates to view their time behind bars as an opportunity for redemption and reconciliation.

Prison ministry is an important tenet of the Catholic faith, a principle the report linked to the Biblical mandate, “I was in prison, and you came to me.” Leo’s announcement adds a dedicated coordination structure to that ministry: García Cuerva will serve as the first coordinator, and the service will report to the Vatican’s development and charity office.
