## Apple says quarterly earnings included 5 cents per share from tariff refunds

Apple Inc. reported stronger-than-expected fiscal fourth-quarter revenue on November 2, following John Ternus's start as CEO in September. The Cupertino, California-based company generated $114.5 billion in revenue and earned $30.71 billion, or $2.10 per share, in the July-September period.

Analysts, on average, expected $1.99 per share on $113.4 billion in revenue, according to a FactSet poll. Apple's $2.10 per-share result and $114.5 billion in revenue both exceeded the averages in that poll.

Revenue increased 12% from $102.5 billion in the same quarter a year earlier. Earnings also rose 12%, reaching $30.71 billion compared with $27.47 billion, while per-share earnings increased to $2.10 from $1.85. Apple said its earnings included 5 cents per share from tariff refunds.

Apple's stronger showing bucked a downward trend for tech giants this quarter. Alphabet, Amazon and Intel were among the companies with disappointing quarterly results.

Bourne also said record iPhone revenue and strong growth in China demonstrated the resilience of Apple's business. In Bourne's assessment, Apple's operating performance contrasted with the investor uncertainty surrounding massive artificial-intelligence investments by Big Tech rivals.
