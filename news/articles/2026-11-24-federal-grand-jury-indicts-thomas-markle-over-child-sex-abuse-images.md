## Markle denies possession and remains in custody in Florida

A federal grand jury in Los Angeles indicted Thomas Markle for possession of child sex abuse images. Markle, 82, is the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, and Prince Harry's father-in-law. He remains in custody in Florida and is expected to be arraigned in Los Angeles in the coming weeks.

The U.S. Attorney's Office in the Central District of California said the indictment alleges that the child sexual abuse material included at least one image involving a prepubescent minor and a minor under 12 years old. The indictment identifies 35mm negatives as the material at issue and alleges Markle knowingly possessed negatives containing child sexual abuse materials on Oct. 30 and Nov. 4.

The indictment further alleges that the negatives had been mailed, shipped and transported through interstate and foreign commerce. The U.S. Attorney's Office said Markle allegedly knew the images contained such materials during both instances.

The FBI arrested Markle on Nov. 6 at an elderly care home that specializes in dementia. His arrest came after investigators searched a storage unit he had allegedly rented in Los Angeles and uncovered child sexual abuse material.

A judge ordered Markle to remain in jail pending trial earlier this month after he allegedly removed a location-monitoring device. Markle remains in custody in Florida as he awaits the Los Angeles arraignment expected in the coming weeks.

Last week, a Florida judge declared Markle mentally competent to stand trial. The finding came after the order keeping him jailed and before the grand jury returned the indictment.

Markle has denied possessing images of child sex abuse.
