## Randy Tifft describes a vehicle parked across river during shooting

Carey Washington Vinson, 19, was fatally shot in the head while camping with friends at a campground in Holt, Florida, near the Blackwater River. His father, newly elected Escambia County commissioner Joe Vinson, said people across the river pointed a gun at the occupied campsite and fired with malicious intent. Police said they responded to an emergency call, found Carey Vinson with a gunshot wound to the head and pronounced him dead at the scene.

The local sheriff's office asked the public for help identifying suspicious activity at the campground. In an Instagram post, Vinson said he needed to “correct a misimpression” that the shooting had been an accident. “This was not a freak hunting accident — not someone firing carelessly in the dark,” he wrote.

Vinson placed his son inside a tent near the river when a bullet struck him in the head. Vinson wrote, “As we understand it, some person(s) stood on the opposite bank of the river and pointed a gun at the sandbar campground, knowing it was occupied,” and said those people were “close enough to see the campfire and hear people talking.”

According to Vinson, the shooters fired shot after shot with malicious intent, paused after the panicked campers screamed for them to stop, fired another volley and laughed as they drove away.

Santa Rosa County Chief Deputy Randy Tifft told US media that a vehicle was parked on the other side of the river at the time of the shooting. According to Tifft, witnesses saw the vehicle leave after the gunfire.

Carey Vinson was studying history at Florida State University, his father said. Vinson described his son as a “thoughtful young man” and said: “He was so outgoing and athletic, playing soccer, hiking mountains, and beating the rest of us to the top of Giotto's Tower and Mount Fuji.”

Vinson also said: “Most of all, he had a tender and loving heart — for his friends and family, for his girlfriend, for animals, for the marginalized.”

Joe Vinson called on anyone with information about the shooting to come forward to the police.
