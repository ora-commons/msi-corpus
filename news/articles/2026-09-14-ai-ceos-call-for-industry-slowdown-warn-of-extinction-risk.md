## B'Tselem details Israeli 'elimination' of Palestinian life in West Bank

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly appealed for the artificial intelligence industry to "slow down," with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk backing the call to pause what they described as "reckless" development, citing fears the technology could soon run out of control. AI-linked stocks tumbled after the joint statement.

The Guardian's Robert Booth detailed Amodei's three-part proposal: first, each US AI company would grant access to third-party evaluators; second, all companies in democratic countries would establish common safety standards; and third, the world's democratic AI powers would coordinate with autocracies — notably China — to control the race. Separately, a former Anthropic employee warned last week that AI could precipitate human extinction by 2030.

President Donald Trump, on a visit to his golf course in Ireland, played down the warnings. Trump said "very negative forces" were "bringing up things that won't happen." Former US President Barack Obama made a direct appeal to Democrats to coalesce around a unified AI safety policy, arguing that waiting for voluntary compliance from tech firms was a recipe for "governance failure."

Israel is working toward the systematic "elimination" of the conditions that sustain Palestinian collective life in the occupied West Bank, according to a report published by the human rights group B'Tselem. The Israeli organisation said Israel is "dramatically intensifying its attacks on every aspect of Palestinian life in the West Bank." The B'Tselem report stated: "Palestinian communities are being pushed out in an accelerating process of ethnic cleansing, while an ever-expanding Israeli presence is being established … Elimination in this context means sustained harm to the conditions that enable Palestinians to exist as a people with land, institutions, language, culture, social ties, leadership, shared memory and history, and a social and political horizon. It is an ongoing political project."

Republican Congressman Tom Tiffany said he had to swim to safety after a plane he was traveling in made an emergency landing in a lake late Saturday. Tiffany is running for governor on the Republican ticket in Wisconsin and has been endorsed by Trump. He said he was returning from the La Crosse County Lincoln Day Dinner when the light aircraft lost power as it approached Wausau Downtown Airport. Tiffany said the pilot made an emergency landing in Lake Wausau and that he and the pilot ended up in the water and swam to safety. Emergency responders took both to a nearby hospital, where they were treated for minor injuries.
