Phillips found the voters, preachers and politicians he met hopeful that Flávio — whom the Guardian's episode calls 'the far-right candidate' — would bring back the mining his father, Jair Bolsonaro, encouraged when in power. The episode says that administration oversaw the felling of 11m acres (4.5m hectares) of rainforest.
