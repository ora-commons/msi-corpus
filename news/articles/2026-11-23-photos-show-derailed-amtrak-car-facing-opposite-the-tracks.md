## Associated Press left messages seeking comment from Amtrak on Monday

Photos showed a train car derailed into a field, sitting upright but facing the opposite way of the tracks.

The bulldozer was shown on its side directly next to the track, with major pieces torn apart. Photos also showed emergency service vehicles and workers helping passengers off the cars.

The Lafayette Fire Department said the crash occurred Sunday evening when the train collided with an 18-wheeler east of Lafayette. Authorities said five people were injured.

Authorities described the Amtrak train as carrying nearly 100 passengers when it partially derailed, while the Lafayette Fire Department said it carried 95 passengers.

The Lafayette Fire Department said the semitruck was carrying a bulldozer. The department posted that detail on social media.

Sgt. Ashley Wood of the Lafayette Police Department told reporters at the scene that the truck had been crossing the intersection of the railroad crossing.

The Lafayette Fire Department said the train was carrying 95 passengers. The department said firefighters helped the passengers exit once it was safe to do so.

Lafayette Mayor-President Monique Blanco Boulet said only minor injuries were reported.

The Associated Press left messages seeking comment from Amtrak on Monday.
