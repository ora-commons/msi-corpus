## FBI seized about 78 items from two Los Angeles-area storage units

A Florida judge said Thursday that Dr. Amie Gerodimos, an expert, determined that Thomas Markle, the 82-year-old estranged father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, was mentally competent to face charges involving child sexual abuse material. Markle remains in custody while the case remains in federal court in Tampa and is set to shift eventually to a similar court in Southern California.

Markle's defense lawyer said the defense would not contest the finding by Amie Gerodimos. The competency exam was ordered after Markle's arrest at a senior care center in Lakeland, Florida, on November 6.

The FBI said investigators searched two storage units in the Los Angeles area and seized approximately 78 items. Slides, film negatives and books among the items contained suspected child sexual abuse images, the FBI said. Some images appear to depict Markle with children, the FBI said.

Markle was initially released on bond. Authorities arrested him again on November 10, accusing him of cutting off his electronic ankle monitor.

Markle had a stroke earlier this year while living in the Philippines and had to have his left leg amputated. The stroke and amputation forced him to return to the United States and settle in Florida.

The Sun reported that Meghan's half-brother found child sexual abuse images while clearing out Markle's home in the Philippines. The discovery led to a search of the two United States storage units in California, which Markle had rented since 2008.

Markle is also under investigation in the Philippines for possession and intent to distribute images. The Women and Children Protection Center, a branch of the Philippine National Police, filed a formal criminal complaint against him on Monday, police spokesperson Brig Gen Allen Rae Co said in a written statement.

The complaint was filed with the prosecutor's office following an investigation into digital devices found at Markle's former home in Cebu City. The prosecutor's office will assess the case for possible charges.

Markle did not attend his daughter Meghan's 2018 wedding to Prince Harry at Windsor Castle. She said at the time that he needed to tend to his health. Meghan is an American citizen who starred in the US television drama *Suits*.
