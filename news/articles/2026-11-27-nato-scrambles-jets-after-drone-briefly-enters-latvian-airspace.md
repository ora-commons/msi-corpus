## Latvian interceptor drone deployed; army leaves craft’s origin unspecified

Latvia’s army said a drone briefly flew over Latvian territory near the border with Russia during the night from Thursday to Friday. NATO fighter jets assigned to the alliance’s air-policing mission in the Baltic States were scrambled, and Latvia’s national armed forces deployed an interceptor drone.

The Latvian army statement said: “Fighter jets from Nato’s air-policing mission in the Baltic States were scrambled, and an interceptor drone from the national armed forces was deployed,” without specifying the origin of the drone.

The report described NATO fighter jets as protecting the skies of Baltic states. Reuters reported that two drones had been shot down over Latvia by NATO fighter jets in 2026. The army account of the overnight overflight did not say that the aircraft was shot down.

Since Russia began its invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, several countries bordering the conflict have reported incursions or drone and missile crashes on their territory. The Guardian did not report a connection between those wider incidents and the aircraft that briefly crossed Latvian airspace.

The response itself was documented by the Latvian army: NATO aircraft were scrambled and Latvia deployed its own interceptor. The aircraft’s origin remained unresolved.
