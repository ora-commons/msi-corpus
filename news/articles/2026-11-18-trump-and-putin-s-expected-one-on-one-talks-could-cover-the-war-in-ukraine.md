## Trilateral meeting in Shenzhen will bring Trump, Xi and Putin together

Donald Trump arrived in Shenzhen on Wednesday, where Xi Jinping greeted him on the tarmac and Vladimir Putin later exchanged greetings with him before a reception. BBC News said the main focus of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum would be the Thursday US-China-Russia talks, the first time the three leaders would meet together in person. Trump and Putin are expected to hold one-on-one talks on Thursday on the sidelines of the forum, and BBC News reported the war in Ukraine could be discussed during those talks.

Trump's welcome included a handshake and hug with Xi on the tarmac and a red carpet for Air Force One. The arrival ceremony also featured a cheering crowd, honour guard, military flyover and flowers. Xi's personal reception for Trump echoed the airport welcome Trump staged when Xi visited Washington in September, a gesture Xi called "special."

Xinhua quoted Xi as saying he wanted to pursue a "constructive strategic and stable relationship based on respect, fairness, and equality." BBC News described the greeting as a diplomatic gesture designed to begin the week's talks between Trump and Xi on a cordial note.

Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Putin and Trump had not yet held a substantive meeting and had merely greeted each other on Wednesday. Peskov said that "no more than an hour" had been allocated for Thursday's trilateral session. A full agenda had not been made public, but BBC News reported that possible subjects included the wars in Ukraine and Iran, nuclear proliferation, trade tensions and developments in artificial intelligence.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky raised fears that a plan to end the war was being discussed in the absence of representatives from Kyiv or Europe. BBC News reported that it remained unclear whether China wanted the war in Ukraine on the agenda in Shenzhen, while China talked more generally about the need to discuss "safeguarding world peace and promoting strategic stability."

China has officially remained neutral since Putin launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022. Beijing has rejected accusations from Kyiv and its allies that China has tacitly supported Moscow, including by exporting dual-use technology used by the Russian military.

Wednesday's handshake marked the first in-person meeting between Trump and Putin since August 2025, when Putin travelled to Alaska for talks on the Ukraine war that failed to deliver a breakthrough. It was also their first in-person meeting in more than a year.

Several hours before Trump's arrival, Putin landed in Shenzhen and held around two hours of talks with Xi, with whom he has pressed for closer economic and defence relations. Xi said both countries would "move forward steadily and boldly" and that the relationship was "stable as a mountain, unshaken by storms."

BBC News described Shenzhen, the south-east Chinese city hosting the summit, as a booming area for manufacturing and advanced technology. The summit was taking place against the backdrop of the ousting of Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang, who admitted to having an affair with a woman whom US intelligence suspected of spying for Beijing. China dismissed the spying accusation as a "smear."

Trump has pursued warmer relations with China, but BBC News said the Huang episode pointed to ongoing tensions between the two powers. An accompanying BBC segment examined the Nvidia case as an unexpected hurdle for China's artificial-intelligence ambitions, while correspondent Steve Rosenberg characterized the historic talks with Xi and Trump as "a coup for Putin."

For Thursday, the public agenda remained limited to a broad list of possible subjects, and Peskov said no more than one hour had been allocated for the trilateral session. BBC News reported that the war in Ukraine could be discussed in the expected Trump-Putin one-on-one talks. Zelensky's concern about a plan to end the war without Kyiv or European representatives, and the uncertainty over whether China wanted Ukraine on the Shenzhen agenda, remained unresolved before the scheduled meeting.
