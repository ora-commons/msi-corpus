## Planned consultation and shareholder vote precede a possible 2028 transition

Compass Group said it plans to move its primary stock-market listing to the United States while retaining a secondary listing in the United Kingdom, the British catering contractor said as it reported higher fiscal-year earnings. Compass Group said it would start a shareholder consultation on the proposal, and the move would be voted on at a future general meeting, subject to initial shareholder support. If approved, the transition would be expected to take place in 2028.

Compass Group said it would start a shareholder consultation for the proposed move, and the relocation would be voted on at a future general meeting, subject to initial shareholder support. If shareholders approve the proposal, the company expects the transition to take place in 2028.

The board said the United States has become the natural long-term listing venue for Compass Group because it accounts for most of the company's earnings. Under the plan, Compass Group would keep its secondary listing in the United Kingdom even as its primary listing changes.

Compass Group already uses U.S. dollars for financial reporting and for trading its London-listed shares.

Compass Group reported pretax profit of $3.04 billion for the year through Sept. 30, up from $2.58 billion a year earlier.

Revenue grew 10% to $50.8 billion, slightly ahead of a company-compiled consensus of $50.7 billion. Organic revenue growth was 7.5%, beating expectations for a 7.2% rise.

For fiscal 2027, Compass Group expects high single-digit underlying operating profit growth, driven by organic revenue growth of around 7%.

Compass Group also said it would resume its share-buyback program and return up to $1.5 billion over the next two years.

Compass Group's board declared a final dividend of 48.5 cents per share, taking the full-year dividend to 74 cents, compared with 65.9 cents a year earlier.
