## Giuffra's Trump representation drew criticism inside and outside the firm

Robert Giuffra, a litigator, and Scott Miller, a corporate lawyer, will step down as co-chairs of Sullivan & Cromwell in January 2028, according to The Wall Street Journal. Carsten Berrar and Sharon Nelles will succeed them; Giuffra and Miller have co-led the firm since 2022.

The succession was announced a year or more in advance, a practice The Wall Street Journal said was not uncommon among large law firms. Sullivan & Cromwell has no prescribed term limits for its chairs, but its policy calls for lawyers to leave the leadership role after turning 65. Giuffra will be 67 years old next year, and Miller will be 66.

Sullivan & Cromwell is a 147-year-old New York firm with roughly 900 lawyers. The firm attracts Ivy League graduates and represents some of the nation's biggest corporations.

Giuffra's work for Trump supplies part of the context for the change. He agreed after the 2024 election to represent Trump in two New York appeals, including Trump's conviction in the Stormy Daniels hush-money case, and had also been under consideration to run the Justice Department.

The Wall Street Journal described Giuffra as breaking from other elite lawyers when he took the Trump cases. The decision drew controversy inside and outside Sullivan & Cromwell. Partners bristled about not being consulted before he took on the appeals while many larger firms still eschewed work for Trump.

Giuffra also advised Trump, including helping broker a deal for the president to drop an executive order targeting rival law firm Paul Weiss last year, according to the report. That arrangement set off a series of similar deals.

Democratic lawmakers pressed Giuffra to disclose his role in the deals. With the party poised to take control of Congress early next year, lawmakers sent letters to Kirkland & Ellis, Skadden Arps, Simpson Thacher and other dealmaking law firms, asking them to disclose information about work done for the government.

Sharon Nelles will become Sullivan & Cromwell's first woman chair. She has worked with clients including Volkswagen, JPMorgan, Moody's, Airbnb, Boeing, Goldman Sachs and Microsoft.

Carsten Berrar runs the firm's Frankfurt office and helps lead its capital-markets practice. He will be Sullivan & Cromwell's first chair based outside the United States and has advised Delivery Hero on its sale to Uber, as well as Porsche and Zalando.
