## Police search for suspect as mall remains an active crime scene

One person has died and six others were wounded in a shooting Friday night inside the Solano Town Center mall in Fairfield, California, police said.

Police said officers responded to a shooting at around 8:30 p.m. inside the mall at the Spirit Halloween store.

In a post on Facebook, police said there were seven confirmed victims with gunshot wounds. One of the victims later died of their injuries, police said.

The other victims were taken to local hospitals, where at least three were reported to be in critical condition, police said.

Police said the mall location remains an active crime scene, with officers talking to witnesses as they work to identify the suspect responsible for the shooting.

No further information was available on the identity of the victims or the details of the shooting as of the latest statements from police. An investigation was underway.
