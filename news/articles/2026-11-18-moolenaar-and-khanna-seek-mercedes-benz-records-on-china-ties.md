## Lawmakers cite security risks tied to China’s automotive sector

Risk Journal reported that Mercedes-Benz faces a bipartisan Congressional investigation into its ties with China. Reps. John Moolenaar of Michigan, the leading Republican of the House Select Committee on China, and Ro Khanna of California, its top-ranking Democrat, said Tuesday they want records and information about Mercedes-Benz's relationships with BAIC and other Chinese companies. The lawmakers said the automaker's integration with China's automotive sector raises national security concerns. Mercedes-Benz did not respond to a request for comment.

Kalshi will no longer list some betting markets associated with President Trump after regulators ordered the company to pause contracts predicting what the president might say in public, according to people familiar with the matter. The people familiar with the matter said Kalshi is halting all contracts that allow users to place bets on what the president might say on specific dates.

Deutsche Bank shares slid Tuesday after media reports based on leaked documents detailed the lender's internal deliberations and handling of more than a decade's worth of missteps and scandals. The report also noted that Deutsche Bank said it invested in control systems between 2019 and 2025.

A classified internal communications network at the National Security Agency is down, U.S. officials said, the latest in a string of digital systems that have temporarily gone down. A person familiar with the incident said NSANet was offline only temporarily because the agency was updating software to patch the system, and that there was no evidence of a cyberattack.

Shortly before the network revelation on Tuesday, Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin spoke with CNBC about other recent issues, including a Microsoft 365 service interruption and a technical issue that caused a nationwide groundstop for flights. Mullin said those issues likely were not part of an attack on the United States.

OpenAI, the ChatGPT maker, said it was making its advanced cybersecurity models available to NATO, the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, to help the military alliance protect its networks from increasingly sophisticated threats. The alliance's Communications and Information Agency, its technology and cyber hub with a team of more than 3,000 civilian and military experts, was using the models to probe suspicious activity, detect vulnerabilities and deploy fixes, OpenAI said.

Dow Jones Risk Journal scheduled a webinar at 10 a.m. ET on Wednesday, Nov. 18, ahead of Anthropic's IPO, to examine the cost of artificial intelligence compute. The session's description said it would examine data on the economics of AI, from Anthropic's higher-priced models to lower-cost Chinese competitors, and what pricing differences reveal about AI competition, the sustainability of premium pricing and financial decisions businesses face as they expand their use of AI.

In background reporting, the newsletter said the Securities and Exchange Commission was investigating Nvidia following Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang's resignation, according to people familiar with the matter. The newsletter described the civil investigation as adding to the crisis facing Nvidia, whose shares have fallen more than 10% since Huang's departure. People familiar with the matter said Huang's resignation followed revelations of two extramarital affairs, one with a person described as a suspected Chinese spy and another with an employee. The focus of the SEC investigation could not immediately be learned. Some people familiar with the matter said the inquiry includes whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors.

Home Depot's Richard McPhail will replace Chief Executive Officer Ted Decker. McPhail said: “Housing is a cycle, and we have been through a prolonged period of pressure in housing and all those things related to it. Having said that, you should never bet against the housing market of the United States.”
