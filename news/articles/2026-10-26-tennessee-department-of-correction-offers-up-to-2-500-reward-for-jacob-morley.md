## Investigators reviewed surveillance footage of Morley leaving in his prison uniform

Prison officials offered a reward of up to $2,500 for the arrest of Jacob Morley, 28, a man serving life imprisonment who escaped from the Northwest Correctional Complex in northwest Tennessee by slipping through a fence, according to the Tennessee Department of Correction. Law enforcement throughout the area searched buildings and other structures near the prison, which sits about 90 miles (145 kilometers) north of Memphis.

Investigators who reviewed security cameras saw Morley escape while wearing his prison uniform, officials said. They did not release any other details, including how Morley got through the fence.

The department said officials realized Morley was unaccounted for around 11:30 p.m. Sunday at the Northwest Correctional Complex in Tiptonville. Morley is 5-foot-11 (1.8 meters), 240 pounds (109 kilograms), with brown hair and green eyes.

Morley was sentenced to life for killing his co-worker Cody Birchfield in 2022. Birchfield's body was found in a box under Morley's Carter County home after Birchfield had been missing for a few weeks.

Along with first-degree murder, Morley was also convicted of aggravated burglary, tampering with evidence, vandalism and evading arrest. When Morley saw deputies at his home in 2022, he led police on a chase that ended in North Carolina, authorities said.

Several violent attacks between inmates and by employees have been reported in recent years at Northwest Correctional Complex. A grand jury in July returned dozens of indictments for five inmate murders, assaults against staff and inmates and other violence.

Northwest Correctional Complex can house 1,776 men and can house minors who have been convicted as adults, according to the Tennessee Department of Correction. In 2023, an audit of Tennessee prisons found that the department was struggling to fill open positions at the prison and had a 61% vacancy rate for correctional officers in that fiscal year.
