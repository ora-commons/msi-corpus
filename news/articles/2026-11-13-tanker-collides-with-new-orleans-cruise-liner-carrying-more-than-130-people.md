A tanker collided with a river cruise liner on the Mississippi River in New Orleans on Friday, November 13, 2026, with rescue efforts under way at the scene.

The Port of New Orleans' press secretary said the tanker collided with the American Cruise Lines vessel American Heritage at around 12:30 p.m. local time on Friday. Port authorities told local media there were about 129 passengers onboard as well as crew — a total of more than 130 people aboard the cruise liner.
