## TI revenue outlook exceeds forecast; latest quarter had broad-based growth

The Dallas-based semiconductor company reported Wednesday that its third-quarter profit reached $2.43 billion, or $2.62 a share, above FactSet's $2.44 per-share estimate and up from $1.36 billion, or $1.48, a year earlier.

Quarterly revenue climbed 28% over the year to $6.06 billion, exceeding the $5.94 billion analyst forecast.

Texas Instruments' analog segment grew revenue 32% to $4.91 billion. Its embedded processing segment revenue rose 21% to $848 million.

The Wall Street Journal reported that Texas Instruments logged higher profit and revenue in its latest quarter on broad-based growth led by industry, automotive and data-center markets.

A TXN market-data entry displayed a 2.47% increase.

For the fourth quarter, Texas Instruments expects revenue between $5.8 billion and $6.3 billion, above the $5.76 billion analyst expectation.

Texas Instruments also expects fourth-quarter earnings per share of $2.45 to $2.79. Analysts expect $2.31 a share.
