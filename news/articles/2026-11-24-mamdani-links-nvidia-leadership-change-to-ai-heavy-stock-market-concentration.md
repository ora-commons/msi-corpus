## Trump’s intervention preceded Huang’s reinstatement and Nvidia’s 31.5% rebound

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani said on Monday that the Nvidia leadership crisis illustrated the stock market's dependence on the artificial-intelligence boom. Speaking after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive, Mamdani said the market was “disproportionately dependent” on stocks related to the boom powered by Nvidia's chips. Mamdani also said, “It cannot help but give you a sense of concern about just how much this technology is now dominant.”

The Guardian reported that Huang stepped down as Nvidia's chief executive on November 12 after a reported extramarital relationship with a person described as a suspected Chinese spy. Nvidia's share price dropped dramatically during the episode. Huang returned as CEO on Monday after Donald Trump intervened, and the California-based company's value bounced back 31.5%.

Mamdani framed the leadership change as a matter of public economic exposure. Huang and his wife, Lori Huang, have been married since 1985 and have combined assets of about $160bn, and The Guardian said the couple had made no statement about their marriage in the wake of the reported affair. At Monday's news conference, Mamdani said, “I do not think that working people across the country signed up for their pensions being tied to the question of Jensen Huang’s fidelity to his marriage.” He added, “And yet, for many days, that had a massive impact.”

Mamdani then used the reported scale of a potential divorce settlement to make a comparison with social spending. “We’re speaking about a divorce settlement that has been rumored to be around $81bn,” he said. Mamdani said the amount was the cost of providing universal childcare for every three- and four-year-old in the United States for an entire year.

Alluding to Louis Brandeis, the late U.S. Supreme Court justice who was at the forefront of an antitrust movement, Mamdani continued: “We can have democracy. We can have great wealth concentrated in the hands of a few. We cannot have both.” Mamdani used the Brandeis formulation to connect the corporate episode with his argument about unequal wealth and economic security.

The episode also brought two positions that had appeared at odds into the same corporate dispute. Mamdani, a democratic socialist, had advocated for “collectivism” and expansive government intervention in the economy in his inaugural speech at the beginning of the year. Yet his relationship with Wall Street remained ambiguous: the financial center's revenue supports his progressive agenda for New York City. The two-week Nvidia rollercoaster created awkward bedfellows around private enterprise and government control, The Guardian said.

Trump was among the U.S. politicians weighing the fallout, including whether the reported affair posed a national-security risk. In an interview with The Wall Street Journal published on Monday, he said he liked getting involved in companies and pointed to the government taking a 10% stake in chipmaker Intel. “If I can help a company that’s got some problems,” Trump said, “I do it.”

Questions also surrounded Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang, who is not related to him. The Guardian reported that the technology executive has been accused of acting as a Chinese asset and routing advanced chips to Beijing through her datacenter company, Megaspeed. U.S. export controls prohibit the sale of advanced AI chips to China.

Trump administration national intelligence director Jay Clayton said he suspected China was able to obtain Nvidia's most advanced AI chips even as U.S. export controls prohibit their sale to China.
