*Eight billion in farmer relief, with the corporate receipts already filled in.*

Trump’s people call it farm aid. Let’s call the receipt what it is: $8 billion in direct payments, announced two weeks before the midterms, designed to keep cash-strapped farmers buying seed, fertilizer, pesticides, and machinery.

Do not tell me the money does nothing for farmers. Diesel has surged above $6 a gallon, fertilizer costs are up, corn prices are down, and farm debt is at record highs. The money can keep a farmer operating. Hell, it may save somebody’s season.

But the article names the biggest winners: Bayer sells the seed; CF Industries and Nutrien sell fertilizer; Deere wants a new combine or planter. The payments help farmers keep current spending levels, which keeps those sales coming and makes it easier for the seed sellers to pass along price increases. The farmer gets some breathing room. The giants keep the receipts.

That’s the bullshit part. “Farmer relief” is doing double duty as an election-timed purchase pump for agribusiness. You may call it a bailout. I call it the order of the rescue: the farmer buys seed, fertilizer, chemicals, and machines; the suppliers collect at the other end of the scale.

And “direct aid” does not make this innocent. Direct aid can still make the biggest corporate winners bigger. If you actually mean to help farmers, use the goddamn check to lower input costs or give farmers a real chance at debt relief. Don’t hand the farmer a basket marked RELIEF while making sure every item on it carries a supplier’s price tag.

There’s no mystery. The White House puts $8 billion on the table, the timing lands before the midterms, and the effect is plain: farmers keep farming and buying; Bayer, CF Industries, Nutrien, and Deere stay in business. Fine. Farmers weren’t promised a fertilizer check. They were promised that somebody in Washington would remember what it costs to make a living. They remembered who gets paid too.

Fuck the photo-op. Pay the farmer, fight the corporate price hikes, and keep your fucking hands off the till. What the hell is this supposed to be?
