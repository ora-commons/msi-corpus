*Trade deals, my ass: it’s a unilateral threat with paperwork.*

They held up last year’s trade deals as proof this year’s tariff menu belonged to the same policy. Now the Trump administration is threatening 14 trading partners with unilateral duties while talks with some partners were still underway. They used the old fucking deals to cover a brand-new threat with paperwork.

Let me get this straight. The public story is that the United States reached agreements. The policy is: pay up while the administration holds a hearing and maybe imposes the duties later. That is bullshit.

Brazil’s government said it would not yield to the threat and left retaliation possible. China rejected the “industrial overcapacity” allegation as a pretext for political manipulation and urged dialogue. The administration says trading partners “know what we’re proposing.” Yeah, they know. That is what a threat is for.

The receipts are ugly enough. The proposal reaches 14 trading partners. China faces a 20% cap, with some combined duties reaching 45%. Brazil gets a new 10% aircraft tariff aimed at Embraer and higher duties on raw and refined sugar. There is not even a final order yet: affected industries get a hearing on January 25, with duties supposedly landing in February or March. The timeline is the tell. This is not a deal already made. It is pressure attached to a future tariff.

And the excuse? USTR Jamieson Greer says foreign “market distorting practices” are eroding America’s manufacturing base. So the United States is threatening broad new import taxes while negotiations with some partners remain open and calling its own pressure tactics a remedy. Goddamn, that is backwards.

Trump can call it tariffs, a remedy, or whatever hell helps the speech sound tougher. The receipts call it a threat without the finished deal. If you mean a deal, finish the deal. If you mean a threat, do not smuggle it in under a banner saying “trade deals.”

A deal is an agreement. This is a threat with paperwork. Trump can keep the word “deals” on the banner, but the paper trail says “unilateral.” Hell, even the cat can read that receipt.
