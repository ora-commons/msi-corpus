*Legal entry, stalled green card, no Medicaid: America’s bullshit receipt.*

Sahar Amir worked with the U.S. military at Bagram, then spent nearly a decade prosecuting rape cases and violence against women in Afghanistan, including cases involving Taliban members. The United States admitted him with his wife and children on a Special Immigrant Visa in January 2025. He filed the green-card paperwork and waited.

Now the Trump administration and the Congress that passed the 2025 One Big Beautiful Bill Act have him in fucking limbo.

The federal rule that took effect Oct. 1 cut his family off Medicaid and denied these refugees and other humanitarian entrants access to income-based ACA subsidies. SNAP had already stopped in February. The state says 12,500 people in North Carolina lost food aid earlier this year. When the rule took effect, Amir, his family and another 10,500 refugees and others lost medical support. At NeighborHealth, about 500 patients lost Medicaid.

Amir had surgery in July to remove a cancerous kidney. His doctor says the prognosis is good, but he still needs follow-up care, treatment for chronic problems and PTSD. He has $475 in the bank, unpaid credit-card debt, rent due in less than a week, and a GoFundMe campaign while he worries about that follow-up. Meanwhile his green-card application sits stalled. Without the card, no Medicaid. Without Medicaid, he may not afford the care he needs.

They used the legal door and the word ‘support’ to cover a locked exit. That is not a broken system. That is the damn policy working exactly as written.

And do not flatten Amir into some nameless case file. He worked with American military personnel, prosecuted rape and violence against women, and came through a lawful visa process because America was grateful for the help. His former colleague said she expected full support in return. America got the service. Then Trump’s people gave this family the bill.

He cannot safely return to Afghanistan. Pakistan is sending Afghans back. So America leaves him technically legal, sick and broke while the administration has paused many green-card applications.

This is obscene. They admitted him, left his green card stalled, cut off Medicaid after surgery and handed him the bill. Fuck that. Fuck this bullshit country for making people who stood with us pay the receipt.

We took his service. We owe him the care. The bill was never supposed to be his.
