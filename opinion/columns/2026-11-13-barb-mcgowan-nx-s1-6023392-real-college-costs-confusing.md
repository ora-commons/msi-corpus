*They hid the price for thirty years and called the un-hiding generosity.*

I did everything they told us to do. Keep the kids out of debt, they said. Go to college, that's the ladder. Then you find out the price was never a number. It was a fog machine.

NPR says it plain: Americans have soured on higher education because it is frustratingly hard to figure out what you will actually pay. Not hard like calculus. Hard on purpose. Sticker price over here, net price over there, fees nobody mentions until August, an aid formula that changes the second you get a raise. You cannot comparison-shop a number they refuse to give you. That's not a mystery. That's a business model.

And now, bless their hearts, some schools roll out tuition guarantees. Middlebury will freeze free tuition for families under an income line. Cute. They finally hand us a legible number and want a goddamn parade for it. You broke the calculator, hid the receipts for thirty years, watched families sign blind, and now clarity is the favor? Fuck that.

The guarantee isn't charity. It's marketing aimed at the parents who got robbed the first time and are too tired to check the fine print twice. They used the ladder to sell the debt, and now they're selling the rungs back to us.

Eat shit. You knew exactly what the number was the whole time.
