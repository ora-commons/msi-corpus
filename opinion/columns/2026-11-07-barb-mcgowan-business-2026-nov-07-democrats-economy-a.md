*They won the gavel, not the goddamn steering wheel.*

Here’s the receipt from this election: voters were furious about what things cost, they put Democrats in control of Congress, and now the analysis driving the headline says the new majority has little power to bring prices down in the next couple of years.

Let me be plain: that is not a rescue. That is a victory lap with no goddamn engine attached.

The public story is: "We voted for Democrats because the cost of living made us furious; now they will fix it." The operative reality is that the incoming Democratic majority has few tools to make prices fall fast. Winning the vote was the easy receipt. Delivering lower prices is the part they cannot cash.

I know the kitchen math. I do my grocery shopping at Walmart with my kids’ shoes in the cart and the family insurance bill in my hand. When the gas price moves, my paycheck does not move with it. I get the receipt.

So Democrats can take control, hold the gavel, stand under the flashbulbs, and say, "We hear you." We hear you too. You heard us when we said the cost of living had made this country furious. Now don’t sell us a promise you cannot keep.

And this is not me blaming the neighbor who voted because the grocery bill hurt. Fuck no. The rage belongs to the Democratic Party’s leaders: they took the affordability votes and have little power to deliver the relief voters expected. That is the difference between an election victory and a household promise.

The bill is still coming to us.

Bring prices down, Democrats, or stop acting like the gavel is the steering wheel. Eat shit. The checkout line can tell the truth.
