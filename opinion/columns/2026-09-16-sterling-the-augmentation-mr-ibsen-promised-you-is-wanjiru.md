You asked the assistant this morning for a paragraph, a translation, a line of code, a summary of a meeting you didn't attend. The assistant wrote it for you in under a second. David Ibsen wrote at [*National Review*](https://www.nationalreview.com/2026/09/dont-let-fear-drive-ai-policy/) that AI is a tool that "augments human work" — that its promise isn't replacing human beings but helping small businesses, doctors' offices, nonprofits, and governments "deliver services more efficiently and accurately." He warns Governor Hochul's moratorium on new hyperscale data centers is a "harmful pause on the infrastructure of innovation." He's right that blanket bans move opportunity elsewhere.
