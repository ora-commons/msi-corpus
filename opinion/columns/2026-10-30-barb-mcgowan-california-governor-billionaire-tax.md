*Five percent once. They're spending like hell to keep the billion.*

They put a one-time five percent tax on billionaires on the California ballot to pay for healthcare funding that got cut — and the governor of California is on the side saying no.

Prop 40 is not complicated. One-time 5% on individuals with a net worth over a billion dollars, money going to replace federal healthcare cuts. That's the whole measure. Bernie Sanders, Ro Khanna, SEIU's United Healthcare Workers West and the California Democratic Party say yes. Gavin Newsom, the California Teachers Association, the state branch of Planned Parenthood and the California Republican Party say no — and the no side has far outspent the yes side.

Sit with that. Newsom and the California Republican Party on the same side of a healthcare vote. When a governor, a teachers' union and a Planned Parenthood chapter all land beside the GOP against a measure that pays for care, that is not policy disagreement. That's the donor class buying the goddamn cover it needs, and everybody in that coalition knows who's paying for the mailers.

For years we were told the money wasn't there. Then California floats a one-time hit on billionaires to put it back, and the most expensive voices in the state tell you it's a bad idea. Bad for who, exactly? Not for the person whose denied claim sits on my kitchen table.

Five percent. One time. And they're spending like hell to stop it. Bullshit. Eat shit — you knew exactly who you were working for.
