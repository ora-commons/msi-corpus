Operators call this "the preamble": you put the fact that would end the argument into the background, where it cannot be examined, and you move the reader forward to the work you actually want done, which is the evaluating. The bigger the fact, the smaller the grammatical slot it is given. That is what the preamble is for. The evaluating here is done by the adjectives — "dictator," "top despot," "criminal regime."
