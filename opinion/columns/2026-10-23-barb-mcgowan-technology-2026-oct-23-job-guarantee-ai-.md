*They built the machine. We pay for the jobs it eats.*

They said it out loud. That is the part the story skips.

AI executives, the people building the machines, are out front warning about the jobs: mass displacement, crises of vast magnitude, the whole parade. The Guardian asks whether a job guarantee is the solution. Read the headline again. It asks who catches the fallout. It never asks who made the problem.

And here is the fix, with a name on it. In September, Democratic U.S. Rep. Ayanna Pressley reintroduced a resolution for a federally funded, locally administered program paying participants at least $25 an hour, with regular adjustments. In its most ambitious form, anyone who wants work gets a meaningful, decently paid job.

Locally administered. Federally funded.

Read those two words next to each other at my kitchen table, where Cinder's tail has been going since the mail came, and tell me what happens. Locally, some clerk hands out a check. Federally, my tax dollars pay for it. And the machine that ate the job? Still running. Still owned by the people who profit from it.

I clock in at Walmart more hours than I want, away from my kids, without the insurance a family needs. My grandfather had a union job where one wage covered the whole house. That ain't nostalgia. That's the receipt. When they cut the job, they did not cut the work. They cut me. The machine does the hours and somebody else keeps the savings off the payroll. That is how this shit works, and don't you fucking tell me it is progress.

So let's be clear about the check, because the check is not the job. A guarantee is a floor under the floor. It says: we know we broke something, here is twenty-five dollars an hour so you can keep the lights on while the owners of the machine get rich off the broken thing. It is more than nothing. It is a hell of a lot more than nothing.

But do not hand me a clipboard and a federal check and call it dignity.

They built the machine that takes your job, then handed us the goddamn bill. Take your hand off the lever and put your money where your mouth is. Go to hell.
