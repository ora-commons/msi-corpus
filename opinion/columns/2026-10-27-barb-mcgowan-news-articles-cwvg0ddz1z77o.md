*Trump called it Liberation Day. Families got the damn surcharge.*

At my kitchen table, I did the math with the national numbers and the receipt beside my coffee. Average hourly earnings rose 3 percent over the last year. Consumer prices rose 3.7 percent. Wage growth did not even keep up with the goddamn bill.

Then Donald Trump gave us “Liberation Day.” A Federal Reserve Bank of New York study found the April 2025 tariffs raised consumer-goods prices by about 2.9 percent. That is a price tag on the kitchen table. That is the goods families buy.

Here is the part where they put on the patriot costume and call a price hike a victory. Trump sold the tariffs as strength for America while the checkout line charged the household for the policy. The worker got the speech. The working family got the goddamn bill. That is the bullshit bargain: flag, victory lap, higher price tag.

I remember my grandfather’s union job, when one paycheck could carry a household. I work more hours than I want at Walmart, and my kids’ shoes still need buying. I am not asking for a fantasy where every price falls forever. I am asking that a promised trade policy not make the same basket cost more while wages run behind.

So the slogan was simple: America first, maybe even “Liberation Day.” The receipt was simpler: tariffs made consumer goods more expensive, and families paid the difference. You do not get to wrap a price hike in a flag and call it patriotism.

Trump, you sold us a rescue and delivered a surcharge on the working family’s shopping. If your plan was to make America great by making what we buy cost more, congratulations. You fucking succeeded. The rest of us are still paying the bill.
