Jason Riley recruits free-market voters against Donald Trump.

In his November 3, 2026, *Wall Street Journal* opinion column, “The Beginning of Trump’s Long Goodbye,” Jason L. Riley runs that recruitment as a coalition operation. He gives the president’s opponents a reason to expect defeat, gives free-market readers a set of economic and governing grievances, and leaves the political vehicle that would receive those readers unnamed. He names instead the party that profits from the wreckage. This column annotates the seven decisive passages in the order they operate. Anchors run by opening words rather than paragraph numbers.

I know the operation from the cable years, where I helped build versions of it: recruit the potential defectors, give them a respectable reason, and leave the old coalition looking divided rather than defeated.
