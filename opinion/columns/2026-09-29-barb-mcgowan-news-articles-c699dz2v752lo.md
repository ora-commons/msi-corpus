*They handed out the tea. They built themselves a hospital.*

This is the part where the government puts on the family-values voice and hopes we don't read the fucking receipt.

Jersey just released its 2027-2030 budget and called it a family cost-of-living package. Let's look at what families actually get: five extra hours of free nursery care a week. A parental grant that goes from £887 to £1,800 — which covers about a month and a half of diapers if you buy the cheap ones. A £250 one-off payment per school-aged child for low-to-middle income households. Free school meals extended to some secondary pupils.

That's it. That's the whole family plan. And Treasury Minister Alan Maclean had the balls to call it "a step in the right direction" while openly admitting the real structural changes — the ones that would actually determine whether Jersey families can afford to live on this island — are deferred to 2028-2030. He called it a "transitional budget." Transitional. That means they know the numbers don't add up, the Fiscal Policy Panel already told them Jersey is spending more than it earns, and their answer is to throw families a few crumbs now and schedule the actual reckoning for later.

Meanwhile, Pillar Two corporate tax revenue — money from multinationals making over £631 million — gets earmarked for building a hospital at Overdale. Not for families. Not for the cost of living. A building project. Because nothing says family support like routing the one new revenue stream away from household budgets and into construction contracts.

Chief Minister Lyndon Farnham wrote that "for many households, the cost of living remains the most immediate concern." No shit, Lyndon. The most immediate concern. And your most immediate response is a transitional budget that defers the structural fixes past the next election cycle. That's not leadership. That's a goddamn placeholder with a ribbon on it.

You dressed up a delay as a plan, handed families a cup of tea, and told them the real food was coming in 2028. They're not stupid. They're broke. There's a difference.
