*Six straight pay cuts, $4 gas — and they want a parade for 65,000 jobs. Bullshit.*

They put the jobs number in the headline and the paycheck number down in paragraph six, and they hoped we'd stop reading. We didn't. Real pay fell 0.7 percent in September — the sixth straight year-over-year drop. Six in a goddamn row. My grocery receipts have been telling me that story for years. Thanks for catching up to my wallet.

Now read the fine print. Hiring averaged 9,700 jobs a month in 2025 — the weakest year outside a recession since 2002. This month the forecast is 65,000, and suddenly that's a comeback. And that 4.2 percent unemployment rate? It holds because the economy now only needs about 5,000 new jobs a month to keep the number from rising — boomer retirements and Trump's immigration policies took a chunk of the competition out of the pool. The rate looks healthy partly because the workforce got smaller. That's not a strong job market. That's a rigged scale with the man who owns it sitting on the goddamn weights.

Meanwhile his double-digit tariffs and the war with Iran pushed gas past four dollars a gallon. He promised jobs and cheap everything. He delivered the worst hiring since 2002 outside a recession, six straight cuts to real pay, and four-dollar gas with his name stamped on the pump.

Tuesday, the country answered. Voters fired Trump's Republicans and handed Democrats Congress over exactly this — the cost of living. And now that crowd wants a fucking parade for finally limping past a bar they buried in the basement themselves? Bullshit. The forgotten man was never forgotten. They knew exactly where his wallet was, and they went there on purpose.

Eat shit, all of you. We already voted.
