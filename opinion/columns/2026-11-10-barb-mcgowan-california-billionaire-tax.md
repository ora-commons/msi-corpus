*Billionaire money killed Prop 40; families still get the healthcare hole.*

California had one plain chance to make the very rich pay for part of the hole Washington cut in healthcare, and billionaire money made sure it died.

Prop 40 would have imposed a one-time 5% tax on Californians worth more than $1 billion. Most of the money would have backfilled federal healthcare cuts. Ten percent would have gone to education and food assistance. SEIU United Healthcare Workers West estimated the federal hole at $100 billion over five years. That is not pocket change. That is a bill big enough to swallow the safety net and leave working people holding the sharp edges.

Instead, the “no” side raised roughly ten times what the “yes” side raised. Building a Better California pulled in more than $333 million. Google co-founder Sergey Brin alone put more than $100 million into the outfit working to defeat the measure.

And Gavin Newsom opposed the measure and called its failure a win. He said a California wealth tax would disadvantage the state. Funny how the state facing an estimated $100 billion healthcare hole can hear the rich very clearly when they threaten to leave.

I know this routine. You call a tax reckless because the money might run, then let the people with the biggest checks decide what happens next. I work part-time at Walmart without the insurance my kids need. The public was handed the hole while the donor cash stayed put and Prop 40 went into the box marked NO.

So fuck you, Newsom. Fuck the billionaire-funded no machine that left the healthcare hole for working families. You made “economic risk” the reason a tax that might have repaired part of what Washington broke had to die, but not one damn word of concern for the families stuck with the wreckage. This is the same old bullshit: the rich call their safety a policy, and everybody else gets the bill.

Billionaires funded their side. Let California’s families live with the fucking healthcare hole they left behind.
