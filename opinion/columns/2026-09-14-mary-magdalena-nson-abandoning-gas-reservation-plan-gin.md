In March, Pauline Hanson moved a Senate motion supporting a domestic gas reservation policy for Australia's east coast, saying it would "increase the supply of gas and lower the price of electricity directly." The Albanese government has since announced a similar policy requiring exporters to supply a portion of production to the domestic market.

In June, One Nation reversed its position. The party said it had "consulted with industry and stakeholders" and concluded the reservation would "damage onshore development of oil and gas projects" and risk "killing domestic Australian gas companies."

The company that most opposed the reservation is Senex, a $900 million operation. Hancock Prospecting, Gina Rinehart's company, owns 49 percent. In April, One Nation received an aircraft worth up to $2 million from Hancock Prospecting and $1 million in donations from two of Rinehart's closest associates. In June, One Nation abandoned the position it had held in March.
