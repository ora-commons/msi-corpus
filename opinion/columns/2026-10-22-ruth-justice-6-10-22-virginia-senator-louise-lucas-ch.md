Federal prosecutors say Virginia state Sen. Louise Lucas used her Senate chairmanship to shield the cannabis business she owned, and they have indicted her for paying $750,000 to buy the shell company that made the deal possible.

The [federal indictment against Lucas](/articles/2026-10-22-federal-indictment-alleges-lucas-led-a-marijuana-and-money-laundering-scheme/) alleges that she bought the full controlling interest in a shell company used to obtain a state marijuana license, took bribes, conspired to distribute marijuana and launder money, and met with co-conspirators on government property to conduct the scheme’s dealings. The charges include conspiracy to distribute marijuana, money laundering and public corruption.

Lucas is 82, has represented Virginia’s 18th District since 1992, and chairs the Senate Finance and Appropriations Committee. She surrendered to federal authorities Thursday. Members of her family have also been charged.
