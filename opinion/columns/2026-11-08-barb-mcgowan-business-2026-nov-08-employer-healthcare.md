*They called it cost control. Workers still got the bill.*

I did everything they told us to do. I took the job. I kept the coverage they offered. I paid my share like a good citizen.

Then the benefits notice landed: health costs are going up again.

Mercer says the total health-benefit cost per employee will rise 8.2% in 2027 even after employers make their planned cost cuts. That is the highest projected jump since 2003. Employers told Mercer their existing plans were already headed up about 11% without changes. So the great cost-control machine still lands on the worker.

They can call choosing a plan “necessary to attract and retain good workers.” Hell, maybe they mean attract good workers and retain their paycheck. An 8.2% rise is not control. It is the sound of your share of the healthcare bill getting heavier while the employer calls the plan “competitive.”

I know the table where that math gets eaten. I work part-time at Walmart. My kids are on the coverage. I do not need some benefits manager telling me health care is complicated. I am the one watching what my coverage costs take from every check.

Gene Marks puts it plainly: U.S. employers keep passing rising healthcare costs to workers. This is employer bullshit dressed as workforce support.

Here is the part I will not soften: the cost control is the employer’s; the rising bill is ours. They can shift the price to us and call that worker choice. It is not choice when the price is set upward and your share follows.

The 8.2% does not become smaller because the spreadsheet has nice formatting. If you are holding the checkbook, pay the fucking bill. Show workers what “cost control” actually costs them, then fix it before the kitchen table gets the notice.

They sold benefit choice as necessary to attract and retain good workers. They delivered a bigger bill and called it retention. Eat shit. Cut the bullshit and pay the damn thing.
