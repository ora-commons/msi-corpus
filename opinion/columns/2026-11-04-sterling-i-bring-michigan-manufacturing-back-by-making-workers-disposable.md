The form isn’t an exception. It’s the governing rule: the owner keeps the output, and somebody else maintains the labor. A Senate speech from 1837, attributed to John C. Calhoun and used in defense of slavery, put the arrangement this way:

> “There never has yet existed a wealthy and civilized society in which one portion of the community did not, in point of fact, live on the labor of the other.”

The press isn’t an exception to that rule. It’s the rule with a die attached. The plantation owner paid to keep the body because he owned it. The factory left that upkeep to wages, families, and public relief. My plant lets Cyril pay with 2 fingers and records the saving as labor flexibility.
