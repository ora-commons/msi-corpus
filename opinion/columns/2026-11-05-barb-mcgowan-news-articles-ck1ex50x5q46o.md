*Poster says America First. Receipt says America pays first. Every damn week.*

We did what they always told us to do. We voted. Not on flags this time — on receipts. The grocery bill. The light bill. What it costs to fill the tank and the feed truck.

Here's what that vote bought: a Congress that can't get a goddamn thing past Donald Trump's veto. An investor, Ken Fisher, looked at the whole mess and called the next two years 'relative quiet.' Quiet for who? Not for Kim Hanks outside Austin, raising wages for 300 workers because groceries and fuel won't stop climbing. Not for the ranchers taking $300 to $500 less per head while fertilizer hits record costs — even a Republican agriculture commissioner, Sid Miller, said it plain: 'They felt betrayed.' Hell, we all felt betrayed. Twenty-one percent of the country is satisfied with where we're headed, and the rest of us are doing math in the checkout line.

And the tariff — the import tax adding thousands of dollars a year to groceries, fertilizer, and building materials — is paying Washington now. Import taxes went from just over 1% of federal revenue in 2024 to more than 5% this year, and Oxford Economics says that makes them 'tough for a future administration to fully dismantle.' Tough for who? It comes out of my cart every single week, and now the government's budget depends on it. They built the books on money skimmed off the checkout line.

The Medicaid cuts Trump signed in 2025 — the coverage Democrats ran on restoring? The smart money says maybe a deal by late 2027, benefits by 2028, and the trade on the table is extending the tax cuts in exchange for putting the health subsidies back. My kid's doctor waits while the billionaires' paperwork gets done first. Meanwhile gas sits at $4.18 a gallon, up nearly 40%, and the forecasters say the war may get bigger now that pump prices can't hurt him at the ballot box. All that forgotten-man love turned out to be bullshit the minute the polls closed.

You didn't lose the election. You just stopped needing us. The votes were the only thing that ever made us count, and now they're cashed. Eat shit.
