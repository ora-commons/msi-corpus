TO THE CLASS —

Lucero is a splice tech I bill at $4.10 a linear foot on a 6,200-mile trench run I am building for a hyperscaler along the back-haul east of the Cascades. She is 42. Her boots are in a 4-foot section outside Stanfield, Oregon. The cable she mends is 11.4 pounds a foot. The line is the layer between you and the model you queried this morning — the assistant you typed your question into, the image you asked it to draw. Renaldo is a rigger on the pipe rack of the 480-megawatt behind-the-meter peaker I own outside New London, Wisconsin. He is 41. His harness costs $3,178.40. The body belt I issued him costs $41.80. The body belt is mine. The answer your chatbot gave you at 3 a.m. ran on the megawatt his turbine was rated to push. God bless the spread.

The six counties along Lucero's route want environmental review, community benefits, and an enforceable wage floor before a permit lands. I have a one-page answer to each. Glenn Davis has filed the brief for this week in *National Review*, indexing the governors layering conditions onto data-center permits — Shapiro in Pennsylvania removed the fast-track, Pritzker in Illinois froze new incentives, Spanberger in Virginia is drafting a 2027 package, Newsom in California signed seven oversight bills, Moore in Maryland wants the tax exemption repealed — and attributing every addition to the 2028 primary. ([Read him here.](https://www.nationalreview.com/2026/10/how-the-2028-primaries-could-cripple-americas-ai-buildout/)) File the chapter under "Pass-Through," the section you were tested on in October. You will recognize the moves. The governor's move is the surcharge. Your move is the pass-through. The pass-through is mine.

Let me put the numbers where you can see them.

The hyperscaler pays $4.10 a linear foot for the lit fiber I turn over. I pay Lucero $0.34. My spread is $3.76 a foot. The 6,200 miles of trench become 32,736,000 linear feet of cable. The arithmetic is large without my having to teach it.

On Renaldo's end: the $3,178.40 I didn't spend on the harness is not the prize. The prize is the avoided liability on each rig I will not hire. The trinket is the test.

Trace the figures with your eye. Hold those two columns together. That is the whole lesson, and most people never learn it.

Widen the frame.

Here is the principle the case was only an example of. The AI buildout is the new enclosure of the commons. Power, water, the heat-rejection plume — these were municipal infrastructure for a century. The 21st-century arrangement is the private enclosure of all three. The data center is the new manor. The hyperscaler is the new landlord.
