*He voted for the tariff, then blamed the other guy for the bill.*

Exit polls said affordability was the top issue for many Georgia voters. So the voters put the cost of groceries at the front of the ballot. And the Republican they handed it to, Mike Collins, had voted to support Trump's tariffs — the tariffs BBC News reported have been linked to rising inflation. Then he ran the oldest con in American politics: blame Biden. Not 'here's the tariff I voted for.' Not 'here's the line item I put my name on.' Blame the guy whose term is over, point the finger backward, and hope nobody walks around the barn and looks at the record. Because it is in the record. He voted for it. He defended that he voted for it. And he handed the cost of living to a man who isn't even in office anymore. He defended Trump's war in Iran too. So the war is fine. The tariff is fine. The gas, the groceries, the rent, the insurance — all of it is the former president's fault. That ain't politics. That's a bullshit con. The tariff he voted for is the one BBC said has been linked to rising inflation. And while Collins was out there eating the blame, Republicans pulled their money and their attention out of Georgia and put them into other contests. Georgia wasn't where the resources went. Georgia was where they left a man to take the heat. My grandfather had a union job and one paycheck covered his family. I work part-time at Walmart, more hours than I want, no insurance for my kids, and I price everything twice before I buy it. I did everything they told us to do. And I'm supposed to be furious at the wrong guy while the right one goes home comfortable. Here is your receipt, Republicans: you voted for the tariff, then you blamed the other guy for the bill. You goddamn cowards. Eat shit. Ossoff is projected to hold that seat after running on the economy and affordability. Collins is projected to lose with a blame-the-wrong-man speech and a tariff vote on the record. That's the whole verdict, and it didn't take a voter to see it.
