*Call them reliable, hand them a secretary tag, and ask if they're a spy.*

They built a country out of a compliment that was never a compliment.

The Asian American Foundation ran the numbers: at least half of us call Asian workers respectful, or reliable. One in ten call them assertive. One. In. Ten. So the kid gets the degree, breaks the ceiling, gets the promotion — and the whole country still calls her the secretary. Dependable is what they call you when they don't have to say you don't get to lead. Misa Chien, who runs The Authentic Asian, told AP the theme she hears in onboarding for new members is her clients' line: 'I'm mistaken for the secretary. I don't look like the typical face of leadership.' That isn't a job title. That's a country telling you where you live.

Then Jensen Huang has his extramarital affair, and he resigns. Fine. Good for him. But somebody still had to hold the bill, and it can't be the guy who resigned — so it lands on a Chinese businesswoman with his last name and no relation to him. Within hours the memes are up and the oldest script in the book is running: spy, trophy wife, seductress. The Justice Department hasn't found sufficient evidence she was a spy. The president says he doesn't know if that's been determined yet. Meanwhile the women who go to work every day get asked about their laptop security when they fly to China, and get asked 'so, are you a spy?' like it's a punchline. Stand with Asian Americans gets calls from Asian women who did the work, got the promotion, and are still called frauds.

And there is always paperwork behind the joke. More than two dozen states have written or proposed alien land laws. Trump's first administration built the China Initiative to profile Chinese professors. Jane Ying Wu — Northwestern neuroscientist, lived in this country forty years, under federal investigation in 2019, never charged with a damn thing — died by suicide in 2024 at sixty. Never charged. Then dead.

So no. You don't get to call a people reliable and then spend them as a stereotype. We were never your damn labor pool. Take the 'reliable' card off the wall before you hang your own bullshit on us. Fucking hell.
