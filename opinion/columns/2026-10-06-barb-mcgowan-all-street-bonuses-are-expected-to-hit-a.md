*They called us the resilient consumer. We paid the bonus pool.*

They told us the economy was resilient. Turns out 'resilient consumer' is what the banks call a person who keeps paying the fees.

In 2025 the average Wall Street bonus was $246,900 and the average total compensation was $561,770. The total bonus pool was $49.2 billion — a record, and this year is supposed to be better.

Mayor Mamdani keeps pushing to make this city work for people who aren't collecting a bonus. The bankers keep pretending they'll bolt if he does. The receipt says they won't.

Take the flag pin off before you rob the family. Wall Street got the speech, the profits, and the goddamn bonus pool. Main Street got called 'resilient' and billed for the privilege.
