*Cindy Banda voted the receipt, not the slogan.*

I did everything they told me to. I voted the family-values ticket. I worked the Walmart shifts I did not want because groceries and coverage do not care about your campaign speech. I kept the house. I kept the cars. I expected this country to keep its side of the bargain.

Cindy Banda kept her side too. She voted for Donald Trump in 2016, 2020 and 2024 because she opposed abortion. Then breast cancer came into her life and the size of the bill made that promise impossible to ignore.

Here are the receipts: surgery, chemo and radiation left her carrying more than $25,000 in medical bills. Her cancer medication costs $16,000 a month. Insurance cut that to $500, but her household runs about $7,000 a month, with six children in her care, a $1,300 car payment, groceries, utilities and two leaks in the house. Relatives and classmates held chicken-and-rice fundraisers. Two GoFundMes pulled in nearly $30,000. She put the house into forbearance and went back to selling Wi-Fi door to door while nauseated and exhausted.

Trump and the Republicans had this woman’s vote. They lost it because healthcare costs had become the ballot she could no longer ignore. Cindy was not reacting to a slogan. She was reacting to a receipt.

James Talarico wants all Americans in Medicare. Ken Paxton wants a $25,000 tax deduction for out-of-pocket expenses. One aims to put people inside coverage. The other waits for the family to bleed the money first and then hands them a tax note. Cindy is choosing between a party that lost her and a candidate who talks about the bill.

I know this kitchen-table math. A missed shift can become a fundraiser. A deductible can become a second mortgage. And you do not get to hide behind a ballot box when the damn cost arrives.

Keep the house. Keep the cars. Keep the kids stable. Then pay the fucking bill. Cindy Banda did not switch parties for a slogan. She switched because the cancer bill came due and the family-values pitch did not pay it. Republicans, you had her vote. You do not get her health back. Eat shit.
