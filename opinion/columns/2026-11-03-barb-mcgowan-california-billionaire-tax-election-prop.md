*They spent over $333 million on the ballot fight; healthcare got the shaft.*

They turned California’s wealth tax into a donor-money machine.

Proposition 40 would impose a one-time 5% tax on Californians with more than $1 billion in net worth. Most of the money would replace federal health-care cuts. That is the public case: the ultra-rich pay their share, and California replaces the holes in health-care funding. The yes side is led by SEIU United Healthcare Workers West and the California Federation of Labor Unions.

Then look at the other ledger.

Building a Better California has raised more than $333 million against the measure—about 30% from Sergey Brin. The anti-tax side has roughly ten times the money of the yes side. Larry Page and Brin started cutting ties with California after Proposition 40 was unveiled. Two measures backed by the same PAC could nullify the tax entirely if either passed with more yes votes than Proposition 40.

That is the deal: donate hundreds of millions to California’s ballot machine, buy the chance to block the bill, and then talk about budget effects if the rich leave. Mark Zuckerberg’s eight-year Meta deal helps him pay the tax without losing his ownership stake, while Gavin Newsom warns that the measure could push the wealthiest residents out of California even as he supports a national minimum tax on wealth above $100 million.

Planned Parenthood and the California Teachers Association joined the opposition over revenue distribution and the budget effects of a wealthy exodus. Read that again: health-care and education groups are fighting the tax that would replace federal health-care cuts.

The mid-October PPIC poll had 52% of likely voters against Proposition 40 and 46% for it. They have not won yet.

Well, they spent more than $333 million trying. Good luck with that, you donor-funded fucking frauds. Keep the money out here and leave the health-care tab to everybody else. That is institutional bullshit. Eat shit. You knew exactly what you were doing.
