*They called it family values. The receipt says you pay.*

I voted for the family-values crowd because I thought one of them might actually feed my kids. So let's talk about what that got me.

NPR put it plain: going into the midterms, affordability was the number-one thing on voters' minds, and tariffs and the war in Iran drove up prices, including food costs. Not a bad harvest. Not bad luck. Tariffs and a war. And the Republicans who controlled Washington shipped both, and kept selling themselves as the party of the working family all the way to the vote.

My grandfather had a union job, and it covered the house and it covered us. I work part-time at Walmart for more hours than I want, my kids are still uninsured, and I can read the difference in my own cart: eggs, bread, ground beef, all of it climbing every time I bring home a receipt.

They said: we will keep this country affordable. They said: we stand for the working family. What happened: they raised the price of the working family's dinner and called it policy. Who paid: us. Who kept the costume: them.

Even South Carolina said it out loud. Dr. Annie Andrews beat Republican Sen. Darline Graham. The kitchen table voted.

And now Democrats are putting grocery price proposals on their post-election agenda, and people want the grocery bill to come down. Good. You wrecked the cart. Somebody has to bring the receipt.

They used "family values" to hide a price stamp. That is not a value. That is a fucking price list.

So keep the flag on the mantle, elephant-branded cowards. You shipped the bill and you want applause for the groceries.

Screw your goddamn values. Eat shit. You made the working family pay for the family you love. Go to hell.
