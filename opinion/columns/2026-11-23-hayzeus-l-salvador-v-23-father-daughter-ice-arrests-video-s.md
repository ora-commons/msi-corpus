Trump’s ICE sent masked agents to seize a father and daughter on a jet bridge.

On Saturday, Ricardo Larrondo Mendoza and Sofia Hernandez Mejia were boarding an Alaska Airlines flight from Seattle-Tacoma International Airport to Los Angeles. They were traveling to a family baptism in California. The [53-second video filmed by fellow passengers](/articles/2026-11-22-ice-arrests-two-on-seattle-airport-jet-bridge-delaying-alaska-flight/) shows masked ICE officers, wearing unmarked jackets and khaki pants, detaining them on the jet bridge and struggling with someone on the ground. The flight was delayed. Their attorney, Olia Catala, says they had lived in the United States since 2015, were seeking asylum, and had no criminal record. Mejia, in her mid-twenties, is married to a U.S. citizen, has filed an application to adjust her status, went to college and worked for a nonprofit; her husband accompanied them and was not detained. ICE says Mendoza overstayed his visa and says Mejia was in the country unlawfully after overstaying hers. As of Sunday, their lawyer said both were held at the Northwest ICE Processing Center in Tacoma.
