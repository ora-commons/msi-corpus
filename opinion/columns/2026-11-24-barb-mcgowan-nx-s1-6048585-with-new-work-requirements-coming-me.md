*They call it a safety net. The receipt is a maze with cuts.*

Medicaid is supposed to be the floor under a low-income person's life. You should not need a magic stack of paperwork to keep the coverage that lets you get to a doctor. But the new rules are cuts and work requirements, and the human being trying to keep that coverage is left staring at a maze she cannot read.

The reporting is ugly in its precision. NPR's Selena Simmons-Duffin says cuts and work requirements are set to affect the health insurance of many low-income Americans. Then comes the part that matters: one woman who needs that coverage says the confusion is overwhelming. Not powerful people reading about policy. A person trying to keep her insurance, staring at rules too tangled to understand.

That is the whole receipt, and it is damn enough. You do not get to call something a safety net while bolting a work test to the gate and announcing cuts. The rulemakers get to write the maze; the woman has to live inside it.

Maybe the work requirement is supposed to teach discipline. Maybe the cuts are supposed to look responsible on a spreadsheet. Fine. Put that spreadsheet and the rulebook in front of the people who made the rules and let them explain the fucking maze to somebody who actually has to use it.

Family values, my ass. You do not defend the family by turning health coverage into a bureaucratic obstacle course and calling the obstacle course help.

This is what happens when policy stops being a sentence in a briefing and hits the kitchen table: one more form, one more test, one more reason the coverage you counted on may be gone. Fuck the press release. Fuck the promise that ends before the person can understand it.

For the woman in this report, Medicaid is not a talking point. It is coverage she needs while the rules leave her overwhelmed. A safety net that leaves the person relying on it more confused than protected is not a safety net. It is paperwork with better advertising.

Get rid of the work requirements. Undo the cuts. Put the coverage back where it belongs. Until then, they keep calling it a safety net while their hands cut the ropes. Fuck the lie of the safety net.
