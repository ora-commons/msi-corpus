*They pay to steal you. They won't pay to keep your ass.*

They told us to learn the new thing. So we learned the new thing. Then the same company that ordered us to learn it wouldn't pay us a damn dime for it.

Payscale asked around. 56% of workers say they should be paid more for the AI skills their employers demanded. Only 16% got a raise tied to it. The rest got a lecture about attitude.

Walk in the door as a stranger and you're worth more: 4% more at entry level, 17% more at the senior levels, same title, same work. Serval is waving $30,000 signing bonuses, $30,000 referral bounties and a $10,000 vacation at 100 engineers. Their talent chief said it out loud: Anthropic and OpenAI are taking our people. A recruiting CEO said it plainer: companies would rather steal someone else's person than train one.

So here's the deal they don't print on the poster. Stay loyal and you're the cost they manage. Walk out and you're the asset they overpay for. Loyalty got priced, and it came with a goddamn family-values speech.

And the bullshit about a talent shortage? You can't hire your way out of a workforce you refuse to build. They demand proven experience and won't fund the first hour of it.

Eat shit. You priced us, then pretended we were the problem.
