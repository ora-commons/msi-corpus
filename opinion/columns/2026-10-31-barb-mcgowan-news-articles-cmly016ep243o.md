*They promised jobs. The receipt says ghost town. Eat shit.*

They told us the tariffs were going to bring the jobs back. I believed it, because I wanted to. My grandfather's union paycheck covered a whole family, and they promised me a piece of that again. So when they said tariffs, I heard jobs.

Here's the receipt. Trump hit Canada with 50 percent duties on roughly 28 billion Canadian dollars of goods — wood, pulp, paper. One of the first things to die was a paper mill in Témiscaming, Quebec, that has anchored that town for over a century. RYAM, the American outfit that owns it, called it a goddamn 'economic shutdown' in early October — indefinite. Most of its 360 workers are out. And here's the punchline: roughly three-quarters of what that mill makes gets shipped here. Frozen-food packaging. Scratch-off lottery tickets. The box your pizza rolls come in and the ticket you scratch at the gas station.

Trump says the tariffs create US jobs. The tariff's first receipt is a mill that makes things Americans buy, chained shut, with the workers living on partial government relief pay. That's not a jobs plan. That's bullshit with a flag on it.

And the carve-out. Susan Collins, Republican senator from Maine, up for re-election in November, lobbied and got some tariffs lifted. The mill's products weren't on the list. So relief isn't policy — it's re-election merchandise, and a town full of mill families got nothing because nobody needs their votes this year.

I know how this movie ends. The big paper mill a county over from me idled out in 2020, and that town never got over it. For-sale signs, kids leaving, a main street going gray. Témiscaming is staring down the same road, with sub-zero winter coming to freeze their equipment solid.

But those workers aren't begging. In 1973 they took a ten percent pay cut and bought the mill themselves and built Tembec out of it. Their union man says they're in Game Seven and they'll play till the last whistle. God love them for it.

You promised the jobs would come home. What came home is a chained gate and 360 families on relief pay, while the exemptions went out by re-election calendar. Eat shit, you flag-humping frauds. Take the flag pin off before you rob the family.
